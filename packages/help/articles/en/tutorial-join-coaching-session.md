title: Join your session
---
1. Open “My Human Coaches” or your Agenda and find your session. Before it opens, the page tells you when the link becomes available.
2. From ten minutes before, click “Join on Zoom”, “Join on Google Meet”, “Join on Microsoft Teams” or “Join the video call”. For a phone session, follow “Call …” or “The coach will call you on …”. The link disappears thirty minutes after the scheduled end.
