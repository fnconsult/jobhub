title: What are search criteria for, and how do I change them?
synonyms: [criteria, search criteria, target role, job, locations, city, salary, contract, permanent, remote work, sectors, domains, change my criteria, search preferences]
---
Each profile has its search criteria: « Target role », « Locations », « Minimum gross annual salary (in euros) », « Contract type », « Remote work », « Sectors », « Subsectors » and « Domains ». Jobbbox uses them for the Match Score, the ATS Score's target keyword and the job search.

They are in the profile's fiche: click « Change the criteria », then change a value. It is saved at once, and « Criteria saved » confirms it. You can give up to 5 locations: a job offer near one of them suits you. For contract, remote work and sectors, tick everything that suits you; nothing ticked means no preference.

Changing the criteria does not rename the profile. A job search's scores are recomputed with the profile's current criteria. An archived profile must be restored before you change its criteria.

To help you choose them, click the « Refine my criteria » suggestion in the profile's conversation.
