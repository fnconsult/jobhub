This page shows one job search and the job offers it found, ranked by Match Score with the profile.

Open a job offer to read it in full, then save it to follow it in your applications. « Search again » starts a new search for the same profile.

With the new layout, a job search's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the job offers found and their Match Scores in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Which offers to pick? », « Widen my criteria », « Search again ».

On the right, the search's fiche: its status, the day it started and the profile's search criteria, which « Change the criteria » opens, then « Search again », or « Stop the search » while it is searching. In « Results », each job offer found with its Match Score; « Job offers found », in « Sections », lists them all. A job offer opens beside the conversation, without hiding it, with its score, what explains it, and « Save as an application ». « Close » or the Escape key closes it.
