Some of the Help Centre's tutorials have a short video, placed under their text, in the « The video » section. The text is enough on its own: the video shows the same steps on Jobbbox's screen, with demo data, never yours. In the coach, the card of a tutorial that has a video offers « Watch the video », with its length: the link opens the tutorial in the Help Centre, where it plays.

The video never starts on its own and loads only when you play it: only its preview image shows before. The narration is read by a synthetic voice, which the video says. The captions, in the page's language, show from the start of playback; the player's captions button hides them. « Read the transcript », under the video, unfolds everything it says and shows, step by step.

The videos are hosted in the EU by Scaleway, which sees your browser's IP address when it shows the preview or plays the video, like any host. No cookie or tracker is attached to them. Scaleway is listed in « Data recipients », on the Legal page.
