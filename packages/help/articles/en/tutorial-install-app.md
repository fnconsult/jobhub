The Jobbbox app, on iPhone, iPad and Android, uses the same account as jobbbox.ai. No password: you sign in with a link received by email.

1. Install the « Jobbbox » app from the App Store (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play (Android), then open it.
2. On the « Sign in to Jobbbox » screen, enter « Your email address », then tap « Send me a sign-in link ».
3. On this same phone, open the email you received and tap the link: the app opens and you are signed in. The link is valid for 15 minutes and works once; if it is no longer valid, the app tells you: ask for another one with « Send the link again », or with « Send me a sign-in link » if the app is back on the « Sign in to Jobbbox » screen.
4. If your account has two-step verification, the app shows « Two-step verification »: it is coming to the app soon. Meanwhile, tap « Sign out » or sign in on jobbbox.ai.

If you open the link on a device where the app is not installed, it signs you in on jobbbox.ai, in the browser.

To sign out of the app, open « My account » and tap « Sign out ». A session closed from « My account » on jobbbox.ai also signs the app out, as soon as it comes back to the foreground.
