title: What happens when I am no longer a coach on Jobbbox?
synonyms: [retired coach, retired human coach, coach removed, no longer a coach, stop, end of collaboration, leave jobbbox, coach space unavailable, page not found, I no longer have access, my past sessions, my reviews]
---
When your collaboration with Jobbbox ends, the Jobbbox team removes you from the list of coaches. You then become a retired coach.

Candidates no longer see you in « Choose a coach »: they can no longer book a session with you, nor give you coach access. You can no longer read Candidate files: the address of your coach space shows « Page not found », and « My coach space » leaves your menu. The coach access candidates had given you no longer lets you open their profiles or applications.

Nothing that took place is deleted. Your past sessions stay in the candidates' agenda, and the reviews you sent them stay on their applications, under « Coach reviews ».

You think this is a mistake? Write to the Jobbbox team: « How do I contact you? » explains how.

Historical financial statements and tax copies remain accessible to their authorized owner through private links. They grant no access to Candidate files; Support continues following up any refund already owed.
