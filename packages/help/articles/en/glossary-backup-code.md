title: Backup code
synonyms: [backup code, backup codes, recovery code, recovery codes, lost phone, two-step verification]

A backup code stands in for your authenticator app's code when you do not have it at hand, for instance if you lost your phone. Jobbbox gives you 10 of them when you turn on two-step verification, shown only once: copy or download them, and keep them somewhere safe.

Each code works only once. The « Security » section of My account shows how many you have left; « Renew the backup codes » gives you 10 new ones, and the old ones stop working. Jobbbox does not keep your codes in readable form and cannot show them to you again.
