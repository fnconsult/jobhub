## Sign in
- goto /en
- click link « Sign in »

Click « Sign in », at the top of every public page.

## Get your sign-in link
- fill label « Your email address » « claire.new@motions.jobbbox.test »
- click button « Send me a sign-in link »

Enter « Your email address », then click « Send me a sign-in link ».

## Open the email
- open-email « Your Jobbbox sign-in link »
- click link « Sign in to Jobbbox »

Open the email « Your Jobbbox sign-in link » and click « Sign in to Jobbbox », within 15 minutes. Your account is created, on the Free Plan. If Jobbbox asks you to accept the Terms of use, read them, tick the box then click « Accept and continue ».

## Create your first profile
- click link « Create my first profile »

In My account, under Profiles, click « Create my first profile ». Your coach welcomes you in the conversation.

## Drop your CV
- upload label « Your CV » « cv-claire-demo.pdf »
- click button « Read my CV »

Drop your CV in the « I have a CV » card, as a PDF or a Word file, then click « Read my CV ». Without a CV, click « Answer my coach's questions » instead.

## Confirm what your coach read
- fill label « Your answer » « Responsable comptable »
- click button « Send to the coach »
- fill label « Your answer » « Lyon »
- click button « Send to the coach »
- click button « Skip this question »
- click button « Skip this question »
- click button « Skip this question »

While it reads, the « Profile in progress » card shows what your coach finds in your CV, then folds. It has you confirm the target role and the place it read: click « Yes, … », or write your own. It then asks only what a CV does not say, the contract type, remote work and the minimum salary, and each question can be skipped with « Skip this question ».

## Read the summary
- zoom region « Your job search »
- click button « See the details »

Your coach sums up what it understood in the « Your job search » card. Click « See the details » to check your CV and search criteria.

## Check the details
- zoom complementary « Check your CV and your search criteria »
- click checkbox « Permanent (CDI) »

In the pane that opens beside the conversation, check your CV and search criteria, such as the « Target role » and the « Location ». Correct what needs it, here the contract type: the summary updates.

## Your profile is ready
- zoom off
- click button « That looks right »
- click link « Continue to my profile »

If the summary is right, click « That looks right ». Your first job offers show in « Activity », at the end of the search on us your coach started: « In Activity » takes you there. Without waiting, click « Continue to my profile »: your first profile is ready.
