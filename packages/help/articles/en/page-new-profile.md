A profile is a role you are aiming for: a Master CV and search criteria. You can have several, depending on your plan.

The page is a conversation with your coach, like every other: it opens on its welcome panel (where it says it is an AI and what the page does; « Don't show again » closes it for good), and the message box, at the bottom, carries the « AI Coach » badge, which opens that panel again, and the « Send to the coach » button. If you have a CV, drop it in the « I have a CV » card: your coach reads it and proposes a Master CV. If not, click « Answer my coach's questions », above the message box: it asks about your career, one question at a time, and you answer in the message box; allow about ten minutes.

Either way, your coach then says « Here is how I understand what you are looking for. Did I get it right? » and sums up your search in the « Your job search » card, in a few sentences. The key items are in bold: the target role, the contract types, the places, remote work, the minimum salary, your years of experience (counted from the dates of your jobs), your main skills and your latest roles. This summary is composed by Jobbbox from your profile, not by an AI: what you did not give is not in it.

If it is right, click « That looks right »: your profile is saved and your coach confirms it in the conversation; « Continue to my profile » opens its page. « See the details » opens your Master CV and search criteria beside the conversation: check them and correct what needs it, and the summary updates with your changes. « Save my profile », at the bottom of that pane, saves your profile too. For now, « Edit » opens the same pane. Closed, the pane opens again with « See the details », with nothing lost. Nothing is added that you did not say.

When you answer the questions, your profile is created as soon as you have given the role you are looking for and your city: it appears among your profiles and counts in your plan. If your plan has no room left, your coach tells you so in the conversation and shows you the plan that includes more. Your next answers stay on the page until « That looks right » or « Save my profile », which completes that same profile: its search criteria, then a single new version of its Master CV. If you leave the page before, the profile stays, with what was already saved; its page says « Creation to finish », and « Resume creating it » brings you back here, to the review, to complete and save it.

« Activity », at the top of the page, shows what is happening. While your coach reads your CV, « Right now » shows each step of the reading, which you can stop. The history then groups the whole creation in a single row, « Profile creation »: the CV reading, with the model that did it and its tokens, the only step done by AI; the « Onboarding questionnaire », whose questions are written by Jobbbox, « No AI », with the number of your answers; the « Review »; then « Profile created », with version 1 of your Master CV. When you answer the questions, « Profile created » shows as soon as the profile is created, before the review. The same row follows you to your new profile's page, with its first ATS Score once computed. The questionnaire and the review are not kept: once the page is reloaded, the row shows only the CV reading, version 1 and the ATS Score. The « Conversation context » stays empty, « The coach has not replied yet. », until your coach's first reply in the profile's conversation.

You can also write straight in the message box: your first message starts the questions. During the review, your coach only sends you back to it; once the profile is saved, you can talk to it about the profile on its page.

To answer the questionnaire, click « Send to the coach » or press Enter; for a yes-or-no question, click « Yes » or « No », or type it. The message box starts on one line and grows with your text, up to about eight lines; longer text scrolls inside it. Enter sends your answer and Shift+Enter adds a line. After sending, the box returns to one line.
