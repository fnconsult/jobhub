Your coach tailors your Master CV to a job offer, never inventing anything. You check each change before saving it, then you apply yourself.

1. Open the application, then its « Documents » section.
2. Click « Propose a tailored CV ». When « Proposal ready: check the changes before saving. » shows, the match score before and after is given.
3. If the job offer asks for what your CV does not show, answer « The coach's questions »: « Yes, I have it » or « No ». A point is added only if you confirm you have it.
4. Under « Changes from the reference CV », click « Accept » or « Reject » for each change. A rejected change keeps what your Master CV says.
5. Click « Save the tailored CV ». It replaces the tailored CV already saved for this application, if there is one.
6. Under « Download this tailored CV », choose a CV template, then « Download as PDF » or « Download as Word (.docx) ».
7. Send your application yourself, on the job offer's site or by email: Jobbbox sends nothing on your behalf.
8. In the application's fiche, set the « Status » to « Applied ». If it gets no answer, your coach will suggest a follow-up.

The cover letter is prepared in the same section, with « Draft the letter ».
