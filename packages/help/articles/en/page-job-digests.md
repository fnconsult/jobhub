« Job Digests » is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one every visit. A Job Digest is a selection of new job offers matching a profile's search criteria: you get it by email and find it here too.

Your Job Digests are listed there in a card, by profile: how often it comes, which you set there, within your plan (every week on Monday at 7 am, or every day at 7 am, Paris time), then each Job Digest sent, with its date and its number of job offers. Click a line to open it, or ask the coach which job offers to look at first.

Only job offers you have not seen yet are in it. When there are none, nothing is sent.

In the menu, « Job Digests » unfolds into your most recent Job Digests; « See all » leads to this page.
