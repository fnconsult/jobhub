title: Two-step verification
synonyms: [two-step verification, two-factor authentication, 2fa, mfa, second step, authenticator app, totp, security]
---
Two-step verification adds a second step to signing in by magic link: a 6-digit code from your authenticator app, or one of your backup codes. It is off until you turn it on in My account, « Security » section.

Until the second step is done, Jobbbox opens nothing but the page asking for the code: none of your pages, nor the extension, which asks you to finish signing in on Jobbbox. Signing in with a provider, such as Google, asks for no code. The code is asked at every sign-in by magic link, on every device.
