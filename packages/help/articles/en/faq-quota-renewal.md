title: When does my quota renew?
synonyms: [renewal, renew, reset, monday, limit reached, usage]
---
Every Monday at midnight (Paris time). The « Weekly » bar, at the foot of the menu, shows where you stand; « Your usage this week », in My subscription, details it quota by quota.

Talking to your coach does not count against the quota. A job search that fails, or that you stop before it has found a job offer, does not count either.

To get more each week, change plan from My subscription.
