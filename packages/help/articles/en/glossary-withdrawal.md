kind: glossary
title: Withdrawal
synonyms: [withdraw, renounce, cooling-off period, 14 days, refund]
---
Your right to renounce a contract made online, without a reason: for 14 days after you subscribe to a plan, or after you pay for a session with a human coach.

On Jobbbox, use « Renoncer au contrat ici » (withdraw from the contract here), on the My subscription page or on the session in the agenda. The contract stops at once and you get an acknowledgement by email. You are refunded in full, unless you asked at the order for the service to start at once: a subscription is then refunded less the share of the days elapsed, and a session that has taken place can no longer be withdrawn from.
