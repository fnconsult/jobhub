title: What does the company dossier hold?
synonyms: [company dossier, company, employer, siren, register, revenue, headcount, executives, news, recruitment agency, recruiter, research]
---
The company dossier gathers the public information about an application's employer: its identity, its address, its key figures, its executives' roles, the roles to contact and its news. Open the application's « Company dossier » section, then click « Build the company dossier ».

The dossier says where its information comes from. « Official source. »: the French company register. « Web sources, less reliable. »: websites, to be checked, for a foreign employer for example. For a French company, executives are given by their role, never by their name.

If the job offer is posted by a recruitment agency, Jobbbox suggests the real employer it guesses: confirm it, or give the right one, with « Confirm the employer ». If it guesses none, give the employer, then click « Build the dossier for this employer ». No lookup is made before you confirm, nor on a sole trader. « Not the right employer? » lets you give its name or its SIREN.

« Update the dossier » rebuilds it, with recent news. A target company's dossier also opens from the profile's « Target companies » section.
