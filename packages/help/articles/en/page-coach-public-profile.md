Edit “About you”, “Specialties”, “Languages” and your “Professional photo”, then choose “Send for approval”. “Candidate preview” shows your proposal; “Currently visible version” remains the approved one. A proposal never replaces it before approval.

Photos accept JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB and 20 megapixels. They are cropped square and metadata is removed. “Remove current photo” proposes removal, subject to the same approval. A refusal shows its reason and is emailed to you; correct and submit again.

“Your offer and its seller” summarises the existing offer. Your time zone and permanent meeting contact are also set here; they are not part of the Candidate-facing profile. The meeting contact appears only to authorised participants around their session.
