title: Offers to handle

« Offers to handle » gathers, at the top of Job searches, every job offer that reached you: through a search, a Job Digest, a job offer email you forwarded or a link pasted in a conversation with your coach. The same job offer is listed once, with a chip for each way it arrived.

A job offer is to handle as long as you have not decided anything. « Follow » makes it an application, at « To apply »: it is then « Followed ». « Set aside » puts it aside, with a reason if you wish: it is then « Set aside ». Followed or set aside, it counts among those handled. Opening or reading it does not handle it.

« Later » puts it aside until the next Monday without handling it: it stays to handle and in the count. A job offer left without a decision more than 30 days after it was posted moves to « Expired without a decision », out of the count; it is never set aside for you.

Each job offer is kept there 12 months after it arrived, and deleted with your account or with its profile.
