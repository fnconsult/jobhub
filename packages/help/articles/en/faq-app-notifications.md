title: Which notifications does the app send?
synonyms: [notification, notifications, push notification, push, app notifications, lock screen, alert, reminder, allow, refuse, settings, mobile app, Jobbbox app]

The Jobbbox app can show a notification on your phone's or tablet's lock screen when:

- a job search has found new results;
- your Job Digest is ready;
- you have an appointment the next day: an interview or a coaching session, the day before at 6 PM (Paris time), with the same rules as the reminder email;
- your coach has left a review on one of your applications;
- your coach has set, moved or cancelled the date of a session;
- an application is waiting for a follow-up.

A notification never names a company, a job or a person. Tapping it opens the app on what it announces: the application, the agenda, your job searches or your Job Digests. Each event sends only one notification, even when the processing runs again.

The app does not ask for permission when it first starts. It asks when it is useful: when you start a job search with « Start a search », in Job searches, a screen first explains what notifications are for (« Get notified of new results? »), and the device asks for permission only if you tap « Turn on notifications ». If you tap « Not now » or refuse, the app does not ask again.

In My account, « Reminders » section, « App notifications » has one switch per event: an event switched off sends nothing. The reminder email has its own switch. If you refused notifications on the device, the app's « Reminders » section explains how to turn them on in the device's Settings and offers « Open Settings ».
