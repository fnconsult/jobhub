title: Document version
synonyms: [versions, earlier version, history, restore, old version, old letter, versions of my letter]
---
A saved state of an application's tailored CV, cover letter or outreach message. Each draft by your AI coach, each save of your changes, each edit by your coach that you apply and each restore creates one, with its date, its origin, its language and, for a message, its subject. Several changes saved at once make a single version.

You find them under « Versions », in each tab of the « Documents » section: the current one reads « Current version ». « Restore this version » makes an earlier version the current one again, under a new number, deleting nothing. Jobbbox keeps the last 20 versions of each document; they are deleted with the application and with your account, and are part of your data export.
