When tax collection is activated after the necessary texts become effective, **Income → Tax information** lets you enter your identity, address, professional TIN, SIRET and payout account. An individual, including a sole trader, also enters their first and last name and date of birth. A legal entity enters its legal name. The current flow handles French tax identities without a permanent establishment outside France; contact the team for another situation.

Coaching services do not benefit from the low-activity threshold for sales of goods. Amounts are prepared by credit quarter, including known refunds and fees, separately from bank payouts. Actual Coach compensation for a session sold by Jobbbox is distinguished from Jobbbox’s retail turnover. The team checks the reporting scope before transmission.

The operator prepares its annual return and Seller copies by 31 January following the income year. Your private copy is available from **Income**; an email informs you when the file has passed structural validation. A “Prepared document, not filed” document proves neither filing nor acceptance by DGFiP. Human checks and filing steps remain necessary. Corrections after preparation follow the original file references; the team does not create a second initial return.

This information does not determine your tax or personal tax and social obligations. Consult the relevant authority for your circumstances. Tax information is encrypted at rest and available only to authorized people for your business; legal retention may continue after account closure.

A preparation replaced before filing is identified as such in its earlier copy. Your Income list offers the current copy; a new preparation does not silently rewrite the earlier one.
