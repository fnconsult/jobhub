## What Jobbbox does {#service}

Jobbbox helps you organise and carry out your job search. Depending on your plan, it lets you in particular:

- create one or more profiles, with their search criteria, and upload your CV, which Jobbbox reads to make a master CV you can edit;
- find job offers: Jobbbox searches for them on the web and with public sources of job offers, receives those you forward to it by email, and saves those you add with the extension;
- compute a match score between your CV and an offer, and a score of how well applicant tracking software can read your CV;
- prepare CVs tailored to an offer, cover letters and outreach or follow-up messages;
- build a file on the employer from public data;
- follow your applications and interviews in an agenda, with reminders;
- receive, if you ask for it, a regular summary of new offers;
- talk with the AI coach, and book a session with a human coach.

**What Jobbbox does not do.** Jobbbox does not apply on your behalf and sends no message in your name: it prepares drafts, which you reread, edit and send yourself. Jobbbox is not an employer, a recruitment firm or a placement agency, and **guarantees no interview and no hiring**.

**Your tailored CVs.** A tailored CV only uses facts present in your master CV or that you have confirmed. It is up to you to check, before sending it, that everything in it is accurate.

**Job offers.** Offers come from employers and third-party sites. We do not check their accuracy and do not guarantee that they are still open; Jobbbox flags an offer it knows has expired. Jobbbox does not read sites whose terms of use forbid it.

**Ranking of offers.** The match score, from 0 to 100, is computed by fixed rules, with no payment from any employer or site. It compares your CV and your criteria with the offer on five criteria, with this weight: skills (40), experience (20), location (15), contract type (15) and salary (10). A criterion that the offer or your criteria give nothing to compare is not counted; a score resting on less than half of these weights is marked "partial". When the offer does not state an item, an artificial intelligence may read the offer's text (never your CV) to find it. The score's breakdown is shown with it.

Its replies and the documents prepared with AI may contain errors or omissions: they are suggestions, not professional, legal or financial advice. You alone decide what you do with them.

**Checks.** Automatic checks review your messages to the coach and its replies: attempts to divert the coach, leaks of personal data, advice outside the scope of a job search or dangerous advice, distress. If a message suggests you are in distress, the coach gives you the French national suicide prevention number, 3114; no one is alerted. These checks reduce risks without removing them.
