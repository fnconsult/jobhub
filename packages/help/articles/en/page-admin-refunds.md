The Withdrawals section distinguishes legal withdrawals from contractual session-cancellation follow-up. Support with write access can retry a failed refund; other authorised access remains read-only. The coach’s reason is private information, never a public note.

Refunds already owed remain in the financial list after the account and personal follow-up are erased. Deleted names, addresses and reasons are not reconstructed. A privileged retry request is audited before the financial attempt; its outcome remains in the refund facts.

An uncertain state requires reconciliation in the original Stripe account. Do not create another refund by guessing an earlier write’s outcome. Activation conditions are separately validated by human owners; this page approves no legal wording.
