title: Handle a coaching content report
synonyms: [DSA moderation, remove offer, suspend coach]
---
1. In **Feedback**, open “Coaching content reports”. Check the identified content and evidence. Do not copy the reporter’s contact details or explanation into the reason sent to the parties.
2. Choose “Remove content”, “Suspend content” or “Keep content available”. Write the reason and press “Record and notify decision”. A reason is required and the decision is audited.

The reporter and content owner each receive a message. The Worker retries delivery failures independently for each recipient. Restrictions block page visibility or new orders; they do not erase paid sessions. A restriction remains effective after reporter data is erased; a new reasoned decision can lift it. Other restrictive reports remain effective.
