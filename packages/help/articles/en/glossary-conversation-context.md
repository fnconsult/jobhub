title: Conversation context
synonyms: [context, conversation context, tokens, context bar, the coach forgets, messages read, context breakdown, free space]

The conversation context is what your AI coach reads again before each reply: the conversation's latest messages and what it knows of you and of the page. « Activity », at the top of the page, shows it in a bar: the conversation's number of messages out of those the coach reads, and the tokens of its context out of its ceiling. Both limits depend on your plan (40 messages and 12k tokens, unless your plan sets others), and may be unlimited. A token is a small piece of text, about four characters; it is not your quota.

On a job search, a job digest, an application or a job offer, the coach also reads the reference CV of the profile the offers are scored against, without your name or contact details, and the reasons behind each match score: skills present and missing, seniority, location, salary and contract. When you ask which offers suit you, it relies on your experience and skills; if it lacks something, such as your experience or what matters to you, it asks you for it.

These figures come from Jobbbox: they are those of the context sent to the coach for its latest reply. From 80 %, the bar tells you that the coach reads only the latest messages and offers « Clear the conversation »: once you confirm, every message of the conversation is deleted, and the coach's memory stays. When a plan above yours has the coach read more, the bar also names it, with « Discover the … plan », which leads to its card in My subscription.

« See the breakdown », under the bar, opens the context breakdown: Messages, Coach instructions, Profile and CV, Agents' advice, Sources and Free space, as a bar and a list, in tokens and as a percentage of your plan's ceiling. It is an estimate, that of the coach's latest reply: the breakdown is empty before its first reply and after « Clear ».
