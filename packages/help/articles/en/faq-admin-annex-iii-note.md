**Internal note dated 2 November 2026. Internal, never published. This is not legal advice.**

**Conclusion.** No current Jobbbox feature falls within Annex III of the AI Act. The obligations of high-risk systems therefore do not apply, nor does Article 6(4), which only covers a system within Annex III.

**Why.** Annex III, point 4(a), covers AI systems intended for the recruitment or selection of people, notably to place targeted job advertisements, filter applications and evaluate candidates. Point 4(b) covers decisions on an ongoing work relationship. Jobbbox is a service for the candidate, started and controlled by them, outside the employer's process.
- Tailored CV, cover letter, ATS Score: example 14 of the Commission's Service Desk (tailoring one's CV to a job offer) is out of scope.
- Job discovery, Target Companies, Match Score, Job Digest, Email intake: example 15 (helping the candidate find the right job, ranking job offers for them alone) is out of scope.
- AI Coach, playbooks, Company Dossier, outreach message: advice to the candidate; no output goes to an employer to select them.
- Human Coach: no AI ranks the candidate for a third party.

Jobbbox remains the provider of its AI systems (AI Coach, documents written, Back Office Assistant), and the deployer of the assistant. It owes the information at the point of use of Article 50 and the AI literacy of Article 4.

**When to review this note.** Before any feature, before it is designed, that would bring Jobbbox into scope:
- an Organisation role, a B2B offer or an outplacement service where AI ranks, shortlists, scores or recommends candidates to an employer or a recruiter (examples 1, 2 and 8);
- job offers sponsored by employers and targeted at candidates by AI (example 7);
- the publication of the Commission's final guidelines on classification, expected at the end of 2026;
- at the latest before 2 December 2027, when the Annex III obligations apply.

Every review gives this note a new date.

**To be confirmed by a lawyer.** The « out of scope » finding once the final guidelines are out, and before any B2B or Organisation feature; the text of Article 6(4) after the Digital Omnibus, to be read on EUR-Lex.
