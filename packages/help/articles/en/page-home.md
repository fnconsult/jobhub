The home page is where you start in Jobbbox: a new conversation with your AI coach. Write in the message box at the foot of the page, or pick a suggestion just above it: « Search for job offers », « Add an application », « Schedule an interview », « Ask the coach ». The box takes one line and grows with your text; Shift+Enter starts a new line, Enter sends. While you write, « AI Coach » and « Send to the coach » shrink to « AI » and the Enter key's icon; two seconds of mouse on one of them shows its name. The conversation is saved with your first message and appears under « Coach » in the menu, where you can rename or delete it. « New conversation », at the top of the menu, starts another one; « Clear », at the top of the page, deletes all its messages.

To begin, create a profile: your Master CV and your search criteria. Your coach uses them to search for job offers and prepare your applications. The menu leads to your applications, your agenda, your job searches and your Job Digests; « My account » is at the foot of the menu.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), the home page is the same new conversation. The coach introduces itself in one sentence: it is an AI, not a person, and you can book a human coach. Below, « Your day at a glance » only shows what is waiting for you: your offers to handle, your next interview or your next session with a coach (or the sessions to schedule), the applications to follow up and your latest conversation, to resume. Tap a line to open its page. A line with nothing to show does not appear; with nothing at all, the card does not appear. Until you have a profile, the card is called « To get started »: « Send my CV » opens the choice of a PDF or Word file, which the coach reads to create your profile; « I don't have an up-to-date CV » opens profile creation on jobbbox.ai, with its questions, one at a time.

A question about Jobbbox? While you write, the help's matching articles show above the message box, « In the help, without AI », in place of the suggestions. An article opens beside the conversation, as « How does this page work? » does while the conversation is empty; « Ask the coach » there starts a question about it. « Activity », at the top of the page, opens beside the conversation what AI did for you and the conversation context.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. The top bar fits on one line: « Menu », the page's title, one action as an icon (here « Activity ») and « … », which lists the others by name, such as « Clear the conversation ». While an AI action runs, the icon shown is « Activity »'s, with its stop icon. An article, « Activity » or a form opens over it, full screen: « Close » closes it and brings you back to the conversation. « Menu », at the top of the page, opens the menu.

At the foot of the menu, your account's button shows the name the coaches use (otherwise your first name, otherwise « My account ») and, under it, your plan, for example « Premium », followed, once you have cancelled it, by the day you keep it until; beside them, the initials of your first and last names. It opens a small menu: at its top, your first name, last name and email address; under « Help and legal », the Help Centre (« Help »), the legal texts (« Legal ») and what's new in Jobbbox (« What's new »); then the My account page, « My subscription », with your plan's name at the end of the line, and « Sign out ». On a phone too, it is at the foot of the menu.
