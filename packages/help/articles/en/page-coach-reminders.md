When the new coaching notifications are activated, this page controls session emails and your morning summary. Changes save immediately. The summary has its own switch; app notifications are controlled independently, event by event.

You receive payment, booking, rescheduling and cancellation updates, then reminders the day before at 18:00 and one hour before the session. Coach reminder times follow the time zone chosen in your workspace. The summary is prepared at 07:30 on days with upcoming sessions; it does not contain the Candidate’s private notes. Cal.com does not send a second set of reminders.

“Join” opens the private session page. The call link becomes available ten minutes before it starts. A cancelled or withdrawn session receives no further reminder. Messages from “Cancellations and refunds” are not switched off by this page’s session-update and reminder settings.
