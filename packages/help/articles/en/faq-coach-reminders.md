title: When do you receive session reminders?
synonyms: [your reminders, coach reminder, day before, one hour, morning summary, time zone]
requires: coach-space-v2
---
When the new notifications are activated, Jobbbox informs you of a paid, booked, rescheduled or cancelled session. The day-before reminder is prepared at 18:00 in your time zone for dates known then. A later booking or rescheduling keeps its change notification; it receives no additional day-before reminder.

An email and app notification also remind you one hour before. “Join” opens the private session; the call becomes available ten minutes before the start. Cancelled, withdrawn and retired-coach sessions are excluded from reminders.

At 07:30 in your time zone, a summary groups sessions still upcoming that day. It is not sent on days without an upcoming session. Disable it in **Coach space → Reminders**, independently of other emails and app notifications. If the service resumes later in the morning, it may arrive late; it is sent only once for that local day.

Reminders come from Jobbbox: Cal.com’s are switched off before integrated booking is activated. Date and status checks run again before delivery to exclude obsolete reminders after a change.
