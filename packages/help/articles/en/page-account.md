My account gathers your settings, in sections: Profiles, Subscription, Follow-ups, Reminders, Email intake, European AI, Sign-in, Security, Preferences, Tools, My feedback and My data. Each section has its own address; on a phone, « My account » takes you back to the list of sections.

Profiles lists your active profiles, then the archived ones. Subscription shows your plan and how much of it you used this week: it has its own article, My subscription.

In Follow-ups, choose after how many working days your coach suggests a follow-up; in Reminders, the reminder email the day before your appointments.

Email intake lets you receive in Jobbbox the offers from your job alerts and from recruiters' messages. « Switch email intake on » gives you your forwarding address: forward those emails to it, « Copy the address » copies it, and « Set up forwarding step by step » guides you to do so from your mailbox. Only the text of forwarded emails is read, never their attachments. « Replace the address » gives you a new one and « Switch off » stops email intake: the old address stops working at once, the offers already received stay. In « Your sending addresses », add the addresses you forward from by hand; in « Your alert senders », add the address of an alert Jobbbox does not recognise yet, or switch off, for yourself alone, a sender « Recognised by Jobbbox ». The offers received show in « Received by email », on the Job searches page.

European AI: with your consent, every AI request that contains your identity data (name, address, phone, email or the text of your CV) is processed by Mistral AI, a European AI provider. « Give my consent » switches it on, « Withdraw my consent » stops it, at any time; the section shows the date of your consent.

Sign-in shows your account's email address and the ways you can sign in.

Security manages two-step verification, off until you turn it on. You turn it on with an authenticator app, with a passkey, or with both.

« Turn on with an app » shows a QR code and the key to enter in your authenticator app; enter the « Code the app shows », then click « Confirm ». « Turn on with a passkey » asks for a « Passkey name », for example Marie's iPhone; « Create the passkey » then brings up your device's prompt (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or your security key). Either way, your 10 backup codes are then shown once: « Copy » or « Download » to keep them, then « I have kept my codes ».

From then on, every sign-in by magic link asks for a second step before Jobbbox opens: « Use a passkey » if you have one, otherwise your app's code, or one of your backup codes. Signing in with a provider, such as Google, does not ask for it.

The section shows since when it is on, the app, each passkey with its name, its device when it is known, when it was added and last used, and how many backup codes are left. « Add a passkey » saves another one, on another device for instance. « Rename » changes a passkey's name, « Remove » takes it away. Removing your last passkey, when you have no app, turns the verification off. « Renew the backup codes » gives you 10 new ones, the old ones no longer working; « Turn off » stops the verification and forgets the app, the passkeys and the codes. To renew the codes, turn it off or remove your last passkey, if your last verification is more than 10 minutes old, the section first asks for your passkey or a code. An email tells you when it is turned on or off.

For an administrator or a coach, two-step verification is mandatory: the section says so and has no « Turn off ». Without the app, a passkey or backup codes, a super-admin resets it.

In Preferences, change the interface language, the text size and the theme: Light, Dark, or System, which follows your device's setting. The theme and the text size are remembered on this device. The box « Get an email when Jobbbox announces something new » stays unticked until you tick it: ticked, you get at most one email a week when a new version brings something important, with no open or click tracking, and the box says since when you are subscribed. Untick it, or use the link at the bottom of the email, to stop receiving it.

Tools offers the Jobbbox Chrome extension.

My feedback lists the bugs, ideas and questions you sent the Jobbbox team, newest first, with their status (« Sent », « Read by the team » or « Done ») and the « Reply from the Jobbbox team ». Opening the section clears your feedback notifications. « Delete » removes a piece of feedback: the team no longer sees it. With none sent yet, « Report a problem » and « Suggest an idea » let you send one.

My data lets you download all your data or delete your account.

Under each section, « Jobbbox version » gives the version you are using, for example 0.1.0. On the beta, it is followed by the exact change served, for example 0.1.0 (main·3f9c2a1): quote it if you report a problem.

My account is not a conversation: « Help », at the top of the page, opens this article beside the page (over it, full screen, on a phone). « Ask the coach » there starts a new conversation with your AI coach about My account.
