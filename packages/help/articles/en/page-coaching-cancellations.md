When the new coaching conditions are activated, this private page lets you cancel sessions within the 24-hour limit and follow existing cancellation requests. Each follow-up names the original coach. Times use your time zone, with Paris time beside them when different.

A coach cancellation opens three choices: full refund, continuing with the same coach without another payment, or another approved coach at the same or a lower price. The deadline is shown; without a choice within seven days, a refund is requested automatically. Choosing another coach first refunds the original order, then presents a new order to accept: choosing does not trigger another payment.

Refund status distinguishes the request, Stripe confirmation and required support intervention. Receipt time depends on your bank. A cancellation reported only by Cal.com awaits confirmation of the coach’s reason; the Candidate’s limit still uses the original date. In the app, this same page opens in Jobbbox’s authenticated web companion.
