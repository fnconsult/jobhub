title: Edit and restore your Master CV

Each save of your Master CV creates a new version. The earlier ones can still be viewed and restored.

1. Open the profile, then its « Master CV » section.
2. Edit the « CV title », the « Profile (summary) » or the « Skills, one per line » in place, then click « Save version … ». « Cancel » drops your changes.
3. For your contact details, jobs, education and languages, click « Edit my Master CV »: the full editor opens. Add, change, move or remove items, then click « Save as a new version ».
4. To go back, click « Version history », in the « Master CV » section. The current version is marked « Current version ».
5. Click « View version … » to read it again, then « Restore version … ».
6. The restored version becomes the current version, marked « Restored from version … ». The history is kept: nothing is lost.

A tailored CV already saved on an application does not change when you edit your Master CV; a proposal in progress must be asked for again.
