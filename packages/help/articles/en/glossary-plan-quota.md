kind: glossary
title: Quota
synonyms: [limit, usage, credits, allowance, usage this week]
---
What your plan includes: the number of profiles you can have, and each week the number of Match Scores, ATS Scores, job searches and enriched contacts, as well as the Job Digest frequency.

The « Usage this week » bar, at the foot of the menu, shows where you stand. The count starts again from zero every Monday at midnight (Paris time).

Talking to your coach does not count against the quota.
