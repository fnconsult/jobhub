kind: faq
title: How do I report a problem or reach the team?
synonyms: [feedback, report, bug, problem, idea, support, team, person, reach, talk to someone]
---
For a problem or an idea, send feedback: « Report a problem » or « Suggest an idea », at the foot of the « Help and coach » panel. You also find it with the icon beside « My account », in the menu on phones.

To ask a person a question, ask your AI coach first. If its reply doesn't help, answer « No » under it: « Ask the team » sends your question and the coach's reply to the Jobbbox team.

The Jobbbox team reads every piece of feedback. You follow its status and the team's reply in Jobbbox.

There is no support email address: feedback is how you reach a person.
