title: Does the help use AI?
synonyms: [artificial intelligence, robot, human, written by, help, ai coach, help centre]
---
No. The help calls on no AI to answer you: its articles are checked and published by the Jobbbox team, and the search finds them from your own words. It works even when the AI is unavailable and never counts against a quota.

Only your coach answers with AI, and its replies say so. When the help has no answer, « Send to the coach » asks it your question.

All the help is also in the Help Centre, open without an account: « Help and legal », at the foot of the menu, then « Help ». An article opened beside the conversation offers « Open in the Help Centre ».

If your coach had to answer a question about using Jobbbox, the question is noted, without your name, so the team can write the missing article.
