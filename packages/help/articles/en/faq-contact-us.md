Jobbbox has no support mailbox: whether or not you have an account, you reach the Jobbbox team in a different way.

With an account, sign in and ask your question in « Help and coach »: the help's articles show first, then your AI coach answers. If its reply doesn't help, answer « No » under it: « Ask the team » sends your question, with the coach's reply, to the Jobbbox team. You follow its answer in My account, under My feedback. For a problem or an idea: « Report a problem » or « Suggest an idea ».

Without an account, write to {{contact-email}}, the address of the legal notice.
