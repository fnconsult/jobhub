title: How do I unsubscribe from a Job Digest?
synonyms: [unsubscribe, opt out, stop receiving, stop the emails, job digest, offers by email, newsletter, alert]
---
In a Job Digest received by email, click « Stop receiving this profile's Job Digest ». The page « Unsubscribe from the Job Digest » opens, without signing in: click « Unsubscribe ». It confirms « Done: you will no longer receive this Job Digest. »

Only this profile's Job Digest stops; those of your other profiles go on. You can turn it back on at any time from the profile's page.

If the page shows « This unsubscribe link is no longer valid », you may already be unsubscribed; otherwise, sign in and turn the Job Digest off from the profile's page.
