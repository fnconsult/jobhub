title: Your video-call link
---
1. In “Public page”, enter “Your permanent contact”: choose “Video call” and an HTTPS link, or “Phone” and who calls. For an unrecognised host, tick “I confirm this video-call host.” yourself. Click “Save contact”.
2. For one session, open it in “My sessions” and enter “This session’s contact”. It overrides the permanent contact. The link is offered from ten minutes before until thirty minutes after the scheduled end. Do not record the session or invite an automatic note-taking assistant; these uses are governed by the coach contract.
