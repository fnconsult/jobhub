The home page is where you start in Jobbbox: a new conversation with your AI coach. Write in the message box at the foot of the page, or pick a suggestion just above it: « Search for job offers », « Add an application », « Schedule an interview », « Ask the coach ». The conversation is saved with your first message and appears under « Coach » in the menu, where you can rename or delete it. « New conversation », at the top of the menu, starts another one; « Clear », at the top of the page, deletes all its messages.

To begin, create a profile: your Master CV and your search criteria. Your coach uses them to search for job offers and prepare your applications. The menu leads to your applications, your agenda, your job searches and your Job Digests; « My account » is at the foot of the menu.

A question about Jobbbox? While you write, the help's matching articles show above the message box, « In the help, without AI », in place of the suggestions. An article opens beside the conversation, as « How does this page work? » does while the conversation is empty; « Ask the coach » there starts a question about it. « Activity », at the top of the page, opens beside the conversation what AI did for you and the conversation context.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. An article, « Activity » or a form opens over it, full screen: « Close » closes it and brings you back to the conversation. « Menu », at the top of the page, opens the menu.

At the foot of the menu, « Help and legal » leads to the Help Centre (« Help »), the legal texts (« Legal ») and what's new in Jobbbox (« What's new »). On a phone, these links are in the menu.
