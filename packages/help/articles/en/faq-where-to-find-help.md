title: Where do I find Jobbbox's help?
synonyms: [help, help centre, help center, tutorial, how to, documentation, question, topic, search the help]
---
All of Jobbbox's help is in the Help Centre, open to everyone, no account needed: « Help » at the top of every public page, or « Help Centre » at the foot of the page.

Its articles are sorted into four topics: « Discover Jobbbox », « The extension », « Using Jobbbox » and « Coach space ». The search field, at the top of the Help Centre, looks through all of them, without AI. Tutorials there walk you through a task step by step.

Once signed in, « Help and legal », at the foot of the menu, leads to the Help Centre. When you write to your coach, the matching articles show first, « In the help, without AI »; an article opened there offers « Open in the Help Centre ».

The articles are written by the Jobbbox team, in French and in English.
