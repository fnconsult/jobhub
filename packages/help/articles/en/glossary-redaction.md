title: Removing your identity

Before sending, Jobbbox removes your identity from every AI request and every web search: every email address and every phone number, and what Jobbbox knows of you, your account's « First name », « Last name » and email, and the full name, first name, last name, address, phone and email of your Master CV. Each is replaced by a placeholder, such as `[NOM]`, `[PRÉNOM]` or `[E-MAIL]`. This is pseudonymisation.

Only the name you chose for the coaches is sent to the AI: the one under « What would you like the coaches to call you? », in My account, Identity section. The AI Coach calls you this; if you leave it empty, it calls you by no name. If it is your first name, your first name stays.

Its limits are stated plainly: a name Jobbbox does not know, a former manager's for example, goes through as it is; and the rest of your CV, your employers, your jobs, your dates, goes with the request. So it is still personal data.

When the reply comes back, Jobbbox puts your details back in place: a cover letter is still signed with your name. This removal applies to every request, with or without the « European AI » consent; with the consent, the requests that contain your identity data are also processed in the European Union.
