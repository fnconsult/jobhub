title: What happens when a job offer expires?
synonyms: [expired job offer, expired, expires, no longer published, withdrawn, deleted, posting gone, closed offer, position filled, report]
---
Jobbbox checks that job offers are still published. When one no longer is on its original site, its page says so: « Expired job offer: no longer published on its original site since … ».

Your application does not change: « Your application and its status stay as they are: you decide what happens next. » Its documents, its company dossier and its contacts stay too. A withdrawn job offer is not always a filled position: a follow-up is still possible.

If you see that a job offer is no longer published before Jobbbox does, click « Report this job offer as no longer published », in the application's fiche. It is for you only: the job offer does not change for others. « Undo the report » removes it.

To close an application, set its status to « Abandoned » or « Rejected ».
