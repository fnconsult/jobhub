The agenda shows your interviews and your sessions with a human coach. Show it as a list (upcoming appointments, day by day), by week or by month.

Add an interview with « Add an interview », or from the « Interviews » section of an application at the « Interview » stage.

Past appointments stay visible, dimmed. The day before at 6 pm, Jobbbox can email you a reminder: set it in My account, under Reminders.
