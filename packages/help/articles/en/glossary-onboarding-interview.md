title: Onboarding interview

The interview your AI coach leads to create a profile when you have no CV to upload: click « Answer my coach's questions », on the « Create a profile » page. It asks one question at a time, first the role you are looking for and the city where you want to work, and asks again when an answer is too vague.

When it can guess an answer, it suggests it: « Yes, … » approves it, « No, rather… » lets you write your own. Nothing enters your profile without your answer or your approval. If the AI does not answer, your coach goes on with its usual questions, without the AI.

Your answers are sent to the AI provider to prepare the next question; your name and contact details never are. The exchange is not kept as a conversation and does not count in your plan. In « Activity », the « Profile creation » row shows the « Onboarding interview », with the AI model and its tokens.
