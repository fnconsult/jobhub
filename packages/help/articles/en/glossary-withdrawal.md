---
kind: glossary
title: Withdrawal
synonyms: [withdraw, renounce, cooling-off period, 14 days, refund]
---
Your right to renounce a contract made online, without a reason and at no cost: for 14 days after you subscribe to a plan, or after you pay for a session with a human coach as long as it has not taken place.

On Jobbbox, use « Renoncer au contrat ici » (withdraw from the contract here), on the My subscription page or on the session in the agenda. The contract stops at once, you are refunded in full and you get an acknowledgement by email.
