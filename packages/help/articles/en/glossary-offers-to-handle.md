title: Offers to handle
synonyms: [to handle, handled, followed, follow, offer box, arrived job offers, inbox]
---
« Offers to handle » gathers, at the top of Job searches, every job offer that reached you: through a search, a Job Digest, a job offer email you forwarded or a link pasted in a conversation with your coach. The same job offer is listed once, with a chip for each way it arrived.

A job offer is to handle as long as you have not decided anything. « Follow » makes it an application, at « To apply »: it is then « Followed », and counted among those handled. Opening or reading it does not handle it.

Each job offer is kept there 12 months after it arrived, and deleted with your account or with its profile.
