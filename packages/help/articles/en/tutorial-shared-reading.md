title: Read a document with your Human Coach
synonyms: [shared reading, show my coach, PDF]
---
1. During your paid session’s joining window, open your CV or application documents and choose “Show my coach”. Choose the session. You can also select a document on the session page.
2. Scroll through the document: your coach follows live, even without Coach Access. Your coach can also present a PDF of up to ten megabytes and two hundred pages. Shared Reading is read-only, with no annotation or download control.
3. Choose “Stop” to remove the document immediately. The end of the session window also deletes the temporary document. Your other CVs and applications stay private. The call and screen sharing take place in your video tool.
