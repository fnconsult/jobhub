title: Unsubscribe from what's-new emails
synonyms: [unsubscribe, opt out, stop receiving, stop the emails, what's new, new version, newsletter, announcement]
---
In a what's-new email, click « Stop receiving what's new by email ». The page « Unsubscribe from what's-new emails » opens, without signing in: click « Unsubscribe ». It confirms « Done: you will no longer receive what's-new emails. »

You can also, once signed in, untick « Get an email when Jobbbox announces something new » in My account, under Preferences. Your other emails, such as the Job Digest or appointment reminders, do not change.

If the page shows « This unsubscribe link is no longer valid », you may already be unsubscribed; otherwise, sign in and untick the box in My account, under Preferences. What's new can always be read on the What's new page.
