title: Without an account, how long are my CV and the job offer kept?
synonyms: [guest, without an account, session, 23 hours, 24 hours, delete, delete my data, retention, how long, is my cv kept, personal data, guest session]
---
Without an account, you use the extension as a guest. Your CV and the captured job offer stay in your browser, never in a Jobbbox account. Jobbbox reads your CV to compute the Match Score, without saving it.

Everything is deleted at the latest 24 hours after your first analysis, even if you keep using the extension, and as soon as you close your browser. The panel shows the exact date: « Your data will be deleted by … ». After that, the job offer is no longer available: capture it again to analyse it.

To delete everything sooner, click « Delete my data now », in the extension's panel. Your CV and the job offer are deleted from this browser at once.

To keep your work, create your account before then, without closing your browser: your CV becomes your first profile and the job offer your first application. The tutorial « Create your account from the extension and keep your work » explains how.
