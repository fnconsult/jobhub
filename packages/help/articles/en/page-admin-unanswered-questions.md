This page lists the questions about using Jobbbox that no help article answered, most asked first. They come from two places:

- a Candidate asked their AI coach, no article matched, and the AI coach said the question was about Jobbbox;
- an Administrator clicked « Request a tutorial » in the back office assistant, after a reply without a help article. Nothing is sent without that click.

Each question is kept redacted, without the name or address of whoever asked it: only its audience, the page or back office section it came from, and the language are kept.

Filter the list with « Filter by audience » (Visitor, Guest, Candidate, Human Coach, Administrator), « Filter by page » and « Filter by language ». « The AI's answer » shows what the AI coach or the assistant replied: raw material, never a published article.

Once the article is written, or a synonym added to the help, click « Article written ». For a question that is not a gap in the help, click « Dismiss ». Help editors and super-admins mark the questions; Support reads them.
