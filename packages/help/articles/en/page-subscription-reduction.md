When we reduce what your paid plan includes, you get an email at least 30 days before the date of the change: what changes, why and on which date. If the change does not suit you, you can cancel at no cost.

This page, opened from the email or from My subscription, sets out what changes, the reason and the date. « Confirm the cancellation » stops your subscription at once: you are back on the Free plan, and we refund prorata the part of the paid period that will not have been used. The refund shows on your statement within 5 to 10 days.

You can cancel this way before the date of the change, or up to 30 days after it. If you keep your subscription, there is nothing to do.
