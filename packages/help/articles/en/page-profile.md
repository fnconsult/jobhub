A profile gathers your Master CV and your search criteria for one role. Its sections: Suggestions, Master CV, ATS Score, Download and Manage.

Start with the ATS Score: it says whether recruitment software will read your CV well. The fixes your coach proposes appear in « Suggestions »; accept or dismiss them one by one.

Each time you save the Master CV, a new version is created, and you can go back to an earlier one. « Manage » lets you rename, duplicate or archive the profile.

With the new layout, a profile's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the profile's Master CV and search criteria in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Improve my CV », « Understand my ATS Score », « Refine my criteria », « Which companies to target? ». The conversation is kept with the profile and comes back when you reopen the page; « Clear », at the top of the page, deletes all its messages, while the profile and its CV stay.

On the right, the profile's fiche: whether it is active or archived, « Search for offers », its search criteria, which you change in place, and its ATS Score, then three groups you fold with a click on their title, « Sections », « Activity » and « Results ». A profile's results are the versions of its Master CV, newest first, and its download as PDF or Word. « Fiche », at the top of the page, opens or closes it.

A section (Master CV, ATS Score, Target companies, Download, Manage), a version of the Master CV, its history or the full CV editor opens beside the conversation, without hiding it: you edit, restore or download there as before. « Close » or the Escape key closes it. Each section keeps its address.
