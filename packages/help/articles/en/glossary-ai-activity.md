AI activity shows what AI, the coach and you did on the open page. The « Activity » button, at the top of the page, opens it and closes it again: in the Fiche's « Activity » group on an object's page (a profile, a job search, an application…), in the Activity column on the home page and the menus. Escape, on the button, closes it too. From top to bottom: the conversation's context, « Right now », then the history.

While the activity is closed, the button says on its left what AI did on the page, for example 3 models · 10.9k tokens, or the step under way, with the stop icon that interrupts it. A click on those words opens the activity, as the button does, which a screen reader announces as « AI activity », followed by those words. On a phone, the activity opens full screen, over the conversation: « Close », or Escape, closes it and brings you back to where you opened it, the « Activity » icon or « … » (see below).

« Right now », at the top of the activity, just under the conversation's context, shows the AI actions under way: this page's first, then « Running elsewhere », with a link to their page. A job search running while the coach answers you shows there beside its reply. For each: its glyph, the step under way, the number of steps done and the time so far; for a coach reply, each tool it looks at, ticked once done. The stop icon interrupts it: what was finished is kept. A finished action leaves « Right now »; with nothing under way, it says « Nothing is running on this page. ».

Each step has its glyph, which moves while it runs:

- « Thinking » and « Checking »: the brain; « Writing »: the feather; « Thinking, then writing »: one, then the other;
- « Searching »: the magnifier; « Searching the web »: the magnifier, the globe, then the book; « Reading »: the book turning its pages;
- « Computing »: the ticks appearing one by one, for Jobbbox's rules, without AI;
- « Looking at your data » and « Preparing a proposal »: the toolbox opening, a key coming out when the coach reads your applications, your agenda or your profile, a small card when it prepares a proposal for you to accept, without AI or a model; the tool (Applications, Agenda…) is named in the reply's detail.

If you asked your device for less motion, each glyph stays still.

A stopped or failed action stays in « Right now », in light red, and says so in words, with its reason when known.

Under « Right now », the « History » lists what happened on the page, newest first, by day (« Today », « On … », such as 27/10): the coach's replies, job searches, offers read, versions of the Master CV, ATS Scores, the coach's proposals you applied… Each row gives its glyph, its title, its result, its cost in short and its time. Open it for the detail: your question (« You: … »), the steps, what the coach read (« Read: »), the tools and models, its proposals and their state, what an applied proposal led to (« → led to »), the full cost, and « ↳ follows » when it comes from another row: a coach reply, then the CV version its proposal created, then the ATS Score computed on that version. A link leads to the object (« Open the version », « Open the search »…), and « See in the conversation » scrolls the conversation to the message and highlights it a moment (on a phone, the activity closes first). In the conversation, « In Activity », under a coach reply or a proposal, opens the activity on its row.

The cost is never given in euros:

- what counts in your plan says so, « 1 search from your plan » for example, with the week's use (4 of 7 this week); what has no quota is at most « counted in your consumption ». Both lead to « Your consumption » on My subscription;
- for a coach reply, its calls and the tokens sent (for example 4 calls · 21.4k tokens sent), all its AI calls added up. Tokens are a technical measure, never counted against your quotas. An older reply kept its last call only: it says so (« last call only »);
- what Jobbbox's rules, or you, do without AI says « No AI ».

At the very bottom, « This page's sources and tools », folded, lists each site read and each tool used once. AI activity never shows an internal setting. « Learn more » leads to the « AI in Jobbbox » section of the privacy policy.

On a phone, the top bar keeps icons only. On the home page and the menus, « Activity » is the icon shown; on an object's page, « Fiche » is, and « Activity » is under « … », at the top right. While an AI action runs, « Activity » takes the shown icon's place, with its stop icon, then goes back under « … » once it ends.
