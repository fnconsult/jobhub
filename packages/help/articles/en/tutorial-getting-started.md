A profile is a job you are aiming for: a master CV and search criteria. Your coach uses it to look for offers and prepare your applications.

1. Click « Sign in », at the top of every public page. On « Sign in to Jobbbox », enter « Your email address » then click « Send me a sign-in link ».
2. Open the email « Your Jobbbox sign-in link » and click « Sign in to Jobbbox », within 15 minutes. Your account is created, on the Free Plan.
3. If Jobbbox asks you to accept the Terms of use, read them, tick the box then click « Accept and continue ».
4. In My account, under Profiles, click « Create my first profile ».
5. Your coach welcomes you in the conversation. If you have a CV, drop it in the « I have a CV » card (PDF or Word .docx, 10 MB maximum) then click « Read my CV ». Otherwise, click « Answer my coach's questions », above the message box, and answer in that box: one question at a time, about ten minutes.
6. In the pane that opens beside the conversation, check your CV and search criteria, such as the « Target role » and the « Location », correct what needs it, then click « Save my profile ».

Your first profile is ready: your coach can look for offers for you. In « Activity », at the top of the page, the « Profile creation » row retraces these steps: the reading of your CV, the only step done by AI, the « Onboarding questionnaire », « No AI », the « Review » and « Profile created », with version 1 of your Master CV.
