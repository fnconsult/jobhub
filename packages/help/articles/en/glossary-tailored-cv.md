title: Tailored CV
synonyms: [custom cv, adapted cv, tailor, targeted cv]
---
A copy of your Master CV adapted to one job offer: your coach rephrases, reorders, cuts and highlights what it says, and never invents anything.

When the job offer asks for something your CV does not show, it asks you; it is added only if you confirm. You check the proposal before saving it.

It is prepared in the application's « Documents » section, with the Match Score before and after. It is written wholly in the « Document language »: when your Master CV is in another language, your coach translates it, your jobs' titles and periods included, changing no fact and no figure. Your Master CV itself stays in its language.
