kind: glossary
title: Follow-up
synonyms: [follow up, chase, no answer, no reply, reminder email]
---
An email your coach drafts when an application has stayed « Applied » or « Followed up » too long without news. It appears as a proposal in the application's page.

Jobbbox sends nothing to the employer: copy the text, send it from your own mailbox, then mark it as sent. The application then moves to « Followed up ».

The delay before a follow-up is set in My account, under Follow-ups.
