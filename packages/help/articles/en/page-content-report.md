This form is available without an account from “Report this Coach page” or “Report this offer”. It identifies the content concerned; explain precisely where the problem is and why you are reporting it. Your good-faith declaration is required. Removal is never automatic: a person reviews the evidence.

Your name and email are required except for “Child abuse”. If you provide an address, a receipt and the reasoned decision are sent there. Without an address, keep the displayed reference; you will not receive those messages. Do not include unnecessary sensitive data.

The content owner receives the decision and its reason, without your contact details or original explanation. Reply to the decision message to ask for clarification or contest the decision with the team.
