This Job Digest gathers the new job offers found for a profile, ranked by Match Score.

Open a job offer to read it, then save it to follow it in your applications. A Job Digest never counts against a quota.

With the new layout, a Job Digest's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has its job offers and their Match Scores in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Which offers to pick? », « Refine my criteria ».

On the right, the Job Digest's fiche: its profile, the day it was sent and how many job offers it holds. In « Results », each job offer with its Match Score; in « Sections », « Job offers found » lists them all and « Settings » opens how often this profile's Job Digest comes, which you change in place, with the latest ones sent. A job offer or the settings open beside the conversation, without hiding it. « Close » or the Escape key closes them.
