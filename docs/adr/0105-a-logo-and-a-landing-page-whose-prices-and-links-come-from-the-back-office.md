# A logo, « La boîte qui répond », and a landing page whose prices and links come from the Back Office

ADR-0053 left the logo out of scope: the name « Jobbbox » in bold text stood in for it on every surface. A Visitor at `/` read a title, the tagline and « L'application est en cours de construction ». Decided with the product owner on 2026-11-12, after five rounds of prototypes (three logo directions, then the box in volume, then two page directions for two audiences).

- **The logo is « La boîte qui répond »**: a letterbox in the shape of a speech bubble. The offer goes into the slot; the three dots are the coach answering, and the three b's of the name. Beside it, the name « Jobbbox » stays in text, in Inter 700. This amends ADR-0053's « A logo is out of scope ».
- **Its colours are the brand's, the same in both themes**: an indigo close to the accent that reads on the light and on the dark background. They are written in the symbol (`design/Logo.tsx`, `app/icon.svg`), not in a stylesheet: the Design System's stylesheets still take every colour from the tokens.
- **It moves once, where it opens a page**: in the public header the offer slides into the slot, the bubble is pressed, the dots lift one after the other, in under two seconds. Everywhere else it is still, and nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`: each part's own style is its resting state, as the Micro-animations do (ADR-0066).
- **This change wires it into the public shell and the favicons**: the header and footer of the public pages, `icon.svg` (a flat version: no shadow, no dots, which do not survive 16 px) and the Apple touch icon. The Candidate's Sidebar, the sign-in stage, the error pages, the emails, the extension and the app's store icons keep the name in text until each is redrawn on its own.
- **The landing page is the Visitor's home page**, at `/` and `/en`, in the public shell. One idea, the product under it, one action (« Créer mon compte », which leads to the sign-in page). Then the method in four steps as a story read by scrolling: on a wide screen the steps scroll while the product's screen stays in view and changes with the step being read; on a phone and a tablet each step is followed by its screen. Then the coach's own first message, the extension and the app, the Human Coaches, the Plans, the questions, and the same action.
- **It is written for an experienced reader**: formal and welcoming, vouvoiement, complete sentences, each one saying something the product does. No figures, testimonials, customer logos or press quotes, and no company identity: the legal links are the footer's.
- **Its pictures of the product are redrawn, not captured**: the screens are built with the Design System's components and example content, each marked « Exemple », and read out as one image with its description. Real captures taken by Playwright need a seeded account and a capture step in CI; they can replace the redrawn screens later without changing the page.
- **What changes without a release comes from the Back Office, and what is not set is not shown**: the Plans, their names, prices and what they include are read as Mon abonnement reads them (the Plans catalogue and Stripe's prices); the extension's link is `EXTENSION_STORE_URL`; the app's are two new Platform Settings, `APP_STORE_URL` and `PLAY_STORE_URL`, on Paramètres' « Extension » tab. A Plan without a price shows none; a store without a page has no link; when the Plans cannot be read the section is left out. Nothing is hard-coded in their place.
- **Its stylesheet follows the component layer's rules**: every colour and font size from a token. The page's own type scale is named as tokens on the page (`--font-size-landing-…`, in rem), so the Text Size still scales it.
- **A second audience comes next**: the Human Coaches' path (« Devenir coach sur Jobbbox ») has the same design at its own address and stays behind a switch in Paramètres, off until the coach space's epic is active. It is not in this change.

## Considered Options

- **The box in volume, with its lid and the offer inside**: the product owner's first choice, redrawn twice. It says « a box that receives your offers » but nothing of the coach, and it does not survive 16 px.
- **A wordmark alone, the three b's in the accent**: the favicon could keep only one b, and the name is written with a capital J.
- **Theme-dependent logo colours from the tokens**: the dark theme's on-accent is near black, so the offer's sheet would turn dark; a brand mark that changes colour with the theme is also harder to reuse in an email or a store.
- **An editorial page, text only with a left rail**: calmer, but it showed too little of the product.
- **Tabs for the four steps**: one more thing to operate; the story is read by scrolling, which the reader already does.
- **Placeholders for unset prices and links (« bientôt »)**: a page that promises what is not there; showing nothing is always true.
