title: Conversation context
synonyms: [context, conversation context, tokens, context bar, the coach forgets, messages read]
---
The conversation context is what your AI coach reads again before each reply: the conversation's latest messages and what it knows of you and of the page. « Activity », at the top of the page, shows it in a bar: the conversation's number of messages out of the 40 the coach reads, and the tokens of its context out of the 12k ceiling. A token is a small piece of text, about four characters; it is not your quota.

These figures come from Jobbbox: they are those of the context sent to the coach for its latest reply. From 80 %, the bar tells you that the coach reads only the latest messages and offers « Clear the conversation »: once you confirm, every message of the conversation is deleted, and the coach's memory stays.
