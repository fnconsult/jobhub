Without an account, the Jobbbox extension compares your CV with a job offer and computes its Match Score.

1. Open a job offer's page, then click « Analyse this offer », the button the extension adds to the page. If it does not show, open the Jobbbox extension from your browser's toolbar: if it shows « Job offer found on this page. », click its « Analyse this offer » button; otherwise, click « Capture this page ».
2. The extension's panel opens on the right and shows the captured job offer.
3. Drop your CV in « Drop your CV here », or click « Choose a file » to pick it.
4. Click « Compute my Match Score », at the foot of the panel.
5. The score shows out of 100, with the « Match Score breakdown »: for each criterion, what matches and what does not.
6. To compare another CV, click « Use another CV ». To delete everything, click « Delete my data now ».
