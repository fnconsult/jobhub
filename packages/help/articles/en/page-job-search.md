A job search's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the job offers found and their Match Scores with the profile in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Which offers to pick? », « Widen my criteria », « Search again ».

On the right, the search's fiche: its status, the day it started and the profile's search criteria, which « Change the criteria » opens, then « Search again », or « Stop the search » while it is searching. In « Results », each job offer found with its Match Score; « Job offers found », in « Sections », lists them all. A job offer you already saved carries the label « Saved »; a job offer you set aside in « Offers to handle » carries the label « Set aside »: it can still be read, and « Go back on this choice », in « Set aside », puts it back to handle.

A job offer opens beside the conversation, without hiding it, with its score, what explains it, and « Save as an application » to follow it in your applications. « Close » or the Escape key closes it.

The offers you receive by email for this profile are not among a search's results: they are in « Received by email », on the Job searches page.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. « Fiche » opens the fiche over it, full screen, and so does a job offer: « Close » closes them and brings you back to the conversation.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), a search's page shows its fiche above its conversation: its status, the day it started and the profile's search criteria, to read; « Change the criteria » opens the profile's page of jobbbox.ai in the app. Once the search has ended, « Search again » starts a new one for the same profile, within your plan's quota: when it is used, the app says so as jobbbox.ai does, with nothing to buy. Its job offers follow, closest to your master CV first, each marked « Followed » or « Set aside » when it is. Tap one to open it: its Match Score against the profile, a button explaining the score detailing each criterion, its summary and its text. « Follow » creates its application with this profile; once followed, « See the application » opens it. « Set aside » takes it out of your « Offers to handle », with a reason if you wish, and « Undo » goes back on it; a job offer set aside keeps « Go back on this choice ».
