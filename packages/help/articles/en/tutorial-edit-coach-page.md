title: Edit your public profile
synonyms: [coach page, change photo, update presentation]
---
1. Open “Public profile” in your Coach space. Your agreement must be up to date.
2. Complete “About you”, “Specialties” and “Languages”. Separate specialties and languages with commas.
3. Choose a “Professional photo” if you want one. Review “Candidate preview”; the portrait will be square.
4. Choose “Send for approval”. The status becomes “Awaiting approval”. Candidates keep seeing your previous version.
5. After refusal, read “Reason for refusal”, correct and submit again. Once approved, the new version appears under “My human coaches”, on the site and in the app.
