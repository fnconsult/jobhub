1. Open “Availability” in your Coach space, then “Set my availability in Cal.com”.
2. Set working hours in your time zone and connect your calendar in Cal.com. These permissions remain at Cal.com; Jobbbox collects no Google, Microsoft or iCloud token.
3. Check your team event preview in Jobbbox. This preview does not create a Jobbbox session. Candidates book after payment using a token for their own session.

The Administrator attaches a single-host team event matching your offer’s duration. Both built-in reminders and all team reminder workflows must be disabled: Jobbbox sends its own reminders. Review any old cal.eu link with the Administrator. Without configuration or the required Legal Texts, preview and embedded booking remain closed.
