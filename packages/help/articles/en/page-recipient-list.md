« Data recipients », on the Legal page, names each service that receives personal data, or queries drawn from it, to run Jobbbox: the host, the help videos' host, the AI models, web search, payments, emails and sign-in. It opens from the privacy policy, without an account.

For each one, it says « What it is for », « What it receives » (for example your texts after Redaction, or queries only), the « Contracting entity », « Where it processes data » and the « Transfer safeguard » when the data leaves the EU. A service whose place of processing is not established is marked « Counted as outside the EU ». To get a copy of a transfer's safeguards, the page's « Contact us » link leads to the Jobbbox team.

The list is drawn up at each visit from the services switched on: a service switched off is not on it. « Last changed on » gives the day it last changed. The link « AI in Jobbbox, in the privacy policy » leads to the section that explains where each use of AI goes.
