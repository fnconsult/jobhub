title: What do « Rescore » and « Why …? » do?
synonyms: [rescore, recalculate, new calculation, why this score, why, score breakdown, match score breakdown, match score, score does not change, plan]
---
The Match Score compares a job offer with your CV and your search criteria, on five criteria: « Skills », « Experience », « Location », « Salary » and « Contract type ». It is out of 100.

« Why …? », beside a job offer's score in Jobbbox, opens the breakdown: one line per criterion, with what matches and what does not. In the extension's panel, the breakdown shows under the score, in « Match Score breakdown ». A criterion the job offer says nothing about does not count: the score is then partial, or « not computable » if no criterion could be compared.

« Rescore », in the extension's panel, works out the job offer's score again. Signed in, a new calculation uses one Match Score of your weekly plan: the extension asks you first, and you confirm with « Confirm the recalculation ». The result only changes if the job offer or your profile has changed; the panel tells you whether it is unchanged, or what the score was before.

Rescore, for example, after updating your master CV, or when the panel tells you to because the job offer's text could not be read.
