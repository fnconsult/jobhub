Your coach-space session page brings together the date, relevant Candidate, preparation and report. You can access only your active sessions. Shared reading and the join link follow their time windows and update while the page stays open.

When the new conditions are activated, “Cancel the session with a reason” requires a reason before confirmation. The Candidate receives the follow-up and can choose a full refund, continuing with you without another payment, or another approved coach at the same or a lower price. Without a choice within seven days, a full refund is requested. Also confirm the reason in Jobbbox for a cancellation made only at Cal.com.

Your preparation and draft report are not published in the Candidate’s conversation. Publishing a report is an explicit action; a published correction remains a new message. Avoid copying preparation or private notes into the reason shared with the Candidate.
