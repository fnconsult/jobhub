Email intake turns into Job Offers the offer emails Candidates forward to their Forwarding address: job-board alerts, a careers site's alerts, a recruiter's message. Only emails from a Recognised sender are read, by an AI hosted in the EU, after the « Booby-trapped email content » Guardrail.

Activity's « Received emails » tab lists each offer email received: « Received », the « Sender's domain », the kind (« Alert », « Recruiter » or « Other »), the « Offers new / already stored », the « Guardrail » verdict and the outcome. That outcome can be « Offers found », « Unrecognised sender », « Set aside by a Guardrail », « Daily cap reached », « Forwarding confirmation », « Awaiting reading », « Other » or « Failed ». Filter with « Outcome », « Kind » and « Period », then click « Show ». No Candidate name, email or id here, and nothing of the emails' content.

The settings and the sender lists are in Paramètres, « Sources » tab, on the sheet of MTA Jobbbox (Haraka). « Recognised senders » lists the platform's, recognised by their domain, or by their full address when the domain sends for many companies. Each has a label; « Disable … » keeps it listed without reading it, « Remove … » takes it off. « Proposed senders » lists those Candidates added for themselves alone: how many Candidates, never which. Accepting one adds it for everyone.

Super-admins, Technique and Support read « Received emails ». Super-admins and Technique change the senders; each change is recorded in Administrator actions, section « Recognised senders (Email intake) ».
