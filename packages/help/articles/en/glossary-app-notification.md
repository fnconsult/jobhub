An app notification (a push notification) is a short message the Jobbbox app shows on the lock screen of your phone or tablet when an event concerns you: new results for a job search, a Job Digest, an appointment tomorrow, a review from your coach, a follow-up due.

Its text never names a company, a job or a person, since the lock screen can be seen: « New results for a search ». Tapping it opens the app on what it announces, where the details show.

The app notifies you only once you have allowed it to. In My account, « Reminders » section, « App notifications » has one switch per event; you can also turn all notifications off in your device's settings. Reminder emails are still sent as you set them.
