title: What is coach access, and what happens when a candidate withdraws it?
synonyms: [coach access, access, consent, candidate's consent, withdraw access, access withdrawn, revoke access, candidate gone, I no longer see the candidate, read only, candidate's file, page not found]
---
Coach access is a candidate's explicit consent: it lets you read their profiles and applications, and review their application documents. The candidate gives it, from their « My human coaches » page, with the « Give … access » button; you cannot ask for it from your coach space. With coach access, you read without being able to change anything.

The candidates who gave you access show in « Candidates with coach access ». If nobody has yet, the page says so: « No candidate has given you access yet. »

The candidate can withdraw access at any time, with « Withdraw …'s access ». It takes effect at once: the candidate leaves « Candidates with coach access », and their file and applications show « Page not found ». If you were writing a review at that moment, it is not sent and you go back to « My coach space ».

Withdrawing access deletes nothing you have already done. The candidate keeps the reviews you sent them. The sessions they paid for with you stay in « My coaching sessions », and you can still enter their date and link: they do not depend on coach access.
