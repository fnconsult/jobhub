The Jobbbox Chrome extension saves into Jobbbox the job offer you are reading on a job site, and works out its match score, without copying and pasting. It installs in the Google Chrome browser, on a computer.

To install it, signed in: in My account, under Tools, click « Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store », then add it to Chrome from the page that opens. Without an account, « Extension », at the foot of the public pages, leads to the same page. Until it is published, Tools shows « The extension is coming soon to the Chrome Web Store. »

Once installed, pin it to Chrome's toolbar to find it again. On a job offer's page, it adds the « Analyse this offer » button; you can also open it and click « Capture this page ».

Without an account, the extension works out a job offer's Match Score with your CV, then deletes everything within 24 hours. To keep the offer in your applications, sign in: « Sign in », in the extension. The rest is explained step by step in the tutorial « Save a job offer with the extension ».
