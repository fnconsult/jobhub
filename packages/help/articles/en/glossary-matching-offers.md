title: Job offers that fit you
synonyms: [job offers that fit you, for you, matching offers, counter, narrowed search, narrowed with]
---
The number of job offers found by a search whose Match Score with your profile reaches 60 out of 100. It is the fourth counter of the « Searching for your job offers » card, while you create a profile; on a phone, it shortens to « for you ».

It is worked out on the page, by Jobbbox's rules, without AI: with your Master CV and your search criteria as they stand at that moment. With each criterion you add (a contract type, a minimum salary…), it is worked out again on the job offers already found, without a new search: it can go up as well as down. The « Narrowed with: » line says with which criteria; a criterion your coach assumed and you have not confirmed yet is marked « assumed ».

The card's other counters, « sources consulted », « pages read » and « job offers analysed », are the ones the search measures itself; they all stay at 0 until something is measured.
