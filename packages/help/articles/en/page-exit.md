When you click a link that leads outside Jobbbox (a job offer's ad on its original site, a company's website, a press article, a source your AI coach cites, a human coach's booking or video link, the Chrome Web Store), Jobbbox first shows you the page « You are leaving Jobbbox », in a new tab.

It shows the site you are going to and its full address. Jobbbox is not responsible for that site's content, nor for what it does with your data.

After 5 seconds, it takes you there by itself. Click « Continue to » followed by the site's name to go there at once, or « Cancel » to stay on Jobbbox; « Back to the home page » takes you back to your home page.

If you open this page from an email, a message or another site, it does not go on by itself: check the address, then click « Continue to » if you recognise it.
