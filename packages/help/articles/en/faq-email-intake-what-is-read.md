title: Which emails does Jobbbox read?
synonyms: [email, mail, read my emails, reading, mailbox, inbox, attachments, privacy, personal data, third parties, recruiter, europe, eu, european union, email intake, sender]
---
Jobbbox never reads your mailbox: it has no access to it. It reads only the emails that arrive at your forwarding address, and only if they come from an alert sender it recognises (« Recognised by Jobbbox », or « Added by you ») or from one of your own addresses, those in « Your sending addresses ». The others are ignored unread.

Only the email's text is read, never its attachments or images. It is not kept once read: at most 72 hours, encrypted, if it must be read again. Only the job offers found are kept. Of an ignored email, Jobbbox keeps only the reason, the sender's domain and the date, never the content.

Each email is read by an AI model hosted in the European Union, whatever you chose in the « European AI » section of My account.

A forwarded email sometimes names other people: a recruiter, a contact, a signatory. Jobbbox keeps neither their name, nor their email address, nor their phone number: it keeps only the job offer and the links found in the email. The job offer drawn from a recruiter's message is a private job offer, which only you see.

To stop, click « Switch off » in the « Email intake » section: what then arrives at your forwarding address is discarded unread. The details are in the privacy policy, section [AI in Jobbbox](/legal/confidentialite#ia).
