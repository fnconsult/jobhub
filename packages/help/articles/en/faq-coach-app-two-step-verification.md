As a coach, you are asked for two-step verification in the Jobbbox app as on the website, after each sign-in by magic link. Enter the code from your authenticator app in « App code » and tap « Verify », or tap « Use a backup code », or « Use a passkey » if your account has one.

If your account has no factor yet, the app offers « Set up verification ». The setup opens in a page of jobbbox.ai, inside the app: scan the QR code with your app, enter the code it shows in « Code the app shows », tap « Confirm », then keep your 10 backup codes and tap « I have kept my codes ». The app then comes back by itself and opens your account. « Close » takes you back to the second step's screen if you want to do it later.

The attempt limits and the reset by the Jobbbox team are the website's. The app keeps neither your codes nor your passkey.
