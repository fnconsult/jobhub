The home page is where you start in Jobbbox. The menu leads to your applications, your agenda, your job searches and your Job Digests; « My account » is at the foot of the menu.

To begin, create a profile: your Master CV and your search criteria. Your coach uses them to search for job offers and prepare your applications.

A question? Open « Help and coach » at the top of the page.

With the new layout, the home page is a new conversation with your AI coach. Write in the message box at the foot of the page, or pick a suggestion just above it: « Search for job offers », « Add an application », « Schedule an interview », « Ask the coach ». The conversation is saved with your first message and appears under « Coach » in the menu, where you can rename or delete it. « New conversation », at the top of the menu, starts another one; « Clear », at the top of the page, deletes all its messages.
