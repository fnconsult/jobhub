Jobbbox helps you with your job search, from your CV to the interview. Here, in a few lines, is everything you can already do with it. For the details of a page, open « Help and coach » at the top of the screen.

## New
- **Sign in without a password**: get a sign-in link by email or, when the page offers it, use your Google, Apple or Microsoft account.
- **Your profiles**: a profile is a job you are aiming for. Upload your CV (PDF or Word) or answer your coach's questions, then check everything before you save. Depending on your plan, you can have several profiles.
- **Your Master CV**: edit it, go back to an earlier version, and download it as PDF or Word. The ATS Score tells you whether recruitment software will read it well; accept or ignore, one by one, the fixes your coach suggests.
- **Your search criteria**: the job, places, sectors, contract, remote work and salary you are looking for, and the companies you care about, in « Target companies ».
- **Job searches**: your coach looks for job offers that match a profile's criteria, on job sites, France Travail among them, and on employers' career sites. Follow the search as it goes, and stop it if you wish.
- **A Match Score for every offer**: it tells you how well the offer suits you, criterion by criterion (skills, experience, place, salary, contract).
- **Job Digests**: get the new offers that match a profile by email, every week or every day, depending on your plan.
- **Email intake**: forward your job alerts and recruiters' messages to your forwarding address; Jobbbox picks out the offers, which you find under « Received by email ». « Set up forwarding step by step » guides you through Gmail, Outlook.com and iCloud.
- **Following your applications**: every offer you save becomes an application, which you follow as a list or a board, from « To apply » to « Accepted ».
- **Your application documents**: your coach drafts a CV tailored to the offer and a cover letter, adding nothing your CV does not say. You check every change before you save, then download them as PDF or Word.
- **Follow-ups**: when an application gets no answer, your coach suggests a follow-up email. Jobbbox never sends anything for you: you send it from your own mailbox.
- **The company dossier**: what to know about the employer, from public sources, and, depending on your plan, contacts to write to.
- **Your interviews and appointments**: note your interviews and your sessions with a coach, see them as a list, by week or by month, add them to your calendar, and get a reminder email the day before.
- **The Chrome extension**: on a job offer's page, « Analyse this offer » works out its Match Score against your CV and saves it to your applications. Without an account, your CV and the offer stay in your browser and are erased within 24 hours; create your account to keep them.
- **Your AI Coach**: in « Help and coach », ask it anything, at any time; talking to it never counts against a quota. It always says it is an AI. Your conversations are kept, and « Coach memory » only holds what you confirm.
- **AI activity**: this button, at the top of the page, shows what the AI did for you: its steps, the sites it read and the model it used.
- **Human coaches**: book a video session with an HR professional chosen by Jobbbox. With your consent, which you can withdraw at any time, they read your profiles and applications and comment on your documents, without being able to change anything.
- **Your subscription**: a free plan and paid plans, paid on Stripe's secure page, where you change plan, cancel and find your invoices. Promotion codes are accepted. The bar at the bottom of the menu shows how much you have used this week; it renews every Monday.
- **Your data**: in My account, under My data, download all your data or delete your account. Under European AI, you can ask that whatever holds your identity details be processed by Mistral AI, a European AI.
- **Text at your size**: in My account, under Preferences, the « Text size » slider has four steps, from Small to Large. Buttons are big enough for a finger, and pages can be read with a keyboard or a screen reader. The interface is in French and in English.
- **Help, and a team that reads you**: help articles written by the team, without AI, in « Help and coach ». « Report a problem » and « Suggest an idea » write to the team; follow its replies in My account, under My feedback.
