An application's "Exchanges" section keeps the recruiter's emails about this application, received by your linked mailbox: their reply, an invitation to an interview. Of each email, it keeps the sender, the date, the subject and the text, never attachments, images or headers. It is not "Match", which details your match score with the offer.

An email is filed by itself, by rules and without AI, when it comes from the employer's domain, from one of the application's enriched contacts, or when it answers an exchange already kept. Only emails whose sender is authenticated are filed this way. When it could go on several of your applications, or the match is weak, the section shows it to you (sender, date and subject) with "Link to …?": "Link to …" files it on this application, "Ignore" sets it aside and keeps nothing. Nothing is filed without your click, and an email left waiting is forgotten after 30 days. An email about none of your applications takes the usual path of email intake: its text is not kept.

Before it is kept, each email goes through the "Booby-trapped email content" check. Exchanges are kept as long as the application exists, and deleted with it or with your account. "Delete the exchange …" deletes one, after you confirm. They are in your data export.

Your AI coach can read an exchange and propose a reply: it shows in full under its answer, with the recipient and the subject. "Copy" copies it to paste into your email; "Open in my email" opens a new message ready to send. Jobbbox sends nothing: you send it yourself. An exchange never changes the application's status; if the email invites you to an interview, your coach can propose moving the application to "Interview", and nothing changes without your click.

When Jobbbox sends a message in your name, on your click on « Send » in a card from your AI coach (a follow-up, an outreach message or a reply to an exchange), the section keeps it under « Sent from Jobbbox »: its date, its recipient and its subject. The text stays in the card in the conversation. You received its copy, and replies come straight to you.
