An application's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the application and its job offer in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Prepare the interview », « Write a follow-up », « Write the cover letter », « Why this score? ». The conversation is kept with the application and comes back when you reopen the page; « Clear », at the top of the page, deletes all its messages, while the application and its documents stay.

On the right, the application's fiche: its status and profile, which you change in place, its match, the day you saved it and the link to the job offer, then three groups you fold with a click on their title, « Sections », « Activity » and « Results ». « Fiche », at the top of the page, opens or closes it; « Activity » opens it on what AI did for this page.

Its sections: Suggestions, Documents, Match, Interviews, Coach reviews, Company dossier, Contacts and The job offer. A section or a result (Tailored CV, cover letter, outreach message, suggested follow-up) opens beside the conversation, without hiding it: you edit, download or copy there, and nothing is sent without your click. « Close » or the Escape key closes it. Each section keeps its address: a link to « Interviews » opens that section straight beside the conversation. While no interview is recorded, « Show me how » there shows how to move the application to the « Interview » status so you can add one.

Start with « Match » to see what fits and what is missing, then prepare your Tailored CV and your cover letter in « Documents ». You always check them before saving. Update the status in the fiche as you go: « Applied » once you have sent your application, « Interview » to record your interviews. If it gets no answer, your coach suggests a follow-up. When your coach proposes to change the status, the proposal shows under its reply: you apply it, edit it first or ignore it, and « Origin », under the status, says it came from its proposal. It can likewise propose to add, move or cancel an interview: once applied, the interview in the « Interviews » section carries « Origin » too.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. « Fiche » opens the fiche over it, full screen, and so does a section or a result: « Close » closes them and brings you back to the conversation.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), an application's fiche lists its documents: its tailored CV, its cover letter and its outreach message, with their language and the date they last changed. Tap a document to open it: it shows as a PDF in your phone's reader, Quick Look on iPhone and iPad, your PDF viewer on Android, and stays open beside it with « Open », « Share », « Download as PDF » and « Download as Word ». « Share » and « Download » go through the share sheet: save the file in Files, or send it by Mail or another app. The outreach message shows as text, with its subject: « Share » sends it as it is. The files you open stay in the app until you sign out, which deletes them from the device. In the app, documents are read, shared and downloaded; they are changed on jobbbox.ai.
