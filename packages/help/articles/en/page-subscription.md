This page shows your plan (Free, Standard or Premium) and what it includes: the number of profiles, and each week the scores, job searches and enriched contacts.

« Manage my subscription » opens the secure page of Stripe, our payment provider: that is where you change plan or cancel. Jobbbox never sees your card number.

For 14 days after you subscribe, « Renoncer au contrat ici » (withdraw from the contract here) lets you withdraw, without a reason and at no cost: your subscription is cancelled at once, you are back on the Free plan and what you paid is refunded in full. Changing plan in Stripe does not start this period again.
