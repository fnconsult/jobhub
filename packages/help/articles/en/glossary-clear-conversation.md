---
kind: glossary
title: Clear the conversation
synonyms: [clear, empty the conversation, delete the messages, start the conversation over]
---
« Clear », at the top of a page that is a conversation with your AI coach, deletes all the messages of that conversation. Jobbbox asks you to confirm first; « Cancel » deletes nothing. While the coach is replying, « Clear » waits: stop its reply first with « Stop ».

The coach's memory stays: what you asked it to remember is not cleared. The page becomes a new conversation again.
