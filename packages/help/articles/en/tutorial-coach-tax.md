When tax collection is activated:

1. In **Income → Tax information**, choose the year and “Show this year”. Distinguish “Individual (including sole trader)” from “Legal entity” and complete the applicable fields.
2. Check the professional identity, address and payout account, then press “Save my tax information”. Stripe onboarding can continue only with a supported, current identity.

You can later find your annual copy from **Income** when the operator has prepared it. It contains only your authorized business data. Contact the team before continuing if your circumstances are outside the supported French scope.
