title: How do I follow up with a recruiter?
synonyms: [follow-up, follow up, chase, no answer, no reply, contact again, email]
---
When an application stays « Applied » too long, your coach proposes a follow-up email in the application's page. You can also ask it to draft one.

Jobbbox sends nothing on your behalf: copy the text, send it from your own mailbox, then mark it as sent. The application moves to « Followed up ».

The delay before the proposal is set in My account, under Follow-ups.
