Two-step verification protects your account if someone gets into your mailbox: after every sign-in by magic link, Jobbbox also asks for a code from your authenticator app. You need an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.

1. In My account, open the « Security » section, then click « Turn on ».
2. In your authenticator app, add an account by scanning the QR code. If you cannot scan it, enter the key shown under the QR code.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows in « Code the app shows », then click « Confirm ».
4. Your 10 backup codes are shown, once. Click « Copy » or « Download », and keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone.
5. Click « I have kept my codes ». The section says « On since … », and an email confirms it.
6. Next time you sign in by magic link, enter your app's code in « App code », then click « Verify ». Without your phone, click « Use a backup code » and enter one of your codes: each one works only once.

After five wrong codes, you have to wait a few minutes before trying again. To stop, click « Turn off » in the « Security » section; if your last verification is more than 10 minutes old, you are asked for a code first.
