kind: glossary
title: Coach memory
synonyms: [memory, coach memory, remember, what the coach remembers, forget]
---
The coach's memory is the key facts your AI coach remembers about you from one conversation to the next: your goals, your constraints, your decisions. Nothing goes into it without your consent: in a conversation, « What should my coach remember from this conversation? » has it suggest items, and only those you confirm with « Remember » are kept. It holds 30 items at most.

« Coach memory » is under « Coach » in the menu: it opens beside the conversation, where you can edit or delete each item. « Close » or the Escape key closes it.

Clearing a conversation leaves the coach's memory untouched. It is deleted with your account.
