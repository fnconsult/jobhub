Signed in, the Jobbbox extension reads, at your request and in your browser, a LinkedIn page you opened: a job offer, a company's page or its people, filtered by department. Of each person, Jobbbox keeps only their name, role and company, never their profile. Jobbbox never goes to LinkedIn itself, and nothing is read without your click.

1. First add the company to Jobbbox: an application it is the employer of, or a target company of one of your profiles. With neither, nobody is kept.
2. Sign in to Jobbbox in your browser, then open on LinkedIn the job offer, the company's page or its people tab, filtered for instance on the department you are interested in.
3. Open the Jobbbox extension from your browser's toolbar. Under « LinkedIn page », click « Read this LinkedIn page ».
4. The panel says how many people were kept and where: in that company's application or target company. On your application, they show in « Contacts », under « Read on LinkedIn », with the chip « LinkedIn ». On a target company, they show on its card, in the profile's « Target companies » section, with the same chip, and go with the spontaneous application you prepare there, in its « Contacts ».
5. Then ask your AI coach who to contact: it reads the page the extension has just read, inventing nothing.

A LinkedIn profile is never read: on a person's page, the extension does not offer to read it. Your LinkedIn terms of use apply to your LinkedIn account: you are the one who opens the page and asks for it to be read.
