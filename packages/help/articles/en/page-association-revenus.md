This page groups your Association’s business earnings. It gives access to no Candidate documents. Session, Commission and payout information appears when available.

“Set up my Stripe account” opens the Association’s onboarding. Stripe collects identity documents and bank details directly. The required Legal Texts must be in effect and your contract accepted. “Complete my Stripe information” resumes requested information; “Open Stripe” opens the Association’s secure dashboard. Only its authorized members can use these actions.
