# Masterclasses are sold per unit, on the web only, never included in a Plan

Status: accepted for the next phase (map #841; #848, #849, #850, #858)

Human Coaches and Associations will publish Masterclasses (live, replay, a short series) and Association events. Both stores require in-app purchase for live classes to many and for replays (App Store 3.1.1, 3.1.3(d); Google Play Billing), and a Plan that includes them would be a subscription unlocking content.

- **Published by a validated Human Coach or Association**, reviewed by an Administrator before publication, removed with a stated reason; anyone, signed in or not, can report one (DSA art. 16, 17). A written licence per Masterclass. A replay records only the speaker and their screen.
- **Sold per unit by its Seller** (ADR-0095), with the Plan Feature discount like a Coaching Session; free is possible; no Coupon; not for Guests. **Never included in a Plan**: taking it out later would be a Quota Reduction.
- **The Order** adds, for a replay, a second box never ticked for the Candidate on losing the right of withdrawal for digital content (C. conso. L221-28 13°), beside the Immediate Performance Request.
- **Web only**: no catalogue, price, link or player in the Jobbbox app.
- **The catalogue is a Sidebar menu of the Candidate's, a conversation like the others** (ADR-0070); the AI Coach may recommend one from the Profile, never buy it. No reviews.
- **Live classes need integrated video** (ADR-0097's next phase), which ships before or with them; replays on an EU-hostable video service with captions.
