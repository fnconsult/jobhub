Signed in, the Jobbbox extension works out a job offer's Match Score with one of your profiles, then saves it to your applications, without copying it out.

1. Sign in to Jobbbox in your browser, then open a job offer's page. The extension's panel shows « Signed in as … ».
2. Click « Analyse this offer », the button the extension adds to the page. If it does not show, open the Jobbbox extension from your browser's toolbar: if it shows « Job offer found on this page. », click its « Analyse this offer » button; otherwise, click « Capture this page ». If the Jobbbox icon is not in the toolbar, click the extensions icon, the jigsaw piece, then the pin beside Jobbbox. {{voir-comment:extension-side-panel}}
3. The extension's panel shows the offer and its Match Score, worked out with one of your profiles.
4. To compare the offer with another of your profiles, choose it in « Profile compared with this job offer »: the score is worked out again with it.
5. At the foot of the panel, choose the profile for this application in « Profile used for this application ». If you uploaded a CV in the panel, « A new profile, from this CV » makes it a new profile.
6. Click « Save as an application ». The offer joins your applications, as “To apply”; « See my application » opens it in Jobbbox.
