A profile is a role you are aiming for: a Master CV and search criteria. You can have several, depending on your plan.

The page is a conversation with your coach, like every other: it opens on its welcome panel (where it says it is an AI and what the page does; « Don't show again » closes it for good), and the message box, at the bottom, carries the « AI Coach » badge, which opens that panel again, and the « Send to the coach » button. If you have a CV, drop it in the « I have a CV » card: your coach reads it and proposes a Master CV. If not, click « Answer my coach's questions », above the message box: it asks about your career, one question at a time, and you answer in the message box; allow about ten minutes.

Either way, you check and correct everything in the pane that opens beside the conversation, then click « Save my profile ». Closed, the pane opens again with « Review and save », with nothing lost. Nothing is added that you did not say.

When you answer the questions, your profile is created as soon as you have given the role you are looking for and your city: it appears among your profiles and counts in your plan. If your plan has no room left, your coach tells you so in the conversation and shows you the plan that includes more. Your next answers stay on the page until « Save my profile », which completes that same profile: its search criteria, then a single new version of its Master CV. If you leave the page before, the profile stays, with what was already saved; its page says « Creation to finish », and « Resume creating it » brings you back here, to the review, to complete and save it.

As soon as your profile is created, your coach starts a first job search for it, on us: it does not count against your weekly job searches quota, even on the Free plan, nor in « Your consumption ». It tells you so in the conversation. If you then change the role you are looking for or the city, by starting the questions again, it starts the search again once, on us too, and stops the previous one; beyond that, your searches are started from Job searches. It is a job search like any other: its page, its notification when it ends, its row in Activity. It is offered only once per profile created here.

Just below, the « Searching for your job offers » card follows that search while you go on answering. As long as the questions go on, it stays at the top of the conversation, reduced to the step under way (Step 2 of 4, for instance) and four counters: « sources consulted », « pages read », « job offers analysed » and « job offers that fit you »; on a phone, they shorten to « sources », « pages », « analysed » and « for you ». At the review, it unfolds: the sources' pictograms turn around the magnifier, each source says where it stands (« to come », « in progress… », « consulted »), and the four steps follow one another: « Reading your profile », « Searching France Travail, careers sites and the web », « Analysing the job offers found », « Picking your best job offers ». « Target companies » shows only when your profile has some.

The first three counters are the ones the search measures itself: they stay at 0 until something is measured, and nothing in them is an estimate. The fourth, « job offers that fit you », counts the job offers found whose Match Score with your profile, as you build it, reaches 60. It is worked out again on the page with each criterion you add, without a new search and without AI: it can go up as well as down. The « Narrowed with: » line says with which criteria; a criterion your coach assumed and you have not confirmed yet is marked « assumed ». If the search is stopped or does not succeed, the card says so, with what it found, which is kept. « See the search » opens its page. The animations stop if your device asks for reduced motion, and a screen reader hears only the search starting, each criterion that narrows it and its end, not every counter.

« Activity », at the top of the page, shows what is happening. While your coach reads your CV, « Right now » shows each step of the reading, which you can stop. The history then groups the whole creation in a single row, « Profile creation »: the CV reading, with the model that did it and its tokens, the only step done by AI; the « Onboarding questionnaire », whose questions are written by Jobbbox, « No AI », with the number of your answers; the « Review »; then « Profile created », with version 1 of your Master CV. When you answer the questions, « Profile created » shows as soon as the profile is created, before the review. The same row follows you to your new profile's page, with its first ATS Score once computed. The questionnaire and the review are not kept: once the page is reloaded, the row shows only the CV reading, version 1 and the ATS Score. The « Conversation context » stays empty, « The coach has not replied yet. », until your coach's first reply in the profile's conversation.

You can also write straight in the message box: your first message starts the questions. During the review, your coach only sends you back to it; once the profile is saved, you can talk to it about the profile on its page.

To answer the questionnaire, click « Send to the coach » or press Enter; for a yes-or-no question, click « Yes » or « No », or type it. The message box starts on one line and grows with your text, up to about eight lines; longer text scrolls inside it. Enter sends your answer and Shift+Enter adds a line. After sending, the box returns to one line.
