title: What are Jobbbox's Plans, and their quotas?
synonyms: [plan, plans, price, pricing, cost, free, paid, subscription, standard, premium, quota, limit, how much does it cost]

Jobbbox has three Plans: Free, Standard and Premium. The Free Plan needs no payment: it is your account's Plan when it is created.

Each Plan has its quota: the number of profiles you can have, and each week the number of match scores, ATS Scores, job searches and enriched contacts, as well as how often the Job Digest comes. The count starts again from zero every Monday at midnight (Paris time). Talking to your coach doesn't count towards the quota, but each Plan sets how much of your conversation the coach reads at once (« Coach memory », in messages) and the room for what it reads again before replying (« Coach context », compared with the simplest plan: ×2 reads twice as much).

Once signed in, My subscription shows your Plan and what it includes; « Choose the … Plan » moves you to another, with payment on Stripe's secure page. You can cancel at any time, and withdraw within 14 days.

Sessions with a human coach are paid separately, outside the Plans.
