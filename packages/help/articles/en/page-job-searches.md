Your coach searches for job offers that meet a profile's search criteria. Choose the profile, or all your active profiles, then start the search.

The page updates by itself while the search runs, and you can stop it. The latest job offers found are shown with their Match Score.

Each search counts once against your plan's quota. If it fails, or if you stop it before it has found a job offer, it does not count.
