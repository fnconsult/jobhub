Choose the seller and duration for each active coach, and your price in euros if your Association sells the session. “Save offer” records these choices. Jobbbox pricing follows its own terms and Premium discount. You cannot edit Commission; a third-party price stays unavailable until the seller’s payments and obligations are validated.
