Your AI coach is an artificial intelligence program from Jobbbox, not a person. It helps you through your job search: your Master CV, your criteria, your applications, your follow-ups and your interviews. It always says so: every conversation starts with « I am an AI coach, not a person », and the « AI Coach » badge is next to the message box.

It answers from what you tell it and from what the open page shows. Under each of its replies, a line says how it was produced. It changes nothing without your consent: its proposals are accepted or dismissed one by one.

Your conversations are kept 12 months after their last message, then deleted. To talk to a person, book a session with a human coach in « My human coaches ».
