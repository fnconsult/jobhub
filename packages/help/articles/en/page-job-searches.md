« Job searches » opens on « Offers to handle »: every job offer that reached you, through a search, a Job Digest, a job offer email you forwarded or a link pasted in a conversation with your coach, waits there for your decision. A job offer that arrived by several ways is listed once, with a chip for each (« Search », « Job Digest », « Email », « Coach link »), on the day it first arrived. A job offer captured with the extension or sent from the Jobbbox app by sharing enters only if you save it as an application: it then arrives already « Followed », with the chip « Extension » or « Share ». A job offer only analysed or shared, with no application, leaves nothing there.

At the top, « to handle » counts the job offers still waiting, beside the number of job offers handled, those you followed or set aside; the bar moves towards zero. The chips « All », « Search », « Job Digest », « Email », « Coach link », « Extension » and « Share » filter the list; each says how many of its job offers are to handle. This top of the page, the day and the tier stay in view while you scroll.

**On a phone.** The count and the sources are behind the filter icon, at the top right, beside the page's title: « Filter the offers to handle » opens, under the bar, « Offers to handle » with the number to handle, the progress bar and those handled, then the sources' chips. The panel opens at the top of the screen, wherever you are in the page, and dims the job offers below; touching the dimmed part or pressing Escape closes it, without moving the page. Choosing a source filters the list and closes the panel; while a source other than « All » is chosen, a dot stays on the icon. Under « Today », a line says how many job offers arrived today and how many are to handle. As you scroll, only the current tier stays in view, on one line, with the day and « Set all aside » on the right. The suggestions above the message box keep to one line that scrolls sideways.

Job offers are sorted into « Today », then « Earlier », where each line says the day it arrived; in each, by importance: « Not to miss » (Match Score of 80 and above, with a border turning slowly around it), « Worth a look » (60 to 79), « Less suited » (under 60, or not computable). The most recent come first.

Each line gives the Match Score, the title, the employer, the place, the contract, where the job offer came from, how many days it has been posted and, during its last 7 days out of 30, in how many days it leaves and the profile it relates to, or « Unsorted ». « Follow » saves it as an application, at « To apply »: it becomes « Followed ». An application created elsewhere, from a search, the extension or a proposal of your coach, makes it « Followed » too, as long as it exists. « Read the job offer » opens it alongside, with its score explained. The job offers followed today are folded under « Followed today ».

**Setting a job offer aside.** « Set aside », on each line to handle, asks you why, if you wish: « Not for me », « Salary », « Location », « Contract » or « Already applied elsewhere », or « Set aside without a reason ». The job offer leaves the count at once, counted among those handled, and a message confirms it with « Undo », which puts it back to handle. A job offer set aside is not offered to you again: no Job Digest sends it again, a job offer email that holds it counts it as already seen, and it shows « Set aside » in a search's results. For other candidates, nothing changes. At the foot of the box, « Set aside », folded, lists the job offers set aside in the last 30 days, the latest first, with their reason; « Go back on this choice » puts a job offer back to handle, at any time. A followed job offer cannot be set aside: it is to handle again if you delete its application.

**Set all aside.** In « Earlier », a tier holding at least two job offers to handle offers « Set all aside », followed by their number: the job offers of that tier you see, within the chip chosen, are set aside at once, without a reason. A message says how many, with « Undo », which puts them all back to handle. The other tiers, « Today » and the job offers the chip hides are not touched.

**Later.** « Later », on a line to handle, puts the job offer aside until the next Monday, at midnight (Paris time), with no date to pick; on a Monday, until the Monday after. It is not a decision: the job offer stays to handle and in the count. It waits, folded, under « Later » at the foot of the box, with « Later · back on Monday » and the date; you can still follow it or set it aside. That Monday, it comes back under « Today ». The message confirming « Later » offers « Undo », which puts it back at once.

**Expired without a decision.** A job offer still to handle 30 days after it was posted moves to « Expired without a decision », folded at the foot of the box, which puts it this way: « No decision 30 days after they were posted (or arrived, if the date is unknown), out of the count ». It leaves the count, but it is never set aside for you: « Set aside » is still possible there. A job offer followed or set aside keeps its choice.

At the foot of « Offers to handle », « Searches by profile » opens your searches alongside, by profile: when each was started, where it stands and how many job offers it found; « New results » marks those you have not seen yet. Click a line to open the search, which you can stop while it is searching. To start one, use the « Start a search » suggestion above the message box, one per profile if you have several.

« Received by email », at the foot too, shows the offers taken from the emails you forward to Jobbbox, by profile, newest first, with how many arrived this week. Each one says when and from which domain it was received, and its Match Score. The offers none of your profiles matches closely enough (match under 60) are in « Unclassified ». The emails that gave no offer this week are listed with their reason, for example « Unrecognised sender »: « It's a job alert » has that sender recognised for you from its next email. « Filter too broad » warns you when your mailbox forwards far more than your job alerts. As long as email intake is not switched on, « Set up forwarding step by step » guides you; it is set in My account, Email intake section. While no offer has arrived for a profile, « Show me how » shows how to forward a job offer's email.

The conversation with your AI coach follows « Offers to handle », its message box at the foot of the page: ask it which job offers to look at first.

Each search counts once against your plan's quota. If it fails, or if you stop it before it has found a job offer, it does not count.

Your coach can propose to start a search for a profile, on its search criteria, or to stop a search under way. The proposal shows under its reply with the quota it uses (for example 1 search of 5 this week); « Launch » starts it, and you follow it in its page's Activity, under « Right now », where « Stop the job search » ends it. Once done, it becomes a row of the history, with the coach reply that proposed it, the offers it found and the quota used (« 1 search from your plan », with the week's use). A search started cannot be undone: stop it. When your quota for the week is used, the proposal shows you the plan that includes more, and nothing is started. To search on other criteria, your coach first proposes to change them.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), « Job searches » also opens on « Offers to handle », with the same counts, chips, tiers and decisions: « Follow », « Set aside » and its reason, « Later », « Undo », « Set all aside » in « Earlier », then, folded at the foot, « Later », « Expired without a decision », « Set aside » and « Go back on this choice ». Tap a job offer to open it over the page: its Match Score against the profile, a button explaining the score detailing each criterion, its summary and its text, then « Follow » and « Set aside »; once followed, « See the application » opens it. In the « Your job searches by profile » card, under the conversation, each search says where it stands, how many job offers it found and, when there are some, « New results »: tap it to open it. « Start a search » starts one, within your plan's quota; nothing is bought in the app.

On a phone, the app does as the site does: the count and the sources are behind the top bar's filter icon, named by the number to handle and the source chosen, and marked with a dot while a source other than « All » is. « Filter the offers to handle » opens, under the bar, « Offers to handle », the progress bar and the sources' chips, wherever you are in the page, and dims the rest; touching the dimmed part, Android's back button or Escape on a keyboard closes it, and choosing a source filters the list and closes it. While the coach is at work, « Activity » takes the icon's place and the filter goes under « … ». Under « Today », a line says how many job offers arrived today and how many are to handle; as you scroll, only the current tier stays at the top, on one line, with the day and « Set all aside » on the right. On a tablet, with columns, the count and the chips stay at the top of the list.

**The count.** The number of job offers to handle follows you with no extra notification: in the menu, beside « Job searches »; as a badge on the Jobbbox app's icon, updated each time you open the app and with the notifications already sent; and in each Job Digest, email and page, through a line saying how many job offers you have left to handle, which leads here. It counts the job offers neither followed nor set aside, those put off with « Later » included; a job offer posted more than 30 days ago (or, when its posting date is unknown, that arrived more than 30 days ago) no longer counts. At zero, nothing shows. On iPhone and iPad, the badge only shows if you allowed Jobbbox's notifications; on Android, depending on your home screen.

In the menu, « Job searches » unfolds into your most recent searches, those with results not seen yet marked with a dot; « See all » leads to this page.
