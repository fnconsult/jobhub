A passkey lets you do the second step of two-step verification without typing a code: your device recognises you with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, its screen lock, or a security key plugged in over USB.

You add it in My account, « Security » section, with « Turn on with a passkey » or « Add a passkey », giving it a name to recognise it. After a sign-in by magic link, « Use a passkey » finishes signing in. Only your account's passkeys are accepted. Signing in by magic link is still needed: the passkey does not replace it.

Jobbbox keeps only the public part of the key, which cannot sign in for you: the secret part never leaves your device. Keep your backup codes for the day you no longer have the device.
