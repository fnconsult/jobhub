The Jobbbox extension opens in a panel on the right-hand side of your browser, beside the job offer you are reading. To open it, click the Jobbbox icon in the browser's toolbar, or the « Analyse this offer » button it adds to job offer pages. Once open, the panel stays in view when you switch tabs and follows the job offer in front of you.

At the top, « Job offer analysis » is the panel's title. Without an account, « Guest » shows beside it: you are using the extension as a guest, and nothing is kept in an account.

When the open page is a job offer, the panel shows « Job offer found on this page. » with its title. Otherwise, « Capture this page » reads the page as it shows. Until a job offer is captured, the panel says so: « No job offer captured yet. »

Once the job offer is captured, the panel shows its title, the employer and the location. Drop your CV in « Drop your CV here », or click « Choose a file », then « Compute my Match Score »: the score out of 100 shows, with the « Match Score breakdown », criterion by criterion. Your CV and the job offer stay in this browser for your guest session; the date they will be deleted is written in the panel.

Before your first CV is sent, a box asks you to accept the « terms of use for guests » and to confirm you have read the « information about my data ». It is never ticked for you, and without it your CV is not sent. Your choice stays in this browser, like your CV, and is deleted with it; once ticked, the box does not come back during the session.

At the end of the panel, the « Detect job offers on every site » box adds the « Analyse this offer » button to employers' own career sites.

The buttons stay at the foot of the panel. Before the calculation: « Compute my Match Score » and, if you already have an account, « I already have an account · Sign in ». After the calculation: « Create my account and keep this job offer », which opens Jobbbox in a new tab, to create your account or sign in; and « Delete my data now », which deletes your CV and the job offer from this browser at once.

At the very foot of the panel, « Help », « Privacy » and « Terms of use » open the Jobbbox site in a new tab: the help on the extension, the privacy policy and the terms of use.
