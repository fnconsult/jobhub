The Jobbbox extension saves the job offer you are reading to your applications, without copying it out.

1. Sign in to Jobbbox in your browser, then open a job offer's page.
2. Click « Analyse this offer », the button the extension adds to the page. If it does not show, open the Jobbbox extension from your browser's toolbar and click « Capture this page ».
3. The extension's panel shows the offer and its Match Score, worked out with one of your profiles.
4. At the foot of the panel, choose the profile for this application in « Profile used for this application ».
5. Click « Save as an application ». The offer joins your applications, as “To apply”; « See my application » opens it in Jobbbox.
