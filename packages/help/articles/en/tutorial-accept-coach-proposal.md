title: Accept a proposal from your coach
synonyms: [coach proposal, proposal, apply, edit before applying, ignore, undo, go back, card, origin, ai coach]

In a conversation, your AI coach can propose a change, for instance moving an application to another status. It never changes anything itself: the proposal shows under its reply, in a card « Proposed by your AI coach » that says what changes, before and after, with a link to the item it is about.

1. To accept the proposal as it is, click « Apply » in the card. The item is changed and the card shows « Applied by you at … ».
2. To adjust it first, click « Edit before applying »: the page's form opens beside the conversation, filled in with the proposal. Change what you want, then click « Apply ». If you close the pane before, your changes are kept: « Edit before applying » opens it again as you left it.
3. To go back, click « Undo » in the applied card: the item returns to how it was before and the card shows « Undone at … ». You can do so as long as the item has not changed since.
4. If the proposal does not suit you, click « Ignore »: nothing is changed.

If the item has changed since the proposal, « Apply » is refused: the card shows « The item has changed since the proposal » and nothing is changed. An item changed on your coach's proposal keeps that origin without showing it: open « Origin », under its status, to read « Proposed by your AI coach, applied by you on … ».
