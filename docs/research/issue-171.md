# Research for issue #171: Account data export gaps

Scope: only the external (GDPR) facts needed to decide what `GET /api/account/export` must contain. Sources fetched 2026-10-16. This is not legal advice; the repo-side mapping of data to the Candidate's tables is left to implementation.

## Findings

1. **Art. 15 (access) covers all personal data processed about the person, plus a copy.** The controller must give a copy of the personal data undergoing processing (Art. 15(3)), together with purposes, categories, recipients, retention, source of data not collected from the person, and the existence of automated decision-making/profiling with meaningful information on the logic (Art. 15(1)). Source: https://gdpr-info.eu/art-15-gdpr/ (text of Regulation (EU) 2016/679).
2. **Access includes inferred and derived data.** EDPB Guidelines 01/2022 (v2.1, 17 Apr 2023), section 4 list: data actively provided, observed data, derived data, inferred data (algorithmic results, personalisation/recommendation outputs), pseudonymised data. They state access "includes both inferred and derived data ... whereas the right to data portability only includes data provided by the data subject". Source: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2023-04/edpb_guidelines_202201_data_subject_rights_access_v2_en.pdf (paras 97-99).
   - Implication: ATS Scores, Follow-up Delays/Action Cards generated by the system, Enriched Contacts obtained from providers, and Coach Conversation content (including AI replies) are in Art. 15 scope. Excluding them from the JSON is defensible only for Art. 20 portability, not for access.
3. **Art. 20 (portability) is narrower.** Only data "provided by" the person, where processing rests on consent or contract and is automated; format must be structured, commonly used and machine-readable (Art. 20(1)). Observed data (activity logs, usage) counts as provided per WP29 WP242 rev.01 as cited by EDPB; derived/inferred data does not. Sources: https://gdpr-info.eu/art-20-gdpr/ ; EDPB guidelines above, para 97 and footnotes 56-59.
   - Implication: consent records (EU AI consent #121), Coach Access grants, Job Searches, Digest settings, Master CV and profile data fit Art. 20 clearly.
4. **Format for access:** for an electronic request, a commonly used electronic format unless the person asks otherwise (Art. 15(3)). JSON is acceptable for both articles.
5. **Third-party data limit (Art. 15(4) / 20(4)):** the copy must not adversely affect others' rights and freedoms. Coach Conversations and Coach Access involve a Human Coach; coach identity/messages written by the coach need review before inclusion. Enriched Contacts are third parties' personal data; EDPB (example 16) says such details may be withheld/redacted under Art. 15(4). Source: EDPB guidelines para 97, ex. 16.
6. **Deadline:** respond without undue delay and within one month of receipt (extendable under Art. 12(3)); EDPB treats the request date as the trigger. Source: EDPB guidelines section on timing (paras around "within one month").
7. **Layered approach allowed** but not a way to omit data; "all layers should be provided". The alternative the issue offers ("or the account page says exactly what it contains") is only compatible with this if the export is complete and the page describes it; it does not justify omitting in-scope data. Source: EDPB guidelines section 5.2.3.

## Recommendation for triage (decision input, not a legal ruling)

- Must include (Art. 15 and 20): Coach Conversations, Coach Access grants, EU AI consent records, Job Searches, Job Digest settings, Follow-up Delays and Follow-ups.
- Must include for Art. 15 (system-generated/derived): ATS Scores, other Action Cards, Enriched Contacts obtained (review third-party redaction per Art. 15(4)).
- Exclude only credentials/secrets (password hashes, tokens): not meaningful personal data for the person and a security risk. This exclusion is a judgment call; the sources above do not address it explicitly.
