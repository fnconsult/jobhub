title: Which CV templates can I choose?
synonyms: [template, templates, layout, classic, modern, compact, download, pdf, word, docx, ats, recruitment software, one column]
---
Three CV templates are offered: « Classic » (serif font, centred header, underlined headings), « Modern » (sans-serif font, left-aligned header, dark blue headings) and « Compact », denser, to fit on fewer pages. All are designed to be read well by recruitment software: a single column, no image or table.

You choose the template when you download:

- your Master CV: the profile's « Download » section, choose the « CV template », then « Download as PDF » or « Download as Word (.docx) »;
- a tailored CV or a cover letter: the application's « Documents » section, under « Download this tailored CV » or « Download this cover letter ».

The template changes only the layout: the content stays the same.
