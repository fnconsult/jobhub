title: Name the coaches use
synonyms: [preferred name, chosen name, what to call me, what would you like the coaches to call you, nickname, first name, identity, ai coach, human coach]

The name the coaches use is the one you enter in My account, Identity section, under « What would you like the coaches to call you? ». The AI Coach and your coaches call you this: your first name, a short form, or whatever you prefer. Jobbbox's emails also start with « Hello » followed by this name.

It is the only part of your identity sent to the AI: your first name, your last name and the rest are removed from every request. Without it, the AI Coach calls you by no name and the emails start with « Hello, ».

It is optional, like your first name and last name. Leave it empty not to be called by a name. If your account existed before the Identity section came, it was filled in with your first name, taken from your account's name or your Master CV: change it or clear it at any time, then click « Save ».
