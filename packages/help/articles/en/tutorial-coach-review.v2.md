With coach access, you can give your review of an application document: the tailored CV, the cover letter or the outreach message. You do not change the document: the candidate reads your review and decides what to change. In this example, Claire D. gave you access and is preparing an application for the purchasing manager role at Exemple Industrie, a fictitious company.

1. Open « My coach space ». In « My Candidates », click Claire D.
2. In « Applications », click the application Responsable des achats · Exemple Industrie.
3. Read the « Application documents ». A document the candidate has not written yet shows « Not written yet. »; you can only review a document already written.
4. In « Your review », choose the « Document reviewed », for example « Cover letter ».
5. Write your review in « Your review for the candidate », for example: the second paragraph would gain from quoting a figure.
6. Click « Send the review ». The page confirms: « Review sent to the candidate. »

The candidate finds your review on their application, under « Coach reviews », with your name and the date. They keep it even if they later withdraw your coach access. On the application's page, « Your coaches' reviews » lists the reviews already sent on this application. If the candidate has not written any document yet, the page says so: « The candidate has no application documents to review yet. »
