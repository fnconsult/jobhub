title: Add or retire a Human Coach
synonyms: [add a coach, retire a coach, new coach, human coach, cal.com, booking link, sign-in email address]
---
Candidates book a Human Coach through their Cal.com link, and the coach signs in to Jobbbox with the address you give. You need the Coach manager or Super-admin role.

1. First create the coach's booking page in Cal.com and copy its https link.
2. Open Coaches (Coaching menu).
3. Under « Add a coach », enter their « Name », their « Sign-in email address », the « Cal.com booking link » and, in the old workspace, their « Introduction (optional) » if you like. In the new workspace, the coach submits their introduction from « Public profile »; approve it under « Coach profiles to review » before it becomes visible.
4. Click « Add the coach ». The page shows « Coach … added. » and they appear under « Active coaches ».
5. To retire a coach, click « Retire … » on their row. The page shows « Coach retired. Their past sessions are kept. »

An email address serves one coach at a time. Each addition and each retirement are recorded in Administrator actions.
