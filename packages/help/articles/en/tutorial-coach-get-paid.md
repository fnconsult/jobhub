Payouts are available after the required Legal Texts are approved and in effect. Your professional Seller identity must be complete and your contract accepted.

1. Open “Earnings”, complete your supported tax information, then select “Set up my Stripe account”. For an Association, use its Earnings page.
2. Complete the information requested on Stripe’s website. Stripe collects your identity documents and bank details; Jobbbox does not store these documents.
3. Return to Jobbbox. If information is still required, select “Complete my Stripe information”. You can also “Open Stripe”.

No new session enters Checkout without an active account. Jobbbox sales also require the team to configure the compensation agreement and its professional recipient.

Payouts are monthly, after each payment’s withdrawal period closes, including extensions for a weekend or public holiday. A refund before payout reduces its amount; after payout it is deducted from later earnings. Unavailable or disputed funds are withheld. Each confirmed payout has a service email pointing to official tax and social information.

If Stripe requests a correction or a payout fails, complete your account and contact Support. Jobbbox keeps its reconciliation; it does not blindly send a second transfer.
