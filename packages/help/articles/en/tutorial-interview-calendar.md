title: Add an interview to your calendar

Note your interview in Jobbbox, then add it to your phone's or your mailbox's calendar.

1. Open « Agenda » in the menu, then click « Add an interview ».
2. In « Application », choose the interview's application. If it is not at the « Interview » stage yet, leave « Move the application to Interview » ticked.
3. Enter the « Interview date and time (Paris time) » and, if you wish, the duration, the format, the « Place or link (optional) » and a note.
4. Click « Add the interview ». « Interview added. » confirms it, and it shows in your agenda.
5. On the interview, in your agenda, click « Add to my calendar »: an .ics file is downloaded.
6. Open this file: your calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar…) offers to add the interview.

You can also note an interview from an application's « Interviews » section, at the « Interview » stage, with « Add the interview ». If the date changes, edit the interview in Jobbbox, then download the file again: the one already added to your calendar does not update.
