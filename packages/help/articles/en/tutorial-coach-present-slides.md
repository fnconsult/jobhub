title: Present your slides
---
1. Open the current session and choose a PDF of up to ten megabytes and two hundred pages. Choose “Present PDF”.
2. Scroll through the PDF: the Candidate follows your reading. Either participant can stop sharing. The temporary file is deleted when the session window ends.
