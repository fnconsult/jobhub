On top of your AI coach, you can book a video session with an HR professional selected by Jobbbox. You pay first, then you pick your time.

1. In the menu, open « My human coaches ».
2. Under « Choose a coach », click « Book a session with … » for the coach of your choice.
3. On the « Your order » page, check the summary, then tick « I accept the terms of sale (version of …) ». The box asking for the service to start at once is optional.
4. Click « Go to payment », then pay on the secure page of Stripe, our payment provider. Your order confirmation is sent to you by email.
5. Once payment is confirmed, choose a time in the embedded Cal.com calendar. “Choose later” keeps the session unscheduled; find it in “My human coaches”.
6. The confirmed booking automatically dates the session and your Agenda. Rescheduling and cancellation received from Cal.com update them; history attributes the change “via Cal.com”.
7. To change a linked booking, open “Change the booking in Cal.com”. Manual entry remains a fallback for sessions without a linked booking; it cannot desynchronize an existing booking.

The integration appears only when the team event and activation conditions are ready. Otherwise, the coach’s link and manual date entry remain available as a fallback. The coach configures the joining link in Jobbbox.

Withdrawal also queues cancellation of the Cal.com booking. A Cal.com outage never blocks the refund: Jobbbox retries cancellation separately.
