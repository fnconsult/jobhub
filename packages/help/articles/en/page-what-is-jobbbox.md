Jobbbox helps you with your job search, with an AI coach. It builds your master CV and search criteria with you, looks for the job offers that suit you, then prepares your applications: a CV tailored to each offer, a cover letter. You then follow your applications, interviews and follow-ups in one place.

Jobbbox is for anyone looking for a job, whether for the first time or after years in the same post. It is made to be read without effort: no jargon, a help article for every word to know, and text you can make larger.

Your coach is an AI, and it says so. If you wish, human coaches can also support you, in sessions paid separately. The Jobbbox Chrome extension saves a job offer from the page where you read it.

This page, « Sign in to Jobbbox », is also where you create your account: no password, enter « Your email address » then click « Send me a sign-in link ». If you don't have an account yet, it is created, on the Free Plan: nothing to pay to start.

The email you receive holds a link and a 6-digit code. Reading it on another device, your phone for instance? Stay on this page: type the code in « Enter the code from the email », then click « Sign in with this code ». The link and the code are valid for 15 minutes, once: using one makes the other unusable. After 5 wrong codes, the code no longer works. As soon as a code is refused, « Send me a new email » appears below the field and stays there: click it when the code no longer works, after 5 wrong codes, after 15 minutes, if the link has already been used or if a newer email replaced it. The new email holds a new code: only the one in the latest email works.

Depending on the methods the page offers, « Continue with … » followed by Google, Apple or Microsoft signs you in with an account you already have, without waiting for an email. With Apple, you can choose to hide your email address: Apple then passes on a relay address, and your Jobbbox account is a new one, separate from the account of your usual address. If a Jobbbox account already uses that account's address, the page may ask you to sign in first with a link by email, then link this sign-in method from « My account ».

In the Jobbbox app, the « Sign in to Jobbbox » screen offers the same methods, above the link by email. « Continue with … » opens the provider's page in the phone's browser, over the app; on an iPhone, the system first asks you to let Jobbbox open it. Once you are signed in at the provider, you are back in the app, signed in. You close that page or tap « Cancel »? Nothing changes: the sign-in screen is waiting, pick another method. A sign-in with Google, Apple or Microsoft asks for no second step, even if your account has two-step verification.
