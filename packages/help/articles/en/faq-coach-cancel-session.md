title: How can I cancel a session with a Candidate?
synonyms: [coach cancel, cancellation reason, absence, refund Candidate]
requires: coach-space-v2
---
When the new conditions are activated, open the session in your coach space and choose **Cancel the session with a reason**. You can cancel at any time; a reason is mandatory and visible to the Candidate. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information in it.

The Candidate receives an email and, if allowed, a notification. They can request a full refund, continue with you without another payment, or choose another approved coach at the same or a lower price. Without a choice after seven days, a full refund is requested automatically.

A cancellation made at Cal.com needs confirmation in Jobbbox with your reason: a webhook does not identify who cancelled. Changing the date must not be used to bypass cancellation.

If a refund occurs after your payout, the correction carries into later amounts payable; your earnings statement preserves both the payment and correction. The team follows up failed refunds. Losing coach access does not remove a refund already owed.
