This page lets you withdraw from a contract made on Jobbbox, without giving a reason and at no cost: your subscription to a plan, for 14 days after you subscribed, or a session with a human coach, for 14 days after you paid for it and as long as it has not taken place.

Check the contract, its date and the amount paid, enter your name, then choose where to receive the acknowledgement: your account's address or another one. « Confirmer la rétractation » (confirm the withdrawal) ends the contract at once and refunds you in full, to the payment method you used; depending on your bank, the refund shows within 5 to 10 business days.

You get an acknowledgement by email straight away, with the date and time of your withdrawal: keep it.
