It shows the job offer: the employer, the location, the contract type, remote work and the salary when given, the skills asked for and the job offer's text.

The Match Score says how well the Master CV of one of your profiles fits the job offer. Choose the profile to use, then « Save the job offer »: it becomes an application you follow in « My applications ».
