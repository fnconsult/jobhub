kind: glossary
title: Fiche
synonyms: [fiche, application fiche, right column, sections, right panel, details]
---
The fiche is the column on the right of the conversation on the page of an application, a profile, a job search or a Job Digest: the object at a glance (for an application, its status and profile, which you change in place, its match, the job offer), then three groups, « Sections », « Activity » and « Results ». A click on a group's title folds or unfolds it.

It never hides the conversation: it pushes it aside. « Fiche », at the top of the page, opens or closes it; « Activity » opens it on what AI did for this page. A section or a result of the fiche opens beside the conversation, where you act on it.

On a phone, the fiche opens over the conversation, full screen, with its title; « Close » or the Escape key closes it.
