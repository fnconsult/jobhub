« Edit my Master CV » opens the full editor beside the profile's conversation. Add, edit, move or remove the entries of each section: your contact details, your jobs, your education, your skills and your languages. The editor says which version you are editing.

« Save as a new version » keeps your changes as the next version: earlier ones stay in the history and can be restored. « Cancel and go back to the profile » drops what you changed.

If your Master CV changed elsewhere in the meantime, for example by applying a fix your coach proposed, saving is refused: nothing is merged or silently lost. Reload the page to start again from the latest version.

From the profile's conversation, your coach can also propose to rephrase, cut, reorder or put forward what your CV already says, never a fact it does not hold. It changes nothing itself: « Review the changes » opens them one by one, « Accept » or « Reject » each, and « Save the accepted changes » makes them a single new version. A change that brings words from one of your job offers that your CV lacks waits for your explicit consent. The version created this way keeps its « Origin », shown in the version history, and « Undo » on the card restores the version before.
