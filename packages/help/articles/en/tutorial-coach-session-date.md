The candidate pays for their session on Jobbbox, then books their slot on your Cal.com page. Cal.com does not pass that slot on to Jobbbox: you enter it, or the candidate does, so that it shows in the candidate's agenda. In this example, a session paid for by Claire D. is « To schedule ».

1. Open « My coach space ». In « My coaching sessions », find Claire D.'s session.
2. Click « Enter the date ». If the session is already dated, the button reads « Change the date ». The « Session with … » panel opens, in Claire D.'s name.
3. In « Session date and time (Paris time) », enter the slot booked on Cal.com, for example 12 November at 2:30 pm. Enter it in Paris time, even if you or the candidate are in another time zone.
4. If you wish, give the « Duration in minutes (optional) », between 5 and 600 minutes, for example 60.
5. In « Video call link (optional) », paste the video call link you got when booking, starting with https://, for example https://meet.example.com/demo-session.
6. Click « Save ». The panel closes and the session shows « On … (Paris time) », its duration and the « Video link with … » link, here with Claire D.

The candidate finds the date and the link in their agenda, marked « changed by the coach ». To reschedule the session, click « Change the date » and enter the new slot. « Cancel » closes the panel without changing anything. The note the candidate may have added to the session stays theirs: you do not see it.
