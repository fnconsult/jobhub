title: Report a Coach page or offer
synonyms: [report content, DSA form]
requires: coach-space-v2

1. On the content concerned, open “Report this Coach page” or “Report this offer”. Choose the reason and explain precisely where the content is and what the problem is.
2. Enter your name and email unless the reason is “Child abuse”, then select the good-faith declaration and press “Send report”. Keep the displayed reference.

A receipt is sent to the address provided. Following human review, you receive a reasoned decision; reply if you contest it. Without email, none of these messages can be sent.
