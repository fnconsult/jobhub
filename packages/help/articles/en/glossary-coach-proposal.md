title: Coach proposal
synonyms: [proposal, proposed by your ai coach, apply, undo, origin, proposal card]
---
A change your AI coach prepares during a conversation, for instance an application's status. It shows under its reply, in a card « Proposed by your AI coach »: what changes, before and after, and the item it is about.

Your coach never changes anything itself and never deletes anything: you choose « Apply », « Edit before applying » or « Ignore ». An applied proposal can be undone as long as the item has not changed since. The item keeps that it was changed on your coach's proposal: « Origin » says so.
