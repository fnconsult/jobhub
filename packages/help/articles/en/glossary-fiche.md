kind: glossary
title: Fiche
synonyms: [fiche, application fiche, right column, sections, right panel, details]
---
The fiche is the column on the right of the conversation, with the new layout, on an application's page: the application at a glance (its status and profile, which you change in place, its match, the job offer), then three groups, « Sections », « Activity » and « Results ». A click on a group's title folds or unfolds it.

It never hides the conversation: it pushes it aside. « Fiche », at the top of the page, opens or closes it; « Activity » opens it on what AI did for this page. A section or a result of the fiche opens beside the conversation, where you act on it.
