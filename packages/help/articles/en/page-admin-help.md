The back office help gathers the pages, tutorials and frequent questions the Jobbbox team writes for Administrators. It is never public: the Help Centre (/aide), its search and the sitemap never show it, and only Administrators open it, whatever their roles.

Articles are sorted by topic: « Providers and Tools » first, then one topic per group of the back office menu, then « AI and compliance ». « Search the back office help » finds an article by its words, without AI.

Each back office section also shows its article in the assistant, under « On this page ». The assistant answers from the articles it finds and cites them. When it finds none, click « Request a tutorial » under its reply: the help editors will see the question in « Unanswered questions », without your name. Beside the « AI Assistant » badge, « Learn more » leads to the « AI in Jobbbox » section of the privacy policy: where each use of AI goes.
