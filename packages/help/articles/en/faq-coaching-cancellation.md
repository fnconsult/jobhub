title: How can I cancel or reschedule a coaching session?
synonyms: [cancel session, reschedule, session refund, absent coach, 24 hours, another coach]

When the new coaching conditions are activated, you can cancel or reschedule until 24 hours before the session starts. The limit uses the actual instant, including time-zone changes and daylight-saving transitions. Cancelling in Jobbbox requests a full refund to the original payment method. After the limit, contact the coach or support; the form cannot extend it.

A coach cancelling must provide a reason. In **My human coaches → Cancellations and refunds**, choose a full refund, another booking with the same coach without paying again, or another approved coach at the same or a lower price. Without a choice within seven days, a full refund is requested automatically.

Choosing another coach first refunds the original order. You then review and accept a new order; choosing does not trigger another payment. The price is checked again before payment. No wallet or automatic reassignment of the original order is created.

A cancellation reported only by Cal.com does not prove who requested it. The coach must confirm their reason in Jobbbox to open these choices; the Candidate’s limit still uses the original date. Contact support if you disagree.

Refund status appears in the follow-up. Stripe confirmation precedes receipt by your bank; bank processing time varies. A failed or uncertain refund remains with support: do not pay again to fix it. [Legal withdrawal](/abonnement/retractation) is a separate process.
