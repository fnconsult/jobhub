« My applications » is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one at each visit. Each job offer you save becomes an application, linked to one of your profiles.

Your applications in progress are listed there in a card: their status, their Match Score and those « to handle », when a follow-up is ready or an application still has no answer. Click a line to open the application, where you prepare your documents and record your interviews; « Show them » also shows the archived applications. Ask the coach which one to handle first, or start from a suggestion above the message box: « Add an application », « Search for job offers ».

In the menu, « My applications » unfolds into your most recent applications, and those to handle, marked with a dot; « See all » leads to this page. The arrow beside it folds or unfolds the list, and this browser remembers it.
