# Tailored CVs never invent facts

To push a Match Score higher, the AI Coach could add keywords, skills or experience that the Candidate doesn't have. We forbid this. A Tailored CV may only rephrase, reorder, cut and emphasise facts that are in the Master CV. When the Job Offer asks for something missing, the AI Coach asks the Candidate, and adds it only if they confirm it. The Candidate reviews the changes against the Master CV and approves them before the Tailored CV is saved. Senior candidates are tested hard in interviews, and an invented line on a CV costs them more than a lower score.
