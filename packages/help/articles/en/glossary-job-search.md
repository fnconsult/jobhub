title: Job search
synonyms: [search, look for job offers, find job offers]
---
A search your coach runs for a profile, following its search criteria, on job sites. The job offers found are ranked by Match Score.

You start one from the Job searches page, or by asking your coach. It counts once against your plan's quota, unless it fails or you stop it before it has found a job offer.
