title: Fiche

The fiche is the column on the right of the conversation on the page of an application, a profile, a job search or a Job Digest: the object at a glance (for an application, its status and profile, which you change in place, its match, the job offer), then three groups, « Sections », « Activity » and « Results ». A click on a group's title folds or unfolds it.

It never hides the conversation: it pushes it aside, and scrolls on its own, staying put when you scroll the conversation. « Fiche », at the top of the page, opens or closes it; « Activity » opens it on what AI did for this page. A section or a result of the fiche opens beside the conversation, where you act on it.

Too narrow for what it shows? Widen it: drag its left edge, the handle between the conversation and the fiche, to the left. It widens up to half the window, always leaving the conversation enough room. A click on the handle widens it to the maximum at once, another brings it back; with the keyboard, move to the handle with Tab, then use the left and right arrows, or Home and End. A double-click gives it back its original width. Your browser remembers the width you chose.

On a phone, « Fiche » is the icon at the top right of the page, and « Activity » is under « … »; the fiche opens over the conversation, full screen, with its title; « Close » or the Escape key closes it.
