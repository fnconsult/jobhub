---
kind: glossary
title: Tailored CV
synonyms: [custom cv, adapted cv, tailor, targeted cv]
---
A copy of your Master CV adapted to one job offer: your coach rephrases, reorders, cuts and highlights what it says, and never invents anything.

When the job offer asks for something your CV does not show, it asks you; it is added only if you confirm. You check the proposal before saving it.

It is prepared in the application's « Documents » section, with the Match Score before and after.
