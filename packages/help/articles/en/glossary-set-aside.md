title: Set aside
synonyms: [set aside, dismiss, dismissed, reason, not for me, go back on this choice, rejected, ignored]

A job offer is « Set aside » when you put it aside in « Offers to handle » with « Set aside », giving a reason if you wish: « Not for me », « Salary », « Location », « Contract » or « Already applied elsewhere ». It then counts among those handled.

A job offer set aside counts as already seen everywhere: no Job Digest offers it to you again, a job offer email that holds it counts it as already seen, and it shows « Set aside » in a search's results. The job offer itself is not deleted, and other candidates still see it.

The job offers set aside in the last 30 days are in « Set aside », at the foot of « Offers to handle », with their reason. « Go back on this choice » puts a job offer back to handle, at any time. Your decision and its reason are kept with the job offer, 12 months after it arrived, and deleted with your account or with its profile.
