# The native app is a companion on Expo, on the same API and the same session, phone and tablet

Status: accepted (grilling 2026-11-03, prototype `prototype/mobile-shell` tried on 2026-11-04)

ADR-0005 left native iOS and Android apps for later, on the same API. They start now, for Candidates and Human Coaches (no Administrator), with the Claude iOS app as the reference for how the app is built around a conversation. What the PWA cannot give decides the scope, in this order: push notifications, capture from the share sheet, native ergonomics (Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Face ID), then a presence in the stores.

- **One app, « Jobbbox », for both roles**, `ai.jobbbox.app`, on iOS and iPadOS 15.1+ and Android 7+ (API 24), phones and tablets. A Human Coach is recognised as on the web (ADR-0016) and finds « Espace coach » in the drawer.
- **A companion, not parity.** v1, native: the home page and the menus as Coach Conversations with their cards; Mes candidatures (list, an Application's conversation, its Fiche with the status and Interviews to add, reschedule or cancel); Recherches d'offres (results, keep or dismiss a Job Offer, Match Score, start a Job Search again on an existing Profile); Job Digests; Agenda as a list (« À planifier », then by day); Profiles read-only; Tailored Documents to view, share and download; Mes coachs humains (book, pay, enter the date). In a WebView: Mon compte, the subscription, the Withdrawal, the Legal Texts, setting up Two-step verification factors, editing the Master CV, and in v1 the Coach space. v1.1: the Coach space made native (its Candidates, read-only Profiles and Applications, Coach Reviews, its Coaching Sessions, its push notifications; no AI conversation for coaches), a photo of a printed job posting (a new AI task, EU only per ADR-0055) and the Agenda's week view.
- **Expo, with native screens and WebViews for the rare ones.** `apps/mobile` in the monorepo, Expo Router, the Design System's tokens read from `@jobhub/shared/design/tokens` and the translations from `@jobhub/shared/i18n`. A WebView receives the session cookie for `jobbbox.ai` only; any other address opens in the system browser.
- **The shell is variant A of the prototype**, the Claude pattern: the conversation is the page (ADR-0070) with its box at the foot and « + » for a link or a document (PDF, DOCX); a drawer holds the Sidebar's menus, « Coach » with the Free conversations, then « Espace coach » and Mon compte at its foot; a pencil starts a new conversation. **The layout follows the window width in rem** (16 pt times the system text scale) **with ADR-0070's thresholds**: below 64rem the conversation takes the screen and the Fiche, the Activité column and the Work Pane open as **Sheets** with two detents (about 55 % and 92 % of the height, a drag handle and « Agrandir », « Réduire », « Fermer » buttons so nothing needs a gesture); from 64rem they are columns beside the conversation and the Sidebar is a column when the conversation keeps 28rem. The layout is computed again whenever the window changes (rotation, Split View, Stage Manager, a foldable).
- **Tablets are first-class in v1**, with the same features as the phone: every orientation, multitasking, hardware keyboard (⌘N new conversation, ⌘↩ send, ⌘\ Sidebar, ⌘I Fiche, ⌘W closes the Work Pane, Esc closes a Sheet; Ctrl on Android), pointer hover and context menus, a document or a link dropped on the box. Phones are portrait only.
- **Reads become HTTP.** Candidate pages read in React Server Components; the app needs GETs. They are added to the existing `/api/*` routes, on the same server modules the pages call, their response types in `@jobhub/shared`; no `/api/mobile` layer. The API version check (426, `packages/shared/src/api-version.ts`) forces an outdated app to update. The Coach's reply streams as on the web (`POST /api/coach`, NDJSON).
- **Same session, held by `@better-auth/expo`** in the Keychain or Keystore and sent as the session cookie: revocation from Mon compte, the pending session of Two-step verification (ADR-0075) and the sign-in method work unchanged. Google, Apple and Microsoft sign in through the web OAuth flow in the system's authentication browser and come back by a deep link; ID-token sign-in stays refused (ADR-0011). **Sign in with Apple** ships with the iOS app (ADR-0008). The magic-link email also carries a **6-digit code**, valid as long as the link and used once, for a person who reads their email on a computer; the link opens the app by a universal link or an App Link. The second step is asked natively (a code, or a passkey through `webcredentials:jobbbox.ai`); factors are set up in the WebView.
- **Push notifications go from our worker to APNs and FCM directly**, no Expo Push service. Candidate: new Job Digest or Job Search results, the Appointment Reminder, a Coach Review left, a Coaching Session's date changed by the coach, a Follow-up due. Human Coach: Coach Access granted, a Coaching Session paid, a session's date changed or cancelled by the Candidate. Each event has its switch in Mon compte's Rappels; email stays the default where it already exists. **The text never names a company, a job or a person** (« Nouveaux résultats pour une recherche »): a lock screen is public, and the app fetches the rest once opened. Permission is asked in context (starting a Job Search, adding an Interview), after a screen explaining it, never at first launch.
- **The share sheet captures a job offer**: a URL is fetched by the server under the job discovery rules (ADR-0002); where they refuse (LinkedIn, Indeed), the person shares or pastes the text. It becomes a Private Job Offer (ADR-0049) and opens in its own conversation, « Scorer contre mon profil » suggested. The device never reads the page in a hidden WebView.
- **Payments**: no in-app purchase in v1; the app shows the Plan and says the subscription is managed on jobbbox.ai, with no price. A Coaching Session, a live one-to-one service (App Store guideline 3.1.3(d)), is paid through Stripe Checkout in the in-app browser, back by a universal link; the Withdrawal stays on its web page (ADR-0071, ADR-0074). Legal confirms both before the first submission.
- **What the stores require ships with it**: Account deletion inside the app (the existing flow, ADR-0010, ADR-0073), Sign in with Apple, the App Store privacy labels and the Play Data safety form. Launched in France, Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg and Canada; TestFlight and Play internal testing point at staging, as the Beta does.
- **Accessibility and look**: the Confort Density on the Mercury tokens, Inter and Lucide; text follows the system's Dynamic Type or font scale instead of the Text Size, tested up to the largest accessibility sizes; dark mode from the first version, following the system; FR and EN.
- **On the device**: a read cache of what was last seen (cleared at sign-out), an « Hors ligne » banner, no writes offline; an optional lock by Face ID or fingerprint after 5 minutes in the background, a setting of the device and not of the account, never a second step; « Ajouter au calendrier » opens the system's new-event sheet already filled (write-only access), the Calendar file as fallback (ADR-0029); crash reports in an EU region with no user id, request body or typed text; no product analytics and no advertising identifier.
- **Help and the legal gates are native**: the Help Articles from `@jobhub/help`, suggested while typing and opened in a Sheet; the Signalement form; the acceptance of the Legal Texts after sign-in (ADR-0062, ADR-0072), recorded through the same API.
- **Delivery**: EAS Build and Submit, EAS Update for JavaScript-only fixes on a staging and a production channel. The app carries the platform's Version (ADR-0063); the stores' « What's new » comes from the same Release Note. Tests: Jest with React Native Testing Library, Maestro on a phone, an iPad (landscape, portrait, Split View), an Android tablet and a foldable, a contract test of the GETs against the shared types, accessibility checks at the largest text size, screenshot baselines light and dark.

This amends ADR-0005 (native apps start), ADR-0009 (the system text size in the app), ADR-0011 (the app's session and sign-in) and ADR-0070 (Sheets below 64rem in the app).

## Considered Options

- **Parity with the web in v1**: editing a CV on a phone is where the audience gives up, and the Coach space's reads would all have to become HTTP at once.
- **Two apps, one per role**: double the store reviews and releases, for few coaches.
- **A WebView or Capacitor shell around the responsive web app**: push and the share sheet bolted on, and none of the Claude-like feel the app is for.
- **A `/api/mobile/v1` layer shaped by screen**: a second source of truth beside the routes the web and the extension use.
- **A bearer-token plugin**: a second credential to issue and revoke, for nothing the Expo plugin does not give on the same session row.
- **Native Sign in with Apple and Google returning an ID token**: lifting ADR-0011's refusal would put a second path beside the callback where the linking policy is applied.
- **Expo's push service**: a US intermediary seeing every notification (ADR-0007).
- **Reading a shared page on the device in a hidden WebView**: the extension's grey zone again, on platforms that review behaviour.
- **In-app purchase of Plans beside Stripe**: a second source of truth for ADR-0014 and a 15–30 % fee; revisit if the stores' share of sign-ups calls for it.
- **The prototype's variant B, bottom tabs (a labelled rail on tablets)**, and **variant C, no chrome, destinations as chips above the box**: tried on a phone, A was chosen; it keeps the web's Sidebar as one model across web and app.
- **Phones only in v1, a tablet scaling the phone layout**: dropped once the tablet layout turned out to be ADR-0070's own rules.
