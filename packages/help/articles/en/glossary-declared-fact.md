title: Declared fact
synonyms: [fact you declared, declared, add a skill, add power bi, language level, degree]
---
A fact you tell your AI coach yourself in a conversation: a skill (“add Power BI, I have used it for 3 years”), a language or its level, a degree. Your coach can then propose to add or correct it in your tailored CV or your Master CV, even if your CV did not say it yet.

The change reads « Fact you declared »: check it before you accept it. Jobbbox checks that you did write it in one of your messages in the conversation, in your own words: a typo or another language does not stop you from declaring it. When your coach lists your skills, answering “for the rest it is all good” counts for the ones it has just listed, and for those only. What a job offer says, or what your coach assumes, is never a declared fact: your coach asks whether you have it before proposing anything.
