title: What do I do when the extension says « Jobbbox does not recognise this installation of the extension »?
synonyms: [not recognised, not recognized, installation, capture refused, capture this page, cannot be captured, extension, chrome web store, unpacked, zip]
---
Jobbbox only accepts what its extension sends as published on the Chrome Web Store. A copy installed another way, for instance loaded from a folder on your computer, can read your sign-in but cannot send a job offer: the extension's panel says so with « Jobbbox does not recognise this installation of the extension: the page was not sent. »

The page you are viewing is not the problem. Remove that copy in chrome://extensions, install the Jobbbox extension from the Chrome Web Store, then click « Capture this page » or « Analyse this offer » again.
