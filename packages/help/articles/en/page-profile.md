A profile gathers your Master CV and your search criteria for one role. Its sections: Suggestions, Master CV, ATS Score, Download and Manage.

Start with the ATS Score: it says whether recruitment software will read your CV well. The fixes your coach proposes appear in « Suggestions »; accept or dismiss them one by one.

Each time you save the Master CV, a new version is created, and you can go back to an earlier one. « Manage » lets you rename, duplicate or archive the profile.
