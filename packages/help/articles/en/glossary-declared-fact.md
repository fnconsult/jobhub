title: Declared fact
synonyms: [fact you declared, declared, add a skill, add power bi, language level, degree]
---
A fact you tell your AI coach yourself in a conversation: a skill (“add Power BI, I have used it for 3 years”), a language or its level, a degree. Your coach can then propose to add or correct it in your tailored CV or your Master CV, even if your CV did not say it yet.

The change reads « Fact you declared »: check it before you accept it. Jobbbox checks that you did write it in one of your messages in the conversation. What a job offer says, or what your coach assumes, is never a declared fact: your coach asks whether you have it before proposing anything.
