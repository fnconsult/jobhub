A Job Digest is a selection of new job offers matching a profile's search criteria. You get it by email and find it on this page too.

For each profile, choose whether to receive it and how often, within your plan: every week on Monday at 7 am, or every day at 7 am (Paris time).

Only job offers you have not seen yet are in it. When there are none, nothing is sent.
