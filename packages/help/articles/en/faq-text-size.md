title: How do I make the text larger?
synonyms: [text size, font, larger, bigger, small, large print, zoom]
---
In My account, under Preferences: the « Text size » slider has four steps, Small, Standard, Medium and Large. Standard is the default. Small makes the text smaller than the default; Medium and Large make it larger. If you prefer the slightly larger text from before 26 November 2026, choose Medium.

Your choice is kept on this device. On another computer or phone, set it again.
