title: Approve a Coach Page
synonyms: [Coach public profile, presentation moderation, Coach photo]
---
1. With the Coaching manager or Super-admin role, open “Human coaches”, then “Coach profiles to approve”.
2. Review the proposed presentation, specialties, languages and portrait. Candidates cannot read a pending version; the previous approved version remains visible.
3. Choose “Approve profile” to publish, or enter “Reason for refusal” then choose “Refuse profile”. A reason is required and included in the email to the coach.
4. If the coach submitted another proposal in the meantime, reload the queue: an old decision cannot replace the latest version. A completed decision appears in Administrator actions.
