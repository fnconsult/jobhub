title: Why am I asked for two-step verification?
synonyms: [two-step verification, two-factor, 2fa, mfa, mandatory, code asked, authenticator app, backup codes, set up, magic link, turn off, lost phone, reset]
---
As a coach, you read the profiles and applications of the candidates who gave you access. To protect this data, two-step verification is mandatory for human coaches, as it is for the Jobbbox team.

After a magic link, if your account has no factor yet, Jobbbox asks you to set up an authenticator app before opening your coach space. Scan the QR code with your app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password…), enter the code it shows in « Code the app shows », click « Confirm », then keep your 10 backup codes and click « I have kept my codes ». From then on, every sign-in by magic link asks for a code from your app. Signing in with a provider, such as Google, does not ask for it.

You cannot turn it off: the « Security » section of My account has no « Turn off » button. If you lose your phone and your backup codes, ask the Jobbbox team to reset it: you will get an email, and you will set it up again the next time you sign in.
