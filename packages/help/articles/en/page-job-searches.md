« Job searches » is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one every visit. There your coach searches for job offers that meet a profile's search criteria.

Your searches are listed there in the card « Your job searches by profile »: when each was started, where it stands and how many job offers it found; « New results » marks those you have not seen yet. Click a line to open the search, which you can stop while it is searching. To start one, use the « Start a search » suggestion above the message box, one per profile if you have several. « See the job offers received by email », at the bottom of the card, opens « Received by email » beside the conversation.

« Received by email » shows the offers taken from the emails you forward to Jobbbox, by profile, newest first, with how many arrived this week. Each one says when and from which domain it was received, and its Match Score; save it as you would a job offer found by a search. The offers none of your profiles matches closely enough (match under 60) are in « Unclassified ». The emails that gave no offer this week are listed with their reason, for example « Unrecognised sender »: « It's a job alert » has that sender recognised for you from its next email. « Filter too broad » warns you when your mailbox forwards far more than your job alerts. As long as email intake is not switched on, « Set up forwarding step by step » guides you; it is set in My account, Email intake section.

Each search counts once against your plan's quota. If it fails, or if you stop it before it has found a job offer, it does not count.

In the menu, « Job searches » unfolds into your most recent searches, those with results not seen yet marked with a dot; « See all » leads to this page.
