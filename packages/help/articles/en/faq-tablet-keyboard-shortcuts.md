With a keyboard connected to your iPad, the Jobbbox app answers these shortcuts. On an Android tablet, Ctrl takes the place of ⌘.

- ⌘N: « New conversation ».
- ⌘↩: « Send the message » written in the box.
- ⌘\: « Show or hide the menu ».
- ⌘I: « Show or hide the Fiche », or Activity on the home page and the menus.
- ⌘W: « Close the Work Pane ».
- Esc: « Close the pane » on top, whether the menu, the page open over the conversation or the Work Pane.

To see the ones the page offers right now, hold the ⌘ key on the iPad. On Android, press Meta and / together.

With a mouse or a trackpad, buttons and links light up under the pointer. A right click on an application, a job offer or a conversation in the menu opens its actions, « Open » or « Share the link » for instance; with a finger, it is a long press.

The app shares the screen with another one in Split View, Slide Over or Stage Manager, and opens or folds with a foldable phone: it rearranges itself to the room it has, without losing the conversation or where you were in it.
