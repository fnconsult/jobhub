On top of your AI coach, you can book a video session with an HR professional selected by Jobbbox. You pay first, then you pick your time.

1. In the menu, open « My human coaches ».
2. Under « Choose a coach », click « Book a session with … » for the coach of your choice.
3. On the « Your order » page, check the summary, then tick « I accept the terms of sale (version of …) ». The box asking for the service to start at once is optional.
4. Click « Go to payment », then pay on the secure page of Stripe, our payment provider. Your order confirmation is sent to you by email.
5. Back in Jobbbox, « Payment received. Now pick the time of your session. » shows. Click « Pick my time with … on Cal.com » and book your time.
6. Come back to Jobbbox and click « I've booked: enter the date ». Enter the « Session date and time (Paris time) » and, if you have it, the « Video call link (optional) », then click « Save ».
7. The session joins your agenda. If you entered the video call link, on the day, click « Join the video call with … », in « My human coaches », under « My paid sessions ».

While a session has no date, it waits in « To schedule », in your agenda. During the 14 days after payment, and as long as the session has not taken place, you can withdraw from the session, in your agenda.
