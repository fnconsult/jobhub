---
kind: faq
title: How do I get or delete my data?
synonyms: [data, gdpr, delete, erase, export, download, privacy, close my account]
---
In My account, under My data: « Download all my data » gives you one file with everything Jobbbox keeps about you.

In the same place, « Permanently delete my account » closes it at once, once you have typed your email address to confirm. Your profiles, CVs, applications and documents are erased, for good within 30 days at most, and your subscription ends.

Download your data first if you want to keep it.
