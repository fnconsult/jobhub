European AI is a consent you give, or not, in My account, « European AI » section. With your consent, every AI request that contains your identity data is processed by Mistral AI, a European AI provider. « Give my consent » records it, with its date; « Withdraw my consent » withdraws it, at any time.

Each choice has its trade-off:

- With consent: the request keeps your identity data, and it is processed in the European Union.
- Without consent: before sending, Jobbbox removes your identity from the request, which goes to our usual provider, which may be outside the European Union.

Neither is recommended: choose the one that suits you. Reading your job offer emails stays in the European Union either way. The details are in the privacy policy, section [AI in Jobbbox](/legal/confidentialite#ia).
