title: How do I get a cover letter and an outreach message?

In an application's « Documents » section, your coach drafts the cover letter and the outreach message from your Master CV and the job offer.

- « Draft the letter » prepares a cover letter, signed with your Master CV's contact details.
- « Draft the message » prepares a short message to a contact at the employer. Choose whether it is « Sent by » « Email » or « LinkedIn InMail », and its « Recipient »: one of your Enriched Contacts, or « No one in particular (the roles to contact) ».

Edit the text, then click « Save my changes ». « Draft again » replaces the current text, which stays in « Versions ». The « Document language » is by default the job offer's; it applies to the next draft. The whole text is written in that language, even when your Master CV is in another: your coach translates what it takes from it, and invents nothing.

Nothing is lost: each draft, each click on « Save my changes », each edit by your AI coach that you apply and each restore keeps a version. « Versions », under the text, lists them newest first, with their date, their origin (« Drafted by your AI coach », « Changed by you », « Edit by your AI coach, applied by you », « Restore of version … ») and their language; the current one reads « Current version ». « View version … » shows it read-only, and « Restore this version » makes it the current version again, under a new number: the version you replace stays in the list, nothing is deleted. Jobbbox keeps the last 20 versions of each document; beyond that, the oldest goes. You can also ask your coach to show you the versions of your letter: it lists them, shows you the one you want and proposes to bring it back, under its reply, never restoring it itself.

Jobbbox sends nothing on your behalf: click « Copy the text », or « Open in my mail app » for an email, then send it yourself. The letter also downloads as PDF or Word.
