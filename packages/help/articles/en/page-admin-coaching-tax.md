This page prepares a closed income year for a person authorized in the Coaches Back Office. The currently supported income year is 2026. Collection and preparation require the necessary effective texts. The flow handles French tax identities without permanent establishments outside France; other circumstances require the appropriate team’s assistance.

Enter the operator identity before preparation. Seller identities must be complete. Credits and refunds are read from frozen payment accounts and reconciled with payouts. Unresolved cases prevent preparation; amounts are never silently corrected. The prepared document stays immutable with unique references: retrying does not create a second initial return.

“Download draft XML” remains available for human review. “Download prepared XML” and copy notifications require structural validation against the available official schema. This proves neither business-rule checks, filing nor acceptance by DGFiP. The authorized person performs checks, official test-portal validation, required compression and encryption, and filing. Corrections follow the initial references; do not file multiple initial packets to replace an earlier one.

Preparation, validation and downloads are audited without copying TINs, IBANs or full identities into the journal. Private copies show only the authorized Seller’s data. Recipients without an available verified account remain pending team reconciliation.

Before any filing, “Replace unfiled preparation” recalculates corrected data after explicit confirmation that no initial file for the year has been filed. The earlier preparation remains a superseded archive, its normal export is blocked and its pending copies are no longer notified. Review exact cent-based totals and bank payouts through “Download credit and payout reconciliation”. After filing, use only the corrective procedure appropriate to the original references.
