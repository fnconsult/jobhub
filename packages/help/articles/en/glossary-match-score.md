---
kind: glossary
title: Match Score
synonyms: [match, fit, compatibility, rating, percentage]
---
A score out of 100 that says how well a CV fits a job offer: skills, experience, location, contract type and salary.

The breakdown shows, criterion by criterion, what matches and what is missing. A criterion the job offer or your criteria say nothing about does not count; when too few criteria could be compared, the score is called partial.

It is worked out with the profile's Master CV, and with your Tailored CV if you have one.
