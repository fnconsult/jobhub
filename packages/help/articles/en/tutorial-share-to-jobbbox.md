In the Jobbbox app, on your phone or tablet, you can send a job offer to Jobbbox from your browser, LinkedIn, Indeed or Mail, without copying its link. Jobbbox makes it a private job offer, which only you can see.

1. In the other app, open the job offer and tap the share button, then choose « Jobbbox » in the list. On iPhone and iPad, if it is not there, add it from the end of the row of apps.
2. The Jobbbox app opens on « Share to Jobbbox ». If you are not signed in, sign in: the screen then comes back, with what you shared.
3. Check the « Job offer link » and the « Job offer text », correct them if need be, then tap « Send to Jobbbox ».
4. If the site does not let Jobbbox read the job offer (LinkedIn, Indeed…), the screen says so. Copy the job offer's text in the other app, paste it in « Job offer text », then tap « Send to Jobbbox » again.
5. The job offer opens in its own conversation with your AI coach. Tap « Score against my profile » to see its Match Score.

Jobbbox reads the link, never your phone, and only where the site allows it. Nothing you share is kept, except the job offer created. The Match Score counts against your plan's limits, as on the website; if you have reached them, the app says so, and your subscription is managed on jobbbox.ai.
