title: Consumption
synonyms: [your consumption, ai actions, usage, billing period, grid, day by day, by tool]

What you used of Jobbbox, counted in AI actions: each coach reply, job search, Match Score, ATS Score, tailored CV, cover letter or outreach message, company dossier, enriched contact or CV read counts as one, under its tool. The first job search of a new profile, offered while it is created, is not counted.

« Your consumption », on My subscription, shows it over the billing period (the current month on the Free plan), by tool and day by day over twelve months. It does not replace the quotas, which count each week.

It is counted in uses, never in tokens or euros, kept 12 months, erased with your account and included in your data export. It keeps no content and no name of an AI model: those are in AI Activity.
