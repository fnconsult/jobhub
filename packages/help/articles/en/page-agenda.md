The agenda is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one every visit. It shows your interviews and your sessions with a human coach: the list is a card there, your upcoming appointments day by day, and the sessions « To schedule ». Ask the coach to prepare an interview or to remind you what is coming.

« Week » and « Month » open the calendar beside the conversation. « Add an interview » opens its form there, which closes once the interview is added; you can also add one from the « Interviews » section of an application at the « Interview » stage. On a phone, the calendar and the form open over the conversation, full screen: « Close » closes them.

On an upcoming appointment, « Add to my calendar » downloads an .ics file: open it to add the appointment to your calendar. If the date changes, add it again: the one already added does not update. A cancelled interview stays struck through, with its cancellation date, and can no longer be added.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), the agenda is a list: the sessions « To schedule », then your appointments day by day. Tap an interview to open the « Interviews » section of its application, a session to find its coach. « Add to my calendar » opens your phone's new event screen, already filled in: check it, then save. On iPhone and iPad, Jobbbox only asks for the right to add events, never to read your calendar. If you refuse, or if no calendar is available, the .ics file opens in the share sheet: pick your calendar, or send it.

You can also ask your coach to add, move or cancel an interview, for example by writing to it to add your Airbus interview on Thursday at 2 pm: it prepares a proposal under its reply, with the full date, the duration, the format and the place or link you give it. Nothing changes until you click « Apply »; « Edit before applying » opens the interview form pre-filled, « Ignore » sets it aside, and « Undo », once the proposal is applied, puts the interview back as it was. If the application is not at the « Interview » stage yet, the same proposal moves it there: both changes are listed. A rescheduling undone within the hour leaves no trace; later, it stays in the interview's history. The coach never deletes an interview: « Delete », on the interview, is for an entry made by mistake; an interview that will not take place is cancelled.

Past appointments stay visible, dimmed. The day before at 6 pm, Jobbbox can email you a reminder: set it in My account, under Reminders.

For 14 days after you pay for a session with a human coach, and as long as it has not taken place, « Withdraw from the contract here » cancels it and refunds it in full; the coach is told.
