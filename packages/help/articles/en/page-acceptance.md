This screen shows after you sign in when the Terms of use are waiting for your agreement: the first time, then for each new version that changes your rights. A minor version is not asked again.

« Read the Terms of use » opens the version to accept, with its date. Tick « I accept the Terms of use (version of …) », then « Accept and continue »: you go back to the page you wanted to open. The box is never ticked for you, and the screen does not come back for the same version.

Until you accept, you can still open My data, to download your data or delete your account, and Subscription, to withdraw or cancel. Your subscription keeps running.

In English, the screen says the French text prevails.
