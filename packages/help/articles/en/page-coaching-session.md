After a session, your Human Coach may publish a report. In “My Human Coaches”, “Read the report and continue the conversation” opens that session’s own conversation. The message bears the coach’s name and “Human Coach”; it is not an AI Coach reply. The AI Coach can then help organise your next steps: only actions that copy no text are offered. A correction is a new message. Your data export includes reports, including private drafts that do not appear on screen.

During your paid session’s joining window, “Shared Reading” lets you choose a document and “Show my coach”. Your coach follows your page and scrolling, without access to other documents. “Stop” removes the temporary document immediately. Your coach can also present a PDF.
