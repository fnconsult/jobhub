This page shows while Jobbbox restarts, during an update for instance, or is not answering for the moment. Its title is « Jobbbox will be back in a moment », in English and in French (English first on an English page, French first otherwise), with the error's code (502, 503 or 504) above. Your data is safe and nothing is lost.

You have nothing to do: the page reloads itself every 15 seconds and takes you back to the page you asked for as soon as Jobbbox answers. To try again at once, click the « Try again » button.

If you were writing something, what you had not saved yet may need to be typed again.

The page lasts more than a few minutes? See « How do I contact you? ».
