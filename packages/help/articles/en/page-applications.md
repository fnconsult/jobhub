This page gathers every job offer you follow. Each job offer you save becomes an application, linked to one of your profiles.

Show them as a list or as a board, one column per status: To apply, Applied, Followed up, Interview, Offer received, Accepted, Rejected, Abandoned. Filter by profile or by status; archived applications stay hidden unless you ask to see them.

Open an application to prepare your documents, see the Match Score and record your interviews.

With the new layout, « My applications » is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one at each visit. Your applications in progress are listed in a card: their status, their Match Score and those « to handle », when a follow-up is ready or an application still has no answer. Click a line to open the application; « Show them » also shows the archived applications. Ask the coach which one to handle first, or start from a suggestion above the message box: « Add an application », « Search for job offers ».

In the menu, « My applications » unfolds into your most recent applications, and those to handle, marked with a dot; « See all » leads to this page. The arrow beside it folds or unfolds the list, and this browser remembers it.
