# Recherches d'offres opens on the Offer box: every Job Offer that arrives waits for « Suivre » or « Écarter »

Status: accepted (product owner, 2026-11-05: variant B of the prototype https://claude.ai/artifact/NCr5FgAZxnSuzVcAiAXkpb, its header, day and tier sticky while scrolling, « À ne pas manquer » set off by a turning border). Tracking #820, tickets #815 to #819, Legal Text issue #814. Amends ADR-0070 for the Recherches d'offres menu. Re-scopes #692 (the app's Recherches d'offres), now blocked by #815.

Job Offers reach a Candidate through four doors today, each with its own page and its own idea of « new »: a Job Search's results (« Nouveaux résultats » until its Job Search Notification is dismissed), a Job Digest (an offer never shown before for the Profile), « Reçues par email » (the count of « nouvelles » this week) and a Job Offer link's card in the conversation (« À traiter » until followed). Nothing records what the Candidate decided about a Job Offer they did not save: an offer they looked at and left is indistinguishable from one they never saw, so offers slip through. The product owner wants « a kind of inbox zero: keep involving the user until every entry is at least treated ».

- **The Offer box (« Offres à traiter ») is Recherches d'offres' main view.** It is the page itself, not a card in the menu's thread: its header (the count « N à traiter », « k traitées », a progress bar to zero) and its source chips stay at the top while the page scrolls, and so do the current day's heading and the current tier's heading under them. The menu's Coach Conversation keeps its box at the foot of the page; its messages follow the Offer box, which stays first. The Job Searches by Profile, today the menu card, open in the Work Pane from « Recherches par Profil » at the foot of the Offer box, next to « Reçues par email ». This is the one menu whose page is not its conversation (amends ADR-0070).
- **An Offer box entry is one Job Offer for one Candidate**, made at its first Arrival and holding every Arrival source since: a Job Search that found it, a Job Digest that sent it, a Received offer email that gave it (new for the Candidate), a Job Offer link pasted in a Coach Conversation. The same Job Offer arriving again adds its source chip to the entry (« Recherche · Email »); it does not move it. Its day is that of its first Arrival, Paris time. Its Profile is the one the first Arrival named (Email intake's best Match Score, the Job Search's or Job Digest's Profile, « Non classées » under 60). A Private Job Offer is an entry like any other, for its Candidate only (ADR-0049).
- **A Capture or a Share capture enters only once followed.** The extension and the app's share sheet are already a choice; an unsaved Capture leaves no entry and no new record of who captured what. When the Candidate follows one (creates its Application), the entry is made already « Suivie », with the chip « Extension » or « Partage », so the day's arrivals show every door.
- **« Traitée » is « Suivie » or « Écartée », nothing else.** « Suivre » creates the Application (« À postuler »), as « Enregistrer » does today; an Application created anywhere else (a Job Search's page, the extension, a Coach Action) makes its entry « Suivie » too, and it stays so while the Application exists. « Écarter » asks an optional Dismissal reason (« Pas pour moi », « Salaire », « Lieu », « Contrat », « Déjà postulé ailleurs »). Opening or reading an offer treats nothing. « Revenir sur ce choix » puts an Écartée entry back to à traiter, at any time.
- **« Plus tard » keeps the entry à traiter** and moves it out of sight until the next Monday 00:00 Paris time, when it comes back under « Aujourd'hui ». It is not a decision and the count still holds it. No date picker.
- **« Écartées » is a block of its own**, folded, at the foot of the Offer box: the entries écartées in the last 30 days, the latest first, with their Dismissal reason and « Revenir sur ce choix ».
- **Importance is the Match Score in three Importance tiers**: « À ne pas manquer » (80 and more), « À regarder » (60 to 79), « Peu adaptées » (under 60, or not computable). Within a tier the most recent Arrival comes first. An entry « À ne pas manquer » still à traiter is set off by a border that turns slowly around it (still when the system asks for reduced motion). Rules only, no AI: the Match Score is the one each source already computes against the entry's Profile.
- **The page reads « Aujourd'hui », then « Précédemment »**, each grouped by Importance tier. « Aujourd'hui » folds its entries already suivies under « Suivies aujourd'hui ». « Précédemment » shows each entry's arrival day and offers « Tout écarter (n) » per tier. The source chips (« Toutes », « Recherche », « Job Digest », « Email », « Lien du coach », « Extension ») filter both and count what is still à traiter.
- **An entry left undecided leaves the count when its Posting date passes 30 days** (the rule Job discovery and the Job Digest already apply; the first Arrival stands in when the Posting date is unknown). It goes to « Expirées sans décision », folded, out of the count, where « Écarter » still works. Offers are never écartées automatically.
- **Écartée means already seen everywhere.** A Job Digest never offers an écartée Job Offer again, Email intake counts it « déjà vue », and a Job Search's results show it « Écartée ». The shared Job Offer and other Candidates' entries are untouched.
- **The count reaches the Candidate in three places, with no new notification**: the Sidebar's « Recherches d'offres » (and the app's tab), the app icon's badge, and one line of the Job Digest, « Il vous reste N offres à traiter ». No push, no email of its own.
- **At launch the Offer box is filled from the last 30 days**: the Job Offers of Job Searches, Job Digests, Received offer emails and Job Offer cards the Candidate already has, without an Application, whose Posting date is under 30 days. A Job Offer with an Application starts « Suivie ».
- **The web and the app ship together**, ticket by ticket, through the same `/api` read (ADR-0076). #692's « Garder » / « Écarter » become this ADR's « Suivre » / « Écarter ».
- **What is kept**: one row per Candidate and Job Offer (its Profile, first Arrival, Arrival sources with their dates, decision and when, Dismissal reason, « Plus tard » date). Tied to the Candidate, in their Data export, deleted with their account, and with its Profile. An entry is deleted 12 months after its first Arrival. Never shown to Administrators, never given to the AI Coach beyond what the Candidate's own reads already allow.

## Considered Options

The prototype compared:

- **A, the Offer box as the menu card in the conversation (ADR-0070 as is)**: kept the rule that every menu is its conversation, but the box scrolled away with the messages; an inbox needs its count and filters always in view.
- **B, the Offer box as the page, the coach's box at its foot**: chosen.

Also weighed with the product owner:

- **« Traitée » when opened**: zero could be reached without deciding anything.
- **« Plus tard » leaving the count**: a way to empty the count without deciding.
- **Undecided entries kept forever** or **écartées automatically after 14 days**: either an endless debt after a week away, or decisions the Candidate never made.
- **Importance by source**, or **Match Score raised by urgency**: the source says little about fit; urgency is shown instead on the entry (« sort dans n j » in its last 7 days).
- **Every Capture as an entry**: would need a new record of what a Candidate captured without saving; the product owner keeps Capture as a deliberate gesture.
- **A daily push**: the badge and the Job Digest's line are enough.
