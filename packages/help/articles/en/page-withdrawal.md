This page lets you withdraw from a contract made on Jobbbox, without giving a reason: your subscription to a plan, for 14 days after you subscribed, or a session with a human coach, for 14 days after you paid for it. A session booked with the request for the service to start at once can no longer be withdrawn from once it has taken place. The period runs to the end of the 14th day, Paris time; when that day is a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday, it runs on to the next working day.

Check the contract, its date and the amount paid, enter your name, then choose where to receive the acknowledgement: your account's address or another one. « Confirmer la rétractation » (confirm the withdrawal) ends the contract at once and refunds you to the payment method you used; depending on your bank, the refund shows within 5 to 10 business days. The refund is in full, but for a subscription taken with the request for the service to start at once: you then pay for the service already provided, and the page shows the amount « Refunded if you confirm now » (the amount paid, less the share of the days elapsed since the start of the period).

You get an acknowledgement by email straight away, with the date and time of your withdrawal: keep it.
