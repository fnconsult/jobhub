title: Cancel a session or choose after a coach cancellation
synonyms: [cancel session, choose refund, same coach booking, another coach, cancellation follow-up]
---
When the new conditions are activated, use your private session follow-up. Contractual cancellation and legal withdrawal remain separate processes.

1. Open **My human coaches**, then **Cancellations and refunds**. In the app, this follow-up opens without losing your signed-in session.
2. To cancel yourself, find the session, choose **Request a full refund** and confirm. The control closes this action after the 24-hour limit; contact your coach or support then.
3. If the coach cancelled, read their reason and the deadline, then choose the refund, **Continue with the same coach**, or **Choose another coach**. A cancellation reported only by Cal.com first needs the coach’s confirmation.
4. To continue with the same coach, open **Book without another payment** and choose your slot. For another coach, wait for confirmation of the original refund, then open **Review and accept the new order**: check the seller and price before any payment.
5. Find refund status in the same follow-up. A failure needs support intervention; no new payment is needed to correct it. Without a choice within seven days of the coach’s confirmed cancellation, a full refund is requested automatically.
