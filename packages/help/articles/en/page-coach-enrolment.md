Present your independent coaching activity or Association: contact and legal names, email, legal status, SIRET, specialties, languages and introduction. Add a useful professional PDF, up to ten megabytes and two hundred pages, without Candidate information. “Submit my application” starts email confirmation unless your signed-in address is already verified. The team then reviews the application; professional access awaits the current contract.
