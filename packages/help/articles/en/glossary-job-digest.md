title: Job Digest
synonyms: [digest, job offer selection, offers by email, alert, newsletter]
---
A regular email, also shown in Jobbbox, with the new job offers matching a profile's search criteria.

You turn it on profile by profile, every week (on Monday at 7 am) or every day (at 7 am), depending on your plan. It only holds job offers you have not seen yet, and never counts against your other quotas. While job offers wait for you in « Offers to handle », it ends with a line saying how many you have left to handle.

You can unsubscribe from the email, without signing in.
