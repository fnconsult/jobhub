My account gathers your settings, in sections: Profiles, Follow-ups, Reminders, European AI, Sign-in, Preferences, Tools and My data.

In Preferences, change the interface language and the text size. In Follow-ups, choose after how many working days your coach suggests a follow-up; in Reminders, the reminder email the day before your appointments.

My data lets you download all your data or delete your account.
