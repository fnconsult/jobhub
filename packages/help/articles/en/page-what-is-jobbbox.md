Jobbbox helps you with your job search, with an AI coach. It builds your master CV and search criteria with you, looks for the job offers that suit you, then prepares your applications: a CV tailored to each offer, a cover letter. You then follow your applications, interviews and follow-ups in one place.

Jobbbox is for anyone looking for a job, whether for the first time or after years in the same post. It is made to be read without effort: no jargon, a help article for every word to know, and text you can make larger.

Your coach is an AI, and it says so. If you wish, human coaches can also support you, in sessions paid separately. The Jobbbox Chrome extension saves a job offer from the page where you read it.

This page, « Sign in to Jobbbox », is also where you create your account: no password, enter « Your email address » then click « Send me a sign-in link ». If you don't have an account yet, it is created, on the Free Plan: nothing to pay to start.

The email you receive holds a link and a 6-digit code. Reading it on another device, your phone for instance? Stay on this page: type the code in « Enter the code from the email », then click « Sign in with this code ». The link and the code are valid for 15 minutes, once: using one makes the other unusable. After 5 wrong codes, the code no longer works: click « Send me a new email ».
