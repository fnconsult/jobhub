This section separates emails from app notifications. The existing day-before email groups your appointments and is sent at 18:00 Paris time. When the new coaching notifications are activated, another setting lets you receive or switch off the email one hour before a coaching session.

The “Your session starts soon” push has its own switch: switching off email does not switch it off, and vice versa. An app notification names no person, job or company; details remain in your private session. The app also needs permission to notify you on the device.

The reminder’s “Join” link opens your session. Times use your time zone, with Paris time alongside when different; the call link becomes available ten minutes before the start. A cancelled or withdrawn session receives no further reminder. Cancellation/refund follow-up remains separate from these settings.
