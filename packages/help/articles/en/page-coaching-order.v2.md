This page shows when you book a session with a human coach, before payment. The summary gives the coach and the session's price including VAT, a one-off payment that does not renew.

Once our terms of sale are published, tick « I accept the terms of sale (version of …) » to go on; the link just above opens that version. The French text prevails. The box is never ticked for you.

The second box is optional: « I ask for the service to start at once. I understand that if I withdraw, I will pay for the service already provided, and that I lose my right of withdrawal once the session has taken place. » Without it, you pay and book your session just the same, and a withdrawal within 14 days is refunded in full, even once the session has taken place. With it, you may withdraw as long as the session has not taken place, refunded in full; no longer afterwards.

« Go to payment » opens the secure page of Stripe, our payment provider. Once the payment is confirmed, you receive the confirmation of your order by email: keep it.

## Who sells your session

“Sold by” identifies the offer’s Seller, also named in your order and confirmation. Jobbbox uses its session price and your Plan’s discount. A third-party Seller sets their own price; their legal name, status and SIRET appear, with Jobbbox’s marketplace role. Only offers with payment and selling conditions activated can be ordered.
