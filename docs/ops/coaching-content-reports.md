# Coaching content reports (#886)

`/signaler-coaching?coach=<UUID>&target=page|offer` is a public route. Links on the Coach listing open it without requiring the visitor to retain a sign-in session. The API accepts only existing published targets, bounded JSON, trusted origins, a good-faith declaration and a valid contact except for child-abuse reports. Ten attempts per HMAC address/hour are allowed; the budget is purged after one day. No raw IP or user agent is stored.

The existing Signalements section hosts the human queue. Only its write-authorized administrators see personal coaching reports or submit decisions; Help-editor read access reveals none of them. Decisions require a reason and the current revision. The Back Office transaction saves the outcome and independent recipient outbox entries alongside its Audit. Audit records outcome/revision and reason presence, not the free-form text. A stale form cannot replace a newer decision.

Removing or suspending a page hides its public listing and portrait, and blocks new orders. Restricting an offer blocks its public price/purchase and new Checkout initiation, including an earlier quote. Existing paid services and frozen financial records remain accessible. Each restrictive case applies independently: maintaining one report does not clear another restriction. Restoration requires an explicit reasoned decision for every active case. A previously created Stripe Checkout is an existing remote payment context; operators should investigate its status as part of moderation, without rewriting paid snapshots.

The minute Worker retries each recipient independently with five-minute backoff. Successful receipts/decisions are not replayed because another recipient fails. Missing Jobbbox contact configuration or a Seller without an available contact leaves its notice pending; restore MAIL_FROM/the authorized Seller contact rather than marking it sent manually. Association recipient selection uses an accepted, verified manager; a missing address can recover from the frozen Seller identity. As with other SMTP outboxes, interruption after remote acceptance but before checkpoint can repeat an email.

Reporter account erasure cascades the owned report and its reporter delivery facts. Anonymous reports require Support verification for data-subject handling; never disclose a report merely because someone knows its reference or supplies an email address. The anonymous public-target restriction remains until restored; Seller decision delivery continues independently without the reporter contact or original explanation. Proposed retention: personal reports three years after a decision; completed Seller delivery facts three years; unresolved delivery continues until reconciled. Empty/restored detached cases are purged only after owed notices are complete. These are proposals for #890, not legal approval. No Legal Text Version or production rollout is changed.
