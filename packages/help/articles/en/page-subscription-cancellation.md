This page cancels your subscription to a paid plan. It shows your plan and the date the contract ends, which is the end of the period already paid for: you keep your plan until that date, you are not charged again, and you then move to the Free plan.

« Confirm the cancellation » records your request. You at once receive an acknowledgement by email, with the date of your request and the date the contract ends: keep it.

Changed your mind? Until the end date, undo the cancellation from My subscription, with « Manage my subscription ».
