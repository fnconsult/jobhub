## Your Match Score, without an account
- goto @offre-de-demo
- click button « Analyser cette offre avec Jobbbox »
- goto @panneau-extension
- upload label « Votre CV » « cv-claire-demo.pdf »
- click button « Calculer mon Match Score »

Without an account, your CV and the job offer are deleted at the latest 24 hours after your first analysis, or as soon as you close your browser. By creating your account, you keep them.

## Create your account
- scroll link « Créer mon compte et garder cette offre »
- click link « Créer mon compte et garder cette offre »

In the extension's panel, once your Match Score is computed, click « Create my account and keep this job offer ». Jobbbox opens in a new tab.

## Your email address
- fill label « Your email address » « claire.guest@motions.jobbbox.test »
- click button « Send me a sign-in link »

Enter « Your email address », then click « Send me a sign-in link ».

## Open the email
- open-email « Your Jobbbox sign-in link »
- click link « Sign in to Jobbbox »

Open the email « Your Jobbbox sign-in link » and click « Sign in to Jobbbox », within 15 minutes: your account is created, without a password. If Jobbbox asks you to accept the Terms of use, read them, tick the box then click « Accept and continue ».

## Go back to the job offer
- goto @panneau-extension

Go back to the job offer's tab: the extension's panel updates by itself. Your CV has become your profile, and the job offer is saved in your applications, as “To apply”.

## See your application
- click link « See my application »

Click « See my application » to open it in Jobbbox.
