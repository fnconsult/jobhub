Your AI coach can propose to send, from Jobbbox, a message already written for an application: its follow-up, its outreach message or a reply to an exchange. It never sends anything itself: even if you ask it to send without asking you, it proposes a card, and your click sends it.

1. In the application's conversation, ask for instance for instance: send the follow-up, or send my outreach message to Claire. Under its reply, a card « Send to », followed by the recipient's first name and initial, shows the recipient, the sender (your first and last name via Jobbbox, from a jobbbox.ai address), your sign-in address in copy, the subject and the whole text.
2. To change the subject or the text before sending, click « Edit before sending »: they open beside the conversation. The recipient cannot be changed: it is the one Jobbbox knows for this application (a revealed enriched contact, the outreach message's contact or the exchange's sender).
3. Click « Send ». The email goes once, even if you click twice, and the card shows « Sent by you at … ». You receive its copy, and the recipient's replies come straight to your inbox.
4. Find what you sent in the application's « Exchanges » section, under « Sent from Jobbbox », with its date. A follow-up sent also moves the application to « Followed up ».

Once it has gone, an email cannot be undone. Sends are limited per day: the card shows « Sent today: … of … », and, once the limit is reached, « Limit reached » in place of « Send »; try again the next day, or copy the text to send it from your email. If Jobbbox does not know whether an email went, the card says so and it is never sent again: check your inbox, you are in copy.
