title: Review a coach application
synonyms: [coach enrolment, candidature coach, demande coach]
---
1. With the Coaching manager or Super-admin role, open Human Coaches and “Coach applications”. Only email-confirmed applications appear. Review identity, legal status, SIRET, specialties, languages and presentation. “Read supporting document” opens the private Back Office PDF.
2. After checking, “Approve application” creates the coach or Association and its management grant. Contract acceptance remains required: the new coach cannot yet be booked or read any Candidate. A returning retired coach receives a new identity.
3. To decline, enter the “Reason for declining” and choose “Decline with this reason”. The email repeats the reason. The decision is audited without copying the reason or PDF into the audit. Direct Back Office coach creation remains available.
