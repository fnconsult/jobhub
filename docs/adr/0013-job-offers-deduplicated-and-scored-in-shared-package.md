# Job Offers are deduplicated on capture and the Match Score is computed in the shared package

A captured Job Offer is deduplicated by its normalised source URL (ignoring "www", trailing slash, fragment and tracking parameters) or by a fingerprint of its content (ignoring case and spacing). Capturing a posting again returns the Job Offer stored first and ignores the new content, so one Job Offer can be referenced by many Candidates and Guests without copies.

The Match Score is a pure function (`scoreMatch`) in the shared package, with fixed rules rather than an AI task, so the extension's Guest flow and the web app score identically and a CV can be scored without being stored (ADR-0003).

## Amendment: the Job Offer's text read by an AI when its structured data is not enough (issue #94)

A Job Offer captured from a page without JobPosting data has only a title and its text, so every criterion was unknown (a score of 0) or one was (a confident 100). The scoring rules stay fixed and in `scoreMatch`; what changes is what they are given. When a Match Score asked for at `POST /api/match-score` is not a reliable 70 or more and the Job Offer lacks a detail a criterion needs, the `offer_analysis` AI task (public posting text only, ADR-0007) reads the Job Offer's title and text for the missing fields, which are kept on the Job Offer once and for all (never replacing a stated field), and the CV is scored again by the same rules. No AI judges the fit: the breakdown stays explained and identical wherever the Job Offer is scored, and no CV or identity is sent anywhere. The Job Offer page, Job Searches and Job Digests keep scoring synchronously from what is stored, so they benefit from a reading once it is done. If the AI fails or takes more than 20 seconds, the first-phase score is returned, flagged `offerTextUnread`; a slow reading is still kept when it finishes.
