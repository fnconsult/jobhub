« My coach space » is your human coach's page on Jobbbox. Sign in to Jobbbox with the email address the Jobbbox team registered for you, by receiving a sign-in link: « My coach space » then shows in the menu. The Jobbbox name, at the top of each page of your coach space, brings you back to it, and « My account » opens your account.

The page has two parts.

« My coaching sessions » lists the sessions candidates paid for with you, even when they have not given you coach access. Each session shows the candidate's first name and last initial, for example Claire D., and the payment date: « Paid on … ». Until its date is entered, the session is « To schedule ». Once dated, it shows « On … (Paris time) », its duration and « Video link with … ». The tutorial « Enter a session's date and link » explains how to fill them in.

« Candidates with coach access » lists the candidates who gave you coach access. Click one to open their file: their applications, with their status, and their profiles. Everything there is read only, as the page reminds you: « Read only: only the candidate can change their profiles and applications. » Open an application to read its tailored CV, cover letter and outreach message, and to give your review: the tutorial « Write a review of an application document » explains how.

You see neither the candidate's agenda, nor their conversations with their AI coach, nor the note they may have added to a session. The « Help Centre » link, at the bottom of each page of your coach space, leads to this topic of the help.
