A profile is a role you are aiming for: a Master CV and search criteria. You can have several, depending on your plan.

The page is a conversation with your coach, like every other: it opens on its welcome panel (where it says it is an AI and what the page does; « Don't show again » closes it for good), and the message box, at the bottom, carries the « AI Coach » badge, which opens that panel again, and the « Send to the coach » button. If you have a CV, drop it in the « I have a CV » card: your coach reads it and proposes a Master CV. If not, click « Answer my coach's questions », above the message box: your AI coach leads the onboarding interview.

It asks one question at a time, first the role you are looking for and the city where you want to work, then the contract type, remote work, the salary and your career. If an answer is too vague to use, it tells you so and asks again. When it can guess an answer, it suggests it with its reason: « Yes, … » approves it in one click, « No, rather… » lets you write your own. Some questions are answered by choosing an option, such as « Permanent (CDI) » or « Hybrid »; « Skip this question » skips the optional ones. Nothing enters your profile without your answer or your approval: a suggestion you do not approve is never saved. If the AI cannot ask the next question, your coach says so and goes on with its usual questions, without the AI, without asking again what you have already said.

Either way, your coach then says « Here is how I understand what you are looking for. Did I get it right? » and sums up your search in the « Your job search » card, in a few sentences. The key items are in bold: the target role, the contract types, the places, remote work, the minimum salary, your years of experience (counted from the dates of your jobs), your main skills and your latest roles. This summary is composed by Jobbbox from your profile, not by an AI: what you did not give is not in it.

If it is right, click « That looks right »: your profile is saved and your coach confirms it in the conversation; « Continue to my profile » opens its page. « See the details » opens your Master CV and search criteria beside the conversation: check them and correct what needs it, and the summary updates with your changes. « Save my profile », at the bottom of that pane, saves your profile too. Closed, the pane opens again with « See the details », with nothing lost. Nothing is added that you did not say or approve.

Would you rather say it in your own words? Click « Edit »: the card switches to « Editing » and the summary becomes a text you rewrite, as you would explain it to a recruiter (for example: permanent or fixed-term, in Paris rather than Lyon). « Cancel » leaves everything as it was. « Done » has your AI coach read your text and draw from it the changes to your search criteria: the target role, the contract types, the places, remote work, the minimum salary, the sectors and the domains. It shows them to you before changing anything, for example: + Fixed-term (CDD), − Lyon, + Paris (75). « Confirm these changes » applies them: the summary is composed again and your coach asks again whether it got it right; « Cancel » applies nothing. A skill, an experience or a job you held is not changed this way: use « See the details ». If your coach did not understand your text, it tells you so and your summary stays as it was. Your text itself is not saved: only the changes you confirm are, with your profile, at « That looks right ». This use of AI does not count in your plan.

When you answer the questions, your profile is created as soon as you have given the role you are looking for and your city: it appears among your profiles and counts in your plan. If your plan has no room left, your coach tells you so in the conversation and shows you the plan that includes more. Each search criterion you give or approve afterwards, such as the contract type, is saved on it at once; the rest of your answers stay on the page until « That looks right » or « Save my profile », which completes that same profile: its search criteria, then a single new version of its Master CV. If you leave the page before, the profile stays, with what was already saved; its page says « Creation to finish », and « Resume creating it » brings you back here, to the review, to complete and save it.

As soon as your profile is created, your coach starts a first job search for it, on us: it does not count against your weekly job searches quota, even on the Free plan, nor in « Your consumption ». It tells you so in the conversation. If you then change the role you are looking for or the city, by starting the questions again, it starts the search again once, on us too, and stops the previous one; beyond that, your searches are started from Job searches. It is a job search like any other: its page, its notification when it ends, its row in Activity. It is offered only once per profile created here.

You follow that search in « Activity », the panel to the right of the conversation, which opens by itself when the search starts (on a phone it does not: the « AI activity » icon at the top of the page opens it full screen), while you go on answering. It shows in « Right now », as a search of this page, with its four steps (« Reading your profile », « Searching France Travail, careers sites and the web », « Analysing the job offers found », « Picking your best job offers ») and four counters: « sources consulted », « pages read », « job offers analysed » and « job offers that fit you ». Below them, each source says where it stands: « to come », « in progress… », « consulted », « refused » or « unavailable ». « Target companies » shows only when your profile has some.

The first three counters are the ones the search measures itself: they stay at 0 until something is measured, and nothing in them is an estimate. The fourth, « job offers that fit you », counts the job offers found whose Match Score with your profile, as you build it, reaches 60. It is worked out again on the page with each criterion you add, without a new search and without AI: it can go up as well as down. The « Narrowed with: » line says with which criteria; a criterion your coach assumed and you have not confirmed yet is followed by « assumed », in brackets. If the search is stopped or does not succeed, « Right now » says so, with what it found, which is kept. Once over, it leaves « Right now » and becomes a line of the history, « Job search », « Offered, outside your plan »: opened, it gives its 3 to 5 best job offers, each with its employer, its Match Score and a link to its page, then « See all job offers », which opens the search's page. A screen reader hears only the search starting and its end, not every counter.

« Activity », at the top of the page, shows what is happening. While your coach reads your CV or prepares its question, « Right now » shows the step under way, which you can stop; stopping its question takes you to its usual questions. The history then groups the whole creation in a single row, « Profile creation »: the CV reading, or the « Onboarding interview » with the number of your answers, each with the AI model that did it and its tokens; the « Onboarding questionnaire », whose questions are written by Jobbbox, « No AI », when it took over; the « Review »; the « Summary edited », if you used « Edit », with the model that read your text and its tokens; then « Profile created », with version 1 of your Master CV. When you answer the questions, « Profile created » shows as soon as the profile is created, before the review. The same row follows you to your new profile's page, with its first ATS Score once computed. The interview, the questionnaire and the review are not kept, and your exchange with the coach is not kept as a conversation: once the page is reloaded, the row shows only the CV reading, version 1 and the ATS Score. The onboarding interview does not count in your plan. The « Conversation context » stays empty, « The coach has not replied yet. », until your coach's first reply in the profile's conversation.

You can also write straight in the message box: your first message starts the interview. During the review, your coach only sends you back to it; after « That looks right », it points you to « Continue to my profile »: once the profile is saved, you can talk to it about the profile on its page.

To answer, click « Send to the coach » or press Enter; for a yes-or-no question, click « Yes » or « No », or type it. The message box starts on one line and grows with your text, up to about eight lines; longer text scrolls inside it. Enter sends your answer and Shift+Enter adds a line. After sending, the box returns to one line.
