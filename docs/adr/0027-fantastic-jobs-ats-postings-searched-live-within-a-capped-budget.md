# Fantastic.jobs' ATS postings are searched live, within a capped budget

Job discovery found Job Offers through France Travail, the web search and three ATS public APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) read one posting at a time. Fantastic.jobs indexes the career pages of 58 ATS platforms hourly, with structured fields and a reliable posting date, behind one API. We decided to add it as a Job discovery source of its own (#243): after the France Travail search and before the web search, each run searches its `active-ats` endpoint by the Search Criteria title and location, and captures each job at its ATS page, so it deduplicates with Job Offers already stored and with those read from Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby. Its key is a Provider Secret (`FANTASTIC_JOBS_API_KEY`), entered per environment in the Back Office's Clés, staging and production alike (ADR-0020).

- **`active-ats` only.** `active-jb` holds LinkedIn's postings (a Forbidden site), behind a login wall that no reading, Capture or source re-check can pass, with recruiters' personal data. The hiring manager's name and email `active-ats` may state are never read (GDPR minimisation).
- **Cost.** Fantastic.jobs charges one Jobs credit per job returned and one API request per call, a hard limit on every plan (trial: 500 credits and 100 requests over 14 days; $45 a month: 5,000 and 2,500). One search asks for no more than `FANTASTIC_JOBS_MAX_PER_SEARCH` jobs (default 10), whatever the Job discovery budget has left: the trial then lasts ~50 searches rather than ~16. Its estimated cost (credits charged × `FANTASTIC_JOBS_EUR_PER_JOB`, default €0.008, the $45 plan) is shown in the Fantastic.jobs status and the external APIs' AI Usage, apart from the AI calls' cost.
- **Quota guard.** The worker keeps the last quotas an answer stated (`x-api-jobs-remaining`, `x-api-requests-remaining`). Once either is spent, or Fantastic.jobs answered 402 or 429, no call is made and the status is refused until the quota is renewed: the next billing date it stated, the wait a 429 asked for, else a day. A search never asks for more jobs than the credits left. The guard lives in the worker's memory: a restart or a key replaced reads the quotas again, at the cost of one call.
- **Connection test.** It searches `active-ats` for a job that cannot exist (`id=0`), which uses no Jobs credit but one API request: only when a super-admin saves or tests the key, never on a schedule. Tests and CI use the captured fixture only.

## Considered Options

- **Importing a feed** (all French postings, then matching locally): what Fantastic.jobs advises at scale, but France alone posts ~4,000 jobs a day (~120,000 a month), out of the trial and ≈ $300 a month at overage price. Live search costs per Job Search; revisit once volume makes a feed cheaper per Job Offer.
- **Letting the Job discovery budget alone set the limit:** up to 30 credits a run, the trial spent in ~16 searches.
- **Calling until Fantastic.jobs refuses:** the API requests are a hard limit, so each run past it would spend a call to be turned away, and a 402 or 429 says nothing new until the quota is renewed.
- **More ATS public APIs read one by one:** each ATS is another integration to keep up; Fantastic.jobs covers 58 behind one key, for a price.
