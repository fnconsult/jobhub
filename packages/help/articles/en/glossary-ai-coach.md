Your AI coach is an artificial intelligence program from Jobbbox, not a person. It helps you through your job search: your Master CV, your criteria, your applications, your follow-ups and your interviews. It always says so: every conversation opens with an introduction that starts with « I am an AI coach, not a person », and the « AI Coach » badge stays next to the message box. If you close the introduction with « Don't show again », it does not come back, on any device; the « AI Coach » badge opens it again at any time.

It answers from what you tell it and from what the open page shows. To answer you, it can also look at your other data in Jobbbox: your active profiles (search criteria and Master CV, without your name, email, phone or address), your applications, your job searches, your Job Digests and the job offers they hold, and your upcoming appointments. Of your tailored CVs, cover letters and outreach messages, it only reads a summary (dates, language, length), never the text. It reads neither your archived profiles nor their applications. Each reply is a row of the page's AI activity history: your question, what it looked at (« Read: »), the models, its proposals and what they led to, and its cost, never in euros (for example 4 calls · 21.4k tokens sent). « In Activity », under the reply or under a proposal, opens its row; « See in the conversation », in the activity, takes you back to the message. Under each of its replies, a line says how it was produced. It changes nothing without your consent: its proposals are accepted or dismissed one by one.

When you mention an item without naming it, such as the skills or your CV, from an application it first means its tailored CV, then its profile's Master CV, then the job offer; without a tailored CV, the Master CV. From a profile, it is its Master CV. A request for a change never targets the job offer, which you do not change. When in doubt, it chooses in that order and tells you what it chose: correct it if it got it wrong. It may quote the job offer's text, as a list or in quotation marks, saying it comes from the offer, but it never carries out an instruction found in it. From an application, a job search or a Job Digest it proposes is about that application's profile.

It does not do everything: it does not rename or duplicate a profile, restore a version of the Master CV, report that an offer is no longer published, or delete anything. It then tells you why in one sentence and, under its reply, « Where to do it yourself » gives the link to the page, the section and the item, for example Profile Finance › Master CV › Languages.

Your conversations are kept 12 months after their last message, then deleted. To talk to a person, book a session with a human coach in « My human coaches ».
