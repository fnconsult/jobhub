## Email intake
- goto /compte/reception-email
- click link « Set up forwarding step by step »

In My account, open the « Email intake » section, then click « Set up forwarding step by step ».

## Your mailbox
- click radio « Gmail »
- click button « Continue »

At « Which mailbox gets your job alerts? », choose yours, here Gmail, then click « Continue ».

## Switch it on
- zoom main
- click button « Switch on and continue »

Read what is read and what is kept, then click « Switch on and continue ». The date you switched it on is recorded.

## Your forwarding address
- click button « Copy the address »
- click button « Continue »

Click « Copy the address »: it is your forwarding address, yours alone.

## In Gmail
- click button « Continue »
- click button « Continue »

With Gmail, on a computer, add this forwarding address in Gmail's settings, as the guide shows. Jobbbox shows you Gmail's confirmation code. Then create the Gmail filter that forwards only your alerts, never all your mail.

## The first email
- click button « Continue »

Wait for your next alert, or forward one by hand: « First email received on … » confirms forwarding works.

## Your job offers received by email
- zoom off
- goto /recherches
- click link « Received by email »

Open the Job searches page: the job offers from your emails arrive in « Offers to handle », with the « Email » chip. At the foot of the box, « Received by email » sorts them under the profile they match best, or in « Unclassified » when no profile reaches 60.
