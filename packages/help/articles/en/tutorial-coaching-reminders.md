synonyms: [disable morning summary, coach notification settings]

When the new reminders are activated:

1. Open **Coach space → Reminders**. Clear “Receive my summary at 07:30 in my time zone” to stop the morning summary. Your choice saves immediately.
2. Set session emails and each app notification event separately. Also allow Jobbbox notifications in your device settings to receive them.

The summary follows your time zone and contains no Candidate private notes. Cancellation and refund messages remain independent of these settings.
