title: What do I do when the extension says « Update the extension »?
synonyms: [update, new version, old version, outdated, extension, chrome web store, store, no longer supported]
---
Your version of the Jobbbox extension is too old: Jobbbox no longer supports it. The extension's panel says so with « Update the extension ».

Click « Open the extension's page »: its page on your browser's store opens, where you install the latest version. Your browser also updates the extension by itself, usually within a few hours. Once it is up to date, click « Try again ».

The job offer and CV of your current session stay in your browser during the update: you have nothing to redo.
