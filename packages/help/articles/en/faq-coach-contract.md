Before using your workspace, read “Contract”, tick “I have read and accept the Coach agreement” with the displayed version, then choose “Accept agreement”. Application approval does not replace acceptance. If no agreement is available yet, wait for its publication.

A substantial change is announced by email at least 30 days before it takes effect. From that date, you must accept the new version to continue. A minor correction does not require acceptance again. The French wording is binding; English is its translation.

“Contract” keeps your acceptances with their dates and links to each version. Upcoming versions announced by email can be read in your workspace. An authorised Association member accepts for their Association; this does not replace each coach’s agreement. The agreement and its versions are private pages.
