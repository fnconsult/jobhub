An exchange is a recruiter's email about one of your applications (their reply, an invitation to an interview), received by your linked mailbox and kept in that application's "Exchanges" section: the sender, the date, the subject and the text, never attachments. It is filed there by rules, without AI (the employer's domain, an enriched contact, a reply to an exchange already kept); when in doubt, Jobbbox asks you "Link to …?" and files nothing without your click.

It is kept as long as the application exists, you can delete it, and it is in your data export. It never changes the application's status. Your AI coach can read it and propose a reply, which you copy or open in your email: Jobbbox sends nothing. It is not "Match", which details your match score.
