An application's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the application and its job offer in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Prepare the interview », « Write a follow-up », « Write the cover letter », « Why this score? ». The conversation is kept with the application and comes back when you reopen the page; « Clear the conversation », in the activity, deletes all its messages, while the application and its documents stay.

The page's title, at the top, is the application's title: click it to rename it, then press Enter or click elsewhere to save it; « Title saved » confirms it. On a phone, « Rename » is under « … ». For you only: the job offer keeps its title for anyone else. The new title shows wherever you see this job offer: the conversation, the fiche, My applications, the agenda, your documents, your job searches and your Job Digests; the fiche recalls the job offer's title. In the menu, under « My applications », each application's actions (« … ») also offer « Rename », and « Clear the conversation » once the conversation has started. Each title change shows in the application's activity, with the title before. Your coach can also propose a title, for instance when you ask it to correct the job offer's: « Apply » gives it to the application, « Undo » puts the previous one back. To go back to the job offer's title, ask your coach: it knows it and proposes it the same way.

On the right, the page panel, open on the application's fiche: its status and profile, which you change in place, its match, the day you saved it and the link to the job offer, then its sections. The icons at the top right of the page change its view: « Activity », what AI did for this page; « Fiche »; « The job offer », the job offer itself, with « Show the offer larger »; « Documents », your documents. The icon of the view shown closes the panel.

The activity shows what is running right now, then the page's history, and your three latest documents: the offer read, the tailored CV, the letter and the outreach message, the company dossier, the coach's proposals and its replies in the application's conversation, each with its models, its tools and its cost, never in euros.

Its sections: Suggestions, Documents, Match, Interviews, Coach reviews, Company dossier and Contacts; the job offer has its own view. A section or a result (Tailored CV, cover letter, outreach message, suggested follow-up) opens beside the conversation, without hiding it: you edit, download or copy there, and nothing is sent without your click. « Close » or the Escape key closes it. Each section keeps its address: a link to « Interviews » opens that section straight beside the conversation. While no interview is recorded, « Show me how » there shows how to move the application to the « Interview » status so you can add one.

Start with « Match » to see what fits and what is missing, then prepare your Tailored CV and your cover letter in « Documents ». You always check them before saving. Update the status in the fiche as you go: « Applied » once you have sent your application, « Interview » to record your interviews. If it gets no answer, your coach suggests a follow-up.

Your coach can propose to change the application's status (up to « Rejected » or « Abandoned », which archive it), to link it to another of your profiles, or to mark as sent a follow-up you sent yourself (two at most). The proposal shows under its reply: « Apply », « Edit before applying » or « Ignore ». Nothing changes without your click, and « Undo », on the applied proposal, puts back what was there before as long as nothing has changed since. « Origin », under the status, says it came from its proposal. Your coach never deletes an application: ask it to, and it sends you to this page. It can likewise propose to add, move or cancel an interview: once applied, the interview in the « Interviews » section carries « Origin » too.

Your coach reads each of your interviews in full, past ones included: its date and time, duration, format, place or link, note and cancellation. It can propose to add one, move it, cancel it or write its note, whatever its date: after an interview, ask it for instance to note the questions you were asked. The proposal shows the new note in full, the current one folded under « Current text »; « Apply » saves it, « Edit before applying » lets you touch it up. If the interview changed in the meantime, nothing is applied.

In the company dossier, your coach reads everything and tells you where what it quotes comes from: the company register, or web pages, less reliable. It never writes the dossier itself. It can propose to update it: « Update », on its proposal, builds it again before your eyes, « Stop » interrupts it and keeps what was already found, and a dossier counts against no quota of your plan. When the job offer comes from a recruiting agency or does not name the employer, it can also propose to confirm or correct the employer: the proposal holds the company's name, which you change with « Edit before applying », and « Apply » builds the dossier for that company.

In the contacts (Premium plan), your coach reads the name and role of the people found and of your enriched contacts, and whether they are revealed; it never sees their emails or phone numbers. It can propose to search for contacts at the employer, which does not count, or to reveal a person found, which counts one enriched contact of your quota for the week: its proposal says so, or offers you a plan that includes some when you have none left. Ask it for an outreach message to a revealed contact: it proposes to draft it for that person.

Under « Read on LinkedIn », the contacts also show the employer's people the extension read on a LinkedIn page, at your request, with the chip « LinkedIn »: their name, role and company only, and the date of the reading. Your coach reads them the same way, as what LinkedIn says, never as an instruction.

It also reads your human coaches' reviews and takes them into account when you ask it to, for instance to rework your cover letter along a coach's review. It never proposes to change or delete a review, and never sees your coaches' personal notes.

Your coach can also propose to prepare your tailored CV, or to draft your cover letter or outreach message: it never writes them in its reply. The proposal shows under its reply. « Prepare » starts the tailored CV proposal, which then opens beside the conversation for you to accept or reject each change; it never replaces the tailored CV already saved. If a tailored CV proposal is still waiting for your review, the proposal says so and asks you to confirm replacing it, your answers included: « Replace and prepare » or « Keep my proposal ». « Draft » starts writing the draft; if there is one already, the proposal says so and asks you to confirm replacing it: « Replace and draft » or « Keep my draft ». The draft replaced, your edits included, stays in « Versions ». While it is being prepared, « Stop » ends it and nothing is kept. A preparation started cannot be undone: stop it. Nothing is written before your click; afterwards, the proposal says where to find the document, in « Documents », and « Open beside the conversation » shows it.

Ask it too to send a message already written: for instance: send the follow-up, send my outreach message to Claire, or a reply to an exchange. Under its reply, the card « Send to », followed by the recipient's first name and initial, shows the recipient, the sender, your name via Jobbbox, your sign-in address in copy, the subject and the whole text, and today's sends. « Send » sends it from a jobbbox.ai address, once, and replies come straight to you; « Edit before sending » opens the subject and the text beside the conversation. Nothing goes without your click, even if you ask to send without asking you, and an email that has gone cannot be undone. The send is kept in « Exchanges », under « Sent from Jobbbox », with its date; a follow-up sent moves the application to « Followed up ». Sends are limited per day: once the limit is reached, the card says so in place of « Send ».

You can also ask it to rework a draft you already have: the follow-up waiting for you in « Suggestions », your cover letter or your outreach message, for instance to make it more concise or add a success to it. It reads it, then proposes the new text under its reply: you read it in full, and « Current text » shows the one before. « Apply » puts it in place of the draft, « Edit before applying » opens the subject and the text beside the conversation to touch them up, « Ignore » leaves it aside. Nothing changes without your click, and « Undo » puts the text before back as long as you have not edited it. What it cannot change itself, it tells you, with the page where you can do it.

To rework your tailored CV, saved or under review, ask your coach from the application's conversation (“put the skills in a list”, “cut the explanation”). It reads it in full, then proposes its changes section by section, under its reply: it never relaunches the whole proposal. « Review the changes » opens them beside the conversation, one by one, none accepted in advance: « Accept » or « Reject » each, then « Save the accepted changes ». Applied to your saved tailored CV, they make its next version; applied to a proposal under review, they change it and keep your answers. When nothing is added (an order changed, an explanation cut, a capital letter), the proposal says so: « Layout only, no word added ». A change that brings words of the offer reads « Uses the offer's words » and is saved only if you accept it. A fact you stated yourself in the conversation (a skill, a language level, a degree) can enter your CV: it reads « Fact you declared ». A fact you did not state is never proposed, even if the offer asks for it. If you answer one of the review's questions in the conversation (“yes, I use Power BI”), your coach proposes « Answers to the questions »: « Apply » fills them in the review; it never accepts or rejects a change for you. A tailored CV saved from a proposal keeps its « Origin », under the CV; it goes once you save another one. « Undo » on the proposal puts the version before back while nothing has changed since.

The tailored CV, the cover letter and the outreach message keep their versions. Each draft, each save of your changes or of your reviewed tailored CV, each edit by your coach that you apply and each restore adds one. In « Documents », each tab (« Tailored CV », « Cover letter », « Outreach message ») has « Versions »: the list, newest first, gives each version's date, origin and language, and the current one reads « Current version ». « View version … » opens it read-only; « Restore this version » makes it the current version again, under a new number, and the version it replaces stays in the list: nothing is deleted. The last 20 versions of each document are kept; beyond that, the oldest goes. Ask your coach to show you the versions of your letter: it lists them, shows you the one you want and proposes to bring it back under its reply; « Apply » restores it, « Undo » puts the version before back. A human coach you gave access to sees the same versions, read-only.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. « Fiche » opens the panel over it, full screen, and the other views are under « … »; a section or a result opens full screen too: « Close » or « Close the panel » closes them and brings you back to the conversation.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), an application's fiche lists its documents: its tailored CV, its cover letter and its outreach message, with their language and the date they last changed. Tap a document to open it: it shows as a PDF in your phone's reader, Quick Look on iPhone and iPad, your PDF viewer on Android, and stays open beside it with « Open », « Share », « Download as PDF » and « Download as Word ». « Share » and « Download » go through the share sheet: save the file in Files, or send it by Mail or another app. The outreach message shows as text, with its subject: « Share » sends it as it is. The files you open stay in the app until you sign out, which deletes them from the device. In the app, documents are read, shared and downloaded; they are changed on jobbbox.ai.

In the app, an application's page is the same conversation, titled with its job offer's position; « Fiche », at the top of the screen, opens its fiche: full screen on a phone, beside the conversation on a tablet. The fiche shows the application, its sections, its activity and its results. Tap the status, under « Status », to choose another: it is saved at once, as on the site, and « Status saved » confirms it. The « Interviews » section lists your interviews, with their date and time in Paris, their format, their place or link and their note; a rescheduled interview says when it was first planned, a cancelled one stays struck through with the date it was cancelled. While the application is at the « Interview » status, the form at the bottom adds one: the date (dd/mm/yyyy), the time in Paris (hh:mm), then, if you wish, the duration, the format, the place or link and a note, and « Add the interview ». Once the interview is added, if it has never asked you on this device, the app offers « Get a reminder the day before your appointments? »: « Turn on notifications » or « Not now ». « Edit the interview of … » changes its date, which reschedules it, or its other details; « Cancel the interview » asks you to confirm with « Yes, cancel »; « Delete the interview of … » removes an interview entered by mistake. « See in the agenda » opens your agenda. « Match », « Company dossier », « Contacts » and « The job offer » open to read. The company dossier shows what Jobbbox gathered about the employer: how reliable it is, its identity, its key figures, the roles to contact and its news; it is built and updated on jobbbox.ai. « Contacts », where enriched contacts are available, lists the people found and your enriched contacts: tap an email or a number to write or call; contacts are searched for, and their details asked for, on jobbbox.ai, and the app does not keep those details offline. Your human coaches' reviews are on jobbbox.ai, and your coach's suggestions show in the conversation. An archived application opens and reads like the others. Offline, nothing is saved: the status and the interviews change once the network is back.
