The agenda shows your interviews and your sessions with a human coach. Show it as a list (upcoming appointments, day by day), by week or by month.

Add an interview with « Add an interview », or from the « Interviews » section of an application at the « Interview » stage.

Past appointments stay visible, dimmed. The day before at 6 pm, Jobbbox can email you a reminder: set it in My account, under Reminders.

For 14 days after you pay for a session with a human coach, and as long as it has not taken place, « Renoncer au contrat ici » (withdraw from the contract here) cancels it and refunds it in full; the coach is told.

With the new layout, the agenda is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one every visit. The list is a card there: your upcoming appointments day by day, and the sessions « To schedule ». « Week » and « Month » open the calendar beside the conversation; « Add an interview » opens its form there, which closes once the interview is added. Ask the coach to prepare an interview or to remind you what is coming.
