title: Coach memory

The coach's memory is the key facts your AI coach remembers about you from one conversation to the next: your goals, your constraints, your decisions. Nothing goes into it without your consent. When your coach proposes to remember a fact, it does so on a « To remember » card under its reply, one fact per card: « Apply » remembers it, « Edit before applying » lets you change its type or words, « Ignore » sets it aside, and « Undo » forgets it as long as you have not edited it. Where these cards are not offered yet, « What should my coach remember from this conversation? » has it suggest items, and only those you confirm with « Remember » are kept. It holds 30 items of 300 characters at most.

« Coach memory » is under « Coach » in the menu: it opens beside the conversation, where you can edit or delete each item. « Close » or the Escape key closes it.

Clearing a conversation leaves the coach's memory untouched. It is deleted with your account.
