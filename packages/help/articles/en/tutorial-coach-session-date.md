A linked Cal.com booking automatically dates the session and Agenda. To move it, use “Change the booking in Cal.com”: the manual form cannot change either its date or duration. For a session without a linked booking:

1. Open “My sessions”, then the relevant session. Choose “Enter date” or “Edit date”.
2. In “Session date and time (Paris time)”, enter the agreed Paris time even if your display uses another zone. Check the duration in minutes, then select “Save”.
3. Set “This session’s contact” separately if its HTTPS link or phone differs from your permanent profile contact. Choose who calls for a phone contact.

The Candidate finds the date in their Agenda, with the author and rescheduling history. The date drawer preserves other fields, including a contact changed while it was open. “Cancel” closes it without saving. You never see the Candidate’s personal note. The session phase, call access and Shared Reading update automatically at their time boundaries.
