The fiche is one of the views of the page panel, on the right of the conversation on the page of an application, a profile, a job search or a Job Digest: the object at a glance (for an application, its status and profile, which you change in place, its match, the job offer), then its sections.

The icons at the top right of the page show the views of this panel: « Activity », what AI did for the page; « Fiche »; « The job offer », an application's job offer; « Documents », its results, or « Job offers found » for a job search or a Job Digest. The icon of the view shown is highlighted; a click on it closes the panel. With the keyboard, the left and right arrows move from one icon to the next, and Alt+Shift followed by the view's letter (A, F, O or D) shows or closes it, wherever you are.

The panel never hides the conversation: it pushes it aside, and scrolls on its own, staying put when you scroll the conversation. At its top, it names its view; « Close the panel » closes it. « … », beside the icons, also offers « Default panel width » and « Close the panel ». A section or a result opens beside the conversation, where you act on it.

Too narrow for what it shows? Widen the panel: drag its left edge, the handle between the conversation and the panel, to the left. It widens up to half the window, always leaving the conversation enough room. A click on the handle widens it to the maximum at once, another brings it back; with the keyboard, move to the handle with Tab, then use the left and right arrows, or Home and End. A double-click gives it back its original width. Your browser remembers the width you chose.

On a phone, « Fiche » is the icon at the top right of the page, and the other views are under « … »; the panel opens over the conversation, full screen, with its title; « Close the panel » or the Escape key closes it.
