title: Protecting the app with Face ID
synonyms: [lock, app lock, Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, passcode, code, biometrics, unlock, security, phone, tablet, application]

In the Jobbbox app, open the menu, then « App settings » below « My account », and turn on « Lock the app ». The app asks for Face ID, your fingerprint or the device's code at once, to check that you will be able to unlock it.

Once the lock is on, the app locks when it stays 5 minutes or more in the background, and each time it starts. To come back, pass Face ID or your fingerprint; after several failures, the device's code takes over. If you cancel, tap « Unlock » to try again. While the lock is on, the app's content is hidden in the app switcher; on Android, screenshots of the app are blocked too.

This setting applies to this device only: it is not on the website nor on another phone, and nothing is sent to Jobbbox. It is not Two-step verification: your sign-in does not change. Your face and fingerprint are checked by the device's system (iOS or Android); Jobbbox receives and keeps nothing of them.

If the device has no Face ID, fingerprint or code, the lock cannot be turned on: set one up first in the device's settings.
