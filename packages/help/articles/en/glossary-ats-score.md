title: ATS Score
synonyms: [ats, applicant tracking system, recruitment software, robot, readability, keywords]

Recruiters use applicant tracking systems (ATS) that read your CV before a person does. The ATS Score, out of 100, says whether this software will read your Master CV well for the role you are aiming for.

It combines readability (contact details, headline, dated jobs…) and the role's keywords. It is worked out without AI, by fixed rules, and does not depend on any job offer.

The fixes to improve it come from your coach: you find them in the profile's Suggestions.
