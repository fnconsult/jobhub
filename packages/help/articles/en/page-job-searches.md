« Job searches » is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one every visit. There your coach searches for job offers that meet a profile's search criteria.

Your searches are listed there in a card, by profile: when each was started, where it stands and how many job offers it found; « New results » marks those you have not seen yet. Click a line to open the search, which you can stop while it is searching. To start one, use the « Start a search » suggestion above the message box, one per profile if you have several. « See the job offers received by email » opens them beside the conversation.

Each search counts once against your plan's quota. If it fails, or if you stop it before it has found a job offer, it does not count.

In the menu, « Job searches » unfolds into your most recent searches, those with results not seen yet marked with a dot; « See all » leads to this page.
