In « Offers to handle », importance sorts job offers into three tiers, from their Match Score: « Not to miss » (80 and above), « Worth a look » (60 to 79) and « Less suited » (under 60, or not computable). Within each tier, the job offers that arrived most recently come first.

A « Not to miss » job offer still to handle has a border turning slowly around it; it stays still when your device asks for reduced motion. Importance is worked out without AI, by the Match Score's rules.
