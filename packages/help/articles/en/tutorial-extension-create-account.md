Without an account, your CV and the job offer are deleted at the latest 24 hours after your first analysis, or as soon as you close your browser. By creating your account, you keep them: your CV becomes your first profile, and the job offer your first application.

1. In the extension's panel, once your Match Score is computed, click « Create my account and keep this job offer ». Jobbbox opens in a new tab.
2. Enter « Your email address », then click « Send me a sign-in link ».
3. Open the email « Your Jobbbox sign-in link » and click « Sign in to Jobbbox », within 15 minutes: your account is created, without a password.
4. If Jobbbox asks you to accept the Terms of use, read them, tick the box then click « Accept and continue ».
5. Go back to the job offer's tab: the extension's panel updates by itself. Your CV has become your profile, and the job offer is saved in your applications, as “To apply”.
6. Click « See my application » to open it in Jobbbox.

If you already have an account, the same button signs you in to your account, with the same link sent by email; before the score is computed, « I already have an account · Sign in » leads to the same page. The tutorial « Save a job offer with the extension » explains what comes next.
