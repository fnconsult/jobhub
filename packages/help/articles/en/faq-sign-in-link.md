Jobbbox has no password. On the « Sign in to Jobbbox » page, enter « Your email address » then click « Send me a sign-in link ». You receive the email « Your Jobbbox sign-in link »: its button « Sign in to Jobbbox » signs you in. If you don't have an account yet, it is created then.

The email also holds a 6-digit code, to sign in on another device than the one you read it on: type it in « Enter the code from the email », on the page waiting for the link or in the app, then confirm with « Sign in with this code ».

The link and the code are valid for 15 minutes and work only once: using one makes the other unusable. A link that has expired or was already used shows « This sign-in link is no longer valid. Ask for a new one below. » After 5 wrong codes, the code no longer works: ask for a new email, which brings a new code.

Depending on the options on the page, « Continue with … » also signs you in with another account you already have, without waiting for an email.

The email doesn't arrive?
- Wait a few minutes: it can take a little while.
- Look in your junk mail (spam) or promotions.
- Check the address you entered, then ask for a new link.

Still nothing? See « How do I contact you? ».
