title: How do I get a contact's details at the employer?
synonyms: [contacts, enriched contacts, contact details, email, phone, recruiter, hr, hr director, manager, find a contact, lusha, kaspr, apollo]

In an application's « Contacts » section, Jobbbox finds the people in the roles to contact at the employer, then gets their contact details from a licensed data provider. Build the company dossier first: it gives the roles to look for.

1. Click « Find contacts »: the « People found » show.
2. For the person you choose, click « Get contact details ». Each request counts as one Enriched Contact in your quota for the week; nothing is counted if the provider has none.
3. Their contact details join « Your Enriched Contacts », with their source and date.

This personal data comes from a licensed provider: use it only for this application. You can address your outreach message to one of these contacts, from the « Documents » section.
