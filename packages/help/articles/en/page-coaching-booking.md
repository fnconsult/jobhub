This page belongs to the person who paid for the session. Choose a time in the coach’s calendar or “Choose later”. Cal.com confirmation dates the session in your Agenda. For an already linked booking, “Change the booking in Cal.com” keeps both calendars synchronized. The integration stays closed until its activation conditions are met.
