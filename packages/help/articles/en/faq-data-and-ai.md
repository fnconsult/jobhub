title: What does Jobbbox do with my data, and with AI?

Jobbbox uses what you entrust to it, your CV, your criteria and your applications, to help you with your job search. Your coach and several tasks, such as reading your CV or working out a match score, use AI; your coach always says it is an AI. The help, for its part, uses no AI.

With your consent, in My account, under European AI, each request to the AI that holds your identity data is handled by Mistral AI, a European AI provider. You can withdraw this consent at any time.

In My account, under My data, you download all your data or delete your account.

The details are in the privacy policy, section [AI in Jobbbox](/legal/confidentialite#ia).
