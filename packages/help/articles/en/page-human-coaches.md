« My human coaches » is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one every visit. Your sessions and the human coaches are two cards there. Ask the AI coach how to prepare a session.

On top of your AI coach, you can book a video session with an HR professional selected by Jobbbox. « Book a session with … » first opens the Your session order page: the summary, the box to accept the terms of sale and the request for the service to start at once. Pay for the session, then pick your time.

With your consent, a coach can read your profiles and applications, and comment on your application documents. They cannot change anything, and you can withdraw this access at any time.

A dated session offers « See in the agenda », « Change the date » and « Cancel the session ». Once it has ended (its start time plus its duration, one hour if you did not give it), it stays in the list with its date, but can no longer be changed or cancelled. If it ends while the page is open, a message tells you: « This session is over: it can no longer be changed or cancelled. »

In the Jobbbox app, « My human coaches » shows your paid sessions, with their date or « No date yet », then the coaches to book, with what a session costs you. « Book a session with … » opens your order, as on the website: tick the terms of sale box, then « Go to payment ». The payment opens on Stripe's secure page, without leaving the app. Once you have paid, you are back in the app: the session shows as soon as Stripe has confirmed the payment, which usually takes a few seconds, with « Pick my time with … on Cal.com ». If the confirmation is slow, « Refresh » checks again; your order confirmation is also sent to you by email.

Once you have booked your time, « I've booked: enter the date » asks for the date, the time in Paris, the duration, the video call link and a note, as on the website; « Change the date » corrects them, as long as the session has not ended. « Withdraw from the contract here » opens jobbbox.ai's withdrawal page in the app, as long as the session can still be withdrawn from. The app shows no plan price and no plan purchase: your subscription is managed on jobbbox.ai.
