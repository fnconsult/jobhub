Your AI coach is an artificial intelligence program from Jobbbox, not a person. It helps you through your job search: your Master CV, your criteria, your applications, your follow-ups and your interviews. It always says so: every conversation opens with an introduction that starts with « I am an AI coach, not a person », and the « AI Coach » badge stays next to the message box. If you close the introduction with « Don't show again », it does not come back, on any device; the « AI Coach » badge opens it again at any time.

It answers from what you tell it and from what the open page shows. To answer you, it can also look at your other data in Jobbbox: your active profiles (search criteria and Master CV, without your name, email, phone or address), your applications, your job searches, your Job Digests and the job offers they hold, and your upcoming appointments. Of your tailored CVs, cover letters and outreach messages, it only reads a summary (dates, language, length), never the text. It reads neither your archived profiles nor their applications. Each reply is a row of the page's AI activity history: your question, what it looked at (« Read: … »), the models, its proposals and what they led to, and its cost, never in euros (« 4 calls · 21.4k tokens sent »). « In Activity », under the reply or under a proposal, opens its row; « See in the conversation », in the activity, takes you back to the message. Under each of its replies, a line says how it was produced. It changes nothing without your consent: its proposals are accepted or dismissed one by one.

Your conversations are kept 12 months after their last message, then deleted. To talk to a person, book a session with a human coach in « My human coaches ».
