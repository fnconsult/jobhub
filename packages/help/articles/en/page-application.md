An application's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the application and its job offer in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Prepare the interview », « Write a follow-up », « Write the cover letter », « Why this score? ». The conversation is kept with the application and comes back when you reopen the page; « Clear », at the top of the page, deletes all its messages, while the application and its documents stay.

On the right, the application's fiche: its status and profile, which you change in place, its match, the day you saved it and the link to the job offer, then three groups you fold with a click on their title, « Sections », « Activity » and « Results ». « Fiche », at the top of the page, opens or closes it; « Activity » opens it on what AI did for this page.

Its sections: Suggestions, Documents, Match, Interviews, Coach reviews, Company dossier, Contacts and The job offer. A section or a result (Tailored CV, cover letter, outreach message, suggested follow-up) opens beside the conversation, without hiding it: you edit, download or copy there, and nothing is sent without your click. « Close » or the Escape key closes it. Each section keeps its address: a link to « Interviews » opens that section straight beside the conversation.

Start with « Match » to see what fits and what is missing, then prepare your Tailored CV and your cover letter in « Documents ». You always check them before saving. Update the status in the fiche as you go: « Applied » once you have sent your application, « Interview » to record your interviews. If it gets no answer, your coach suggests a follow-up.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. « Fiche » opens the fiche over it, full screen, and so does a section or a result: « Close » closes them and brings you back to the conversation.
