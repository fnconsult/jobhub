A Job Digest is a selection of new job offers matching a profile's search criteria. You get it by email and find it on this page too.

For each profile, choose whether to receive it and how often, within your plan: every week on Monday at 7 am, or every day at 7 am (Paris time).

Only job offers you have not seen yet are in it. When there are none, nothing is sent.

With the new layout, « Job Digests » is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one every visit. Your Job Digests are listed there in a card, by profile: how often it comes, which you set there, then each Job Digest sent, with its date and its number of job offers. Click a line to open it, or ask the coach which job offers to look at first.

In the menu, « Job Digests » unfolds into your most recent Job Digests; « See all » leads to this page.
