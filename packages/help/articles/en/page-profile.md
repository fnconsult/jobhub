A profile's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the profile's Master CV and search criteria for one role in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Improve my CV », « Understand my ATS Score », « Refine my criteria », « Which companies to target? ». The conversation is kept with the profile and comes back when you reopen the page; « Clear », at the top of the page, deletes all its messages, while the profile and its CV stay.

On the right, the profile's fiche: whether it is active or archived, « Search for offers », its search criteria, which you change in place, and its ATS Score, then three groups you fold with a click on their title, « Sections », « Activity » and « Results ». A profile's results are the versions of its Master CV, newest first, and its download as PDF or Word. « Fiche », at the top of the page, opens or closes it.

Its sections: Suggestions, as long as your coach has fixes to propose, then Master CV, ATS Score, Target companies, Download and Manage. A section, a version of the Master CV, its history or the full CV editor opens beside the conversation, without hiding it. « Close » or the Escape key closes it. Each section keeps its address.

Start with the ATS Score: it says whether recruitment software will read your CV well. The fixes your coach proposes appear in « Suggestions »; accept or dismiss them one by one. Each time you save the Master CV, a new version is created, and you can go back to an earlier one. « Download » exports the Master CV as PDF or Word. « Manage » lets you rename, duplicate or archive the profile.

« Target companies » lists the companies the job search aims at for this profile: those La Bonne Boite gives as likely to hire for your job and area, with their hiring potential, and those you add, marked « Added by you ». To add one, enter its name or SIREN in « Add a company »: it comes before La Bonne Boite's. Each company shows the job offers found there; « Company dossier », « Enriched contacts » and « Prepare a spontaneous application » open a spontaneous application, added to your applications as « To apply ». « Set aside » removes a company for good: it will not come back for this profile.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. « Fiche » opens the fiche over it, full screen, and so does a section: « Close » closes them and brings you back to the conversation.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), your profiles are in My account, under « Profiles », to read but not to change. A profile's fiche sums up its positioning, its search criteria, the current version of its Master CV and its ATS Score. To change anything, « Edit the profile » opens the profile's page on jobbbox.ai inside the app: full screen on a phone, beside the conversation on a tablet; « Close » brings you back to the fiche.
