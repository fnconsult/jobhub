title: Manage Coaching Offers and Commission
synonyms: [vendeur, seller, commission, coaching offer]
---
1. In Human Coaches, “Coaching Offers and Sellers” lets you add a third-party Seller with their legal name, status and SIRET. For an independent coach, select that coach; for an Association, leave the coach empty. This does not activate the Seller.
2. For each offer, choose its Seller, duration and price in EUR cents. Jobbbox retains its central session price and Plan discount; the entered price does not replace them. Choose “Save offer”. Third-party offers remain unavailable until payments and legal conditions are activated.
3. Set the Seller’s Commission in basis points: 100 means 1%. “Save Commission” records the change in the audit. Confirmed payments retain their original Seller, price and Commission.
