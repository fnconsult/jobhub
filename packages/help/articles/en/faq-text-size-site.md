title: How do I make the site's text larger?
synonyms: [text size, font, larger, bigger, small, large print, zoom, read, readability, visually impaired]
---
At the top of every public page, the « Text size » list offers Small, Standard, Medium and Large. The whole page's text changes at once.

Your choice is kept on this device, signed in or not: once signed in, you find it in My account, under Preferences. On another computer or phone, set it again.

Your browser's zoom also makes the page larger: Ctrl and + on a computer (Cmd and + on a Mac).
