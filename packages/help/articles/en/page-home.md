The home page is where you start in Jobbbox. The menu leads to your applications, your agenda, your job searches and your Job Digests; « My account » is at the foot of the menu.

To begin, create a profile: your Master CV and your search criteria. Your coach uses them to search for job offers and prepare your applications.

A question? Open « Help and coach » at the top of the page.
