title: Why did I get an email about the terms?
synonyms: [email, mail, notice, terms, terms of use, terms of sale, privacy, new version, change, accept, privacy policy]
---
Jobbbox writes to you when it publishes a substantial version of the Terms of use, the Terms of sale or the Privacy policy: at least 30 days before it takes effect. The email names the text, the date it takes effect and what changes, with « Read the new version » and the « Version history ». A minor version, a typo corrected for instance, gets no email.

It is a service email: you get it even if you do not receive our news. It has no image and no tracking link, and you get it only once per version.

For the Terms of use, Jobbbox will ask you to accept the new version the first time you sign in from the date it takes effect. For the Terms of sale, it will apply to your next purchases. The Privacy policy is not accepted: after it takes effect, a notice « Our privacy policy changed on … » shows once in your workspace, and « Got it » closes it.
