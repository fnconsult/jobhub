title: What is coach access, and what happens when a candidate withdraws it?
synonyms: [coach access, access, consent, candidate's consent, withdraw access, access withdrawn, revoke access, candidate gone, I no longer see the candidate, read only, candidate's file, page not found]
---
Coach Access is the Candidate’s explicit permission to read their Profiles, Applications and documents, and send reviews without editing them. They grant or withdraw it in “My human coaches”; you cannot grant it on their behalf.

“My Candidates” includes people who granted access or have a session with you. Without Coach Access, their page keeps only their limited identity, your sessions and Private Coach Notes. A payment alone never opens their Profiles or Applications.

Withdrawal takes effect immediately: Profiles, Applications and review forms become unavailable. An unfinished review can no longer be sent. Reviews already sent remain with the Candidate. Paid sessions remain in “My sessions”, independently of Coach Access. Their Agenda, AI Coach conversations and personal notes stay private.
