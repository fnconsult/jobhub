« Edit my Master CV » opens the full editor beside the profile's conversation. Add, edit, move or remove the entries of each section: your contact details, your jobs, your education, your skills and your languages. The editor says which version you are editing.

« Save as a new version » keeps your changes as the next version: earlier ones stay in the history and can be restored. « Cancel and go back to the profile » drops what you changed.

If your Master CV changed elsewhere in the meantime, for example by applying a fix your coach proposed, saving is refused: nothing is merged or silently lost. Reload the page to start again from the latest version.

From the profile's conversation or an application's, your coach can also propose to rephrase, cut, reorder or put forward what your CV already says. It changes nothing itself: « Review the changes » opens them one by one beside the conversation; none is accepted in advance: « Accept » or « Reject » each, and « Save the accepted changes » makes a single new version of the ones you accepted. A change that brings words from one of your job offers that your CV lacks reads « Uses the offer's words »: it is saved only if you accept it.

It never adds a fact your CV does not hold, unless you told it yourself in the conversation: a skill (“add Power BI, I have used it for 3 years”), a language or its level, a degree. That change reads « Fact you declared »: check it before you accept it. A fact you did not state, even one an offer asks for, is never proposed: your coach asks whether you have it, or points you to the section where you add it yourself. The version created this way keeps its « Origin », shown in the version history, and « Undo » on the card restores the version before.
