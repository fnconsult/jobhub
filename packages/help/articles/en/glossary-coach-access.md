title: A human coach's access
synonyms: [access, coach access, human coach, share, sharing, give access, withdraw access, who sees my data, coach reviews]
---
A human coach's access is your consent for a human coach to read your profiles and applications. With it, the coach can also comment on your application documents: their reviews arrive in the application's « Coach reviews » section. They cannot change anything.

You give it coach by coach, in « My human coaches »: « Give … access », then « Withdraw …'s access » whenever you want. Withdrawing takes effect at once; the reviews already received stay yours.

A human coach never sees your conversations with your AI coach, nor your coach memory. Without this access, they see only your sessions with them, not your profiles or applications.
