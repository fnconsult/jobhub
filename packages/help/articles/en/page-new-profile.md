A profile is a role you are aiming for: a Master CV and search criteria. You can have several, depending on your plan.

While you have no profile, Jobbbox brings you here as soon as you sign in: « Create my account », on Jobbbox's home page, leads here after the sign-in page, and both the home page and My account take you here. If Jobbbox asks you to accept the Terms of use, that comes first, on their own screen. « Later », above the message box, takes you to My account: Jobbbox no longer brings you back here until you next sign in. Once you have a profile, even one showing « Creation to finish », you are no longer taken here. For a coach, an association or an administrator, nothing changes: they arrive in My account.

The page is a conversation with your coach, like every other: it opens on its welcome panel (where it says it is an AI and what the page does; « Don't show again » closes it for good), and the message box, at the bottom, carries the « AI Coach » badge, which opens that panel again, and the « Send to the coach » button. If you have a CV, drop it in the « I have a CV » card: your coach reads it and proposes a Master CV. If not, click « Answer my coach's questions », above the message box: your AI coach leads the onboarding interview. There is no longer a blank form to fill in to start from scratch: without a CV, your coach's questions build your profile.

For your first profile, while none is finished, the page opens on a computer split in two from the first question: the conversation on the left, « Activity » on the right, until you leave it. The split moves like any panel's edge: drag its left edge, click it to go to its widest or narrowest, double-click it to go back to half, or, with the keyboard, focus it and use the left and right arrows, Home and End; « Default panel width », under « … » at the top of the page, sets it back to half. The width you choose here is kept for this screen only: the panel keeps its own elsewhere. On the right, before your profile is created, « Profile in progress » shows what it already holds, filled in as you answer: « CV reading » (« Not sent yet », « Reading… », read, or « No CV: your answers take its place »), « Target role » and « Locations », each « Not in your profile yet » until you have given or confirmed it. As soon as the first job search starts, it takes its place: its progress, then its job offers with their Match Score. On a phone, the page is not split: as soon as that search has found job offers that match you (a Match Score of 60 or more), a « Your first job offers » card shows in the conversation, with up to 3 offers, each with its title, its company, its location and its Match Score, and « See all job offers », which opens the search's page. If none reaches 60, there is no card, and your coach's message about the search stays. A screen reader announces your first job offers once only. For one more profile, when you already have a finished one (« Add a profile »), the page stays as it was: « Activity » opens only when a CV is read or the search starts, on a computer.

With a CV, your coach shows you what it reads, in « Activity », the panel to the right of the conversation: it tells you so in the conversation, and on a computer the panel opens by itself when the reading starts (on a phone it does not: the « AI activity » icon at the top of the page opens it full screen). While it reads, « Right now » shows « CV reading » and its step « Reading your profile ». Once the CV is read, the history, in the « Profile creation » row, shows under « CV reading » what it found, line by line: « Target role », « Experience in years », « Based in », « Skills », « Contract types » and « Desired salary ». What your CV does not say is marked « Not in your profile yet ». If your device asks for less motion, everything shows at once. Your coach does not ask again what your CV already says: it only has you confirm the target role and the place it read there (« Yes, … », or « No, rather… » to write your own), then asks what a CV does not say and what matters to find your job offers: the contract type, remote work and the minimum salary. When it can deduce one from your career, it suggests it. Each of these three questions can be skipped with « Skip this question »; the role and the place cannot: your profile is created as soon as you have confirmed them.

Without a CV, it asks one question at a time, first the role you are looking for and the city where you want to work. Your profile is then created, and your coach tells you what comes next: « Four parts are left: your criteria, your career, your skills and your introduction. » The criteria are the contract type, remote work, the salary and the sector; the career, your jobs and your degrees; the skills, your know-how and your languages; the introduction, your CV's title and a few sentences about you. In the conversation, a heading marks the start of each part, for example « Part two of four: your career »; it appears only once per part, not at each question, and a screen reader reads it as a heading. If an answer is too vague to use, it tells you so and asks again. When it can guess an answer, it suggests it with its reason: « Yes, … » approves it in one click, « No, rather… » lets you write your own. Some questions are answered by choosing an option, such as « Permanent (CDI) » or « Hybrid »; « Skip this question » skips the optional ones. Nothing enters your profile without your answer or your approval: a suggestion you do not approve is never saved. If the AI cannot ask the next question, your coach says so and goes on with its usual questions, without the AI, without asking again what you have already said. Those questions come in the same order: first the role you are looking for, then your city, and your profile is created as soon as you have given both; then your first and last names, which cannot be skipped, then your contact details and your career.

You can do this in several sittings. As soon as your profile is created, « Continue later », above the message box, saves your answers, whenever you choose, and takes you to the profile's page, which says « Creation to finish ». « Resume creating it » then brings you back to the interview, at the part where you stopped, not at the start: your coach picks up from what is saved and does not ask again what you have already said. The interview still counts forty questions at most, those before the break included; past them, you move on to the review.

Either way, your coach then says « Here is how I understand what you are looking for. Did I get it right? » and sums up your search in the « Your job search » card, in a few sentences. The key items are in bold: the target role, the contract types, the places, remote work, the minimum salary, your years of experience (counted from the dates of your jobs), your main skills and your latest roles. This summary is composed by Jobbbox from your profile, not by an AI: what you did not give is not in it.

If it is right, click « That looks right »: your profile is saved and your coach confirms it in the conversation. It offers you two ways on: « See my offers to handle », which opens Job searches on the job offers waiting for your decision, and « Continue to my profile », which opens its page. With the keyboard, the focus is on the first; Tab moves to the second. Under them, « For what comes next, the help shows you what Jobbbox can do. » opens your coach's help, the one behind the « AI Coach » badge: that is where, rather than here, Jobbbox shows you what else it does. If its first job search has started, your coach adds that your first job offers show in « Activity », at the end of that search: « In Activity », under its sentence, opens the panel on it. You do not have to wait for it: you can go on to your profile at any time. « See the details » opens your Master CV and search criteria beside the conversation: check them and correct what needs it, and the summary updates with your changes. « Save my profile », at the bottom of that pane, saves your profile too. Closed, the pane opens again with « See the details », with nothing lost. Nothing is added that you did not say or approve.

Would you rather say it in your own words? Click « Edit »: the card switches to « Editing » and the summary becomes a text you rewrite, as you would explain it to a recruiter (for example: permanent or fixed-term, in Paris rather than Lyon). « Cancel » leaves everything as it was. « Done » has your AI coach read your text and draw from it the changes to your search criteria: the target role, the contract types, the places, remote work, the minimum salary, the sectors and the domains. It shows them to you before changing anything, for example: + Fixed-term (CDD), − Lyon, + Paris (75). « Confirm these changes » applies them: the summary is composed again and your coach asks again whether it got it right; « Cancel » applies nothing. A skill, an experience or a job you held is not changed this way: use « See the details ». If your coach did not understand your text, it tells you so and your summary stays as it was. Your text itself is not saved: only the changes you confirm are, with your profile, at « That looks right ». This use of AI does not count in your plan.

When you answer the questions, with or without a CV, your profile is created as soon as you have given or confirmed the role you are looking for and your city: it appears among your profiles and counts in your plan. If your plan has no room left, your coach tells you so in the conversation and shows you the plan that includes more. Each search criterion you give or approve afterwards, such as the contract type, is saved on it at once; the rest of your answers stay on the page until « That looks right » or « Save my profile », which completes that same profile: its search criteria, then a single new version of its Master CV. « Continue later » also saves the rest of your answers, in a new version of its Master CV. If you leave the page otherwise, the profile stays, with what was already saved; its page says « Creation to finish », and « Resume creating it » brings you back here, to the review, to complete and save it. If your plan has no room left and one of your profiles says « Creation to finish », « Create a profile » also brings you back to its review, instead of showing you the plan that includes more; if there are several, the most recent one opens. It still counts in your plan.

As soon as your profile is created, your coach starts a first job search for it, on us: it does not count against your weekly job searches quota, even on the Free plan, nor in « Your consumption ». It tells you so in the conversation. If you then change the role you are looking for or the city, by starting the questions again, it starts the search again once, on us too, and stops the previous one; beyond that, your searches are started from Job searches. It is a job search like any other: its page, its notification when it ends, its row in Activity. It is offered only once per profile created here.

You follow that search in « Activity », the panel to the right of the conversation, which opens by itself when the search starts (on a phone it does not: the « AI activity » icon at the top of the page opens it full screen), while you go on answering. It shows in « Right now », as a search of this page, with its four steps (« Reading your profile », « Searching France Travail, careers sites and the web », « Analysing the job offers found », « Picking your best job offers ») and four counters: « sources consulted », « pages read », « job offers analysed » and « job offers that fit you ». Below them, each source says where it stands: « to come », « in progress… », « consulted », « refused » or « unavailable ». « Target companies » shows only when your profile has some.

The first three counters are the ones the search measures itself: they stay at 0 until something is measured, and nothing in them is an estimate. The fourth, « job offers that fit you », counts the job offers found whose Match Score with your profile, as you build it, reaches 60. It is worked out again on the page with each criterion you add, without a new search and without AI: it can go up as well as down. The « Narrowed with: » line says with which criteria; a criterion your coach assumed and you have not confirmed yet is followed by « assumed », in brackets. While it searches, « Your best job offers so far » lists, under those counters, the job offers found that fit you (a score of 60 or more), the best first, added to as it finds more; you can carry on without waiting: its notification will tell you when it is over. If the search is stopped or does not succeed, « Right now » says so, with what it found, which is kept. Once over, it leaves « Right now » and becomes a line of the history, « Job search », « Offered, outside your plan »: opened, it gives its 3 to 5 best job offers, each with its employer, its Match Score and a link to its page, then « See all job offers », which opens the search's page. If none of the job offers found fits you, it says so (« No job offer found matches your criteria yet. ») and offers « See my profile », to look at your criteria again, and « Search again »: unlike the first one, this new search counts toward your job searches this week, and the line tells you so before the button; if that quota is used up, it shows you the plan that includes more. If it did not succeed, the line says « Failed at » and the time, then why. A screen reader hears only the search starting and its end, not every counter.

« Activity », at the top of the page, shows what is happening. While your coach reads your CV or prepares its question, « Right now » shows the step under way, which you can stop; stopping its question takes you to its usual questions. The history then groups the whole creation in a single row, « Profile creation »: the CV reading, if there is one, and the « Onboarding interview » with the number of your answers, each with the AI model that did it and its tokens; the « Onboarding questionnaire », whose questions are written by Jobbbox, « No AI », when it took over; the « Review »; the « Summary edited », if you used « Edit », with the model that read your text and its tokens; then « Profile created », with version 1 of your Master CV. When you answer the questions, « Profile created » shows as soon as the profile is created, before the review. The same row follows you to your new profile's page, with its first ATS Score once computed. The interview, the questionnaire and the review are not kept, and your exchange with the coach up to « That looks right » is not kept as a conversation: once the page is reloaded, the row shows only the CV reading, version 1 and the ATS Score. The onboarding interview does not count in your plan. The « Conversation context » stays empty, « The coach has not replied yet. », until your coach's first reply in the profile's conversation.

You can also write straight in the message box: your first message starts the interview. During the review, your coach only sends you back to it. After « That looks right », the conversation goes on under what came before, with your AI coach, as on every other page: it really answers you, with your new profile in view, and « Stop » interrupts its reply. This conversation is your profile's: you find it again on its page.

To answer, click « Send to the coach » or press Enter; for a yes-or-no question, click « Yes » or « No », or type it. The message box starts on one line and grows with your text, up to about eight lines; longer text scrolls inside it. Enter sends your answer and Shift+Enter adds a line. After sending, the box returns to one line.
