A profile is a role you are aiming for: a Master CV and search criteria. You can have several, depending on your plan.

The page is a conversation with your coach. If you have a CV, drop it in the « I have a CV » card: your coach reads it and proposes a Master CV. If not, click « Answer my coach's questions », above the message box: it asks about your career, one question at a time, and you answer in the message box; allow about ten minutes.

Either way, you check and correct everything in the pane that opens beside the conversation, then click « Save my profile ». Closed, the pane opens again with « Review and save », with nothing lost. Nothing is added that you did not say.

« Activity », at the top of the page, shows what is happening. While your coach reads your CV, « Right now » shows each step of the reading, which you can stop. The history then groups the whole creation in a single row, « Profile creation »: the CV reading, with the model that did it and its tokens, the only step done by AI; the « Onboarding questionnaire », whose questions are written by Jobbbox, « No AI », with the number of your answers; the « Review »; then « Profile created », with version 1 of your Master CV. The same row follows you to your new profile's page, with its first ATS Score once computed. The questionnaire and the review are not kept: once the page is reloaded, the row shows only the CV reading, version 1 and the ATS Score. The « Conversation context » stays empty, « The coach has not replied yet. », until your coach's first reply in the profile's conversation.
