An application's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the application and its job offer in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Prepare the interview », « Write a follow-up », « Write the cover letter », « Why this score? ». The conversation is kept with the application and comes back when you reopen the page; « Clear the conversation », in the activity, deletes all its messages, while the application and its documents stay.

On the right, the page panel, open on the application's fiche: its status and profile, which you change in place, its match, the day you saved it and the link to the job offer, then its sections. The icons at the top right of the page change its view: « Activity », what AI did for this page; « Fiche »; « The job offer », the job offer itself, with « Show the offer larger »; « Documents », your documents. The icon of the view shown closes the panel.

The activity shows what is running right now, then the page's history, and your three latest documents: the offer read, the tailored CV, the letter and the outreach message, the company dossier, the coach's proposals and its replies in the application's conversation, each with its models, its tools and its cost, never in euros.

Its sections: Suggestions, Documents, Match, Interviews, Coach reviews, Company dossier and Contacts; the job offer has its own view. A section or a result (Tailored CV, cover letter, outreach message, suggested follow-up) opens beside the conversation, without hiding it: you edit, download or copy there, and nothing is sent without your click. « Close » or the Escape key closes it. Each section keeps its address: a link to « Interviews » opens that section straight beside the conversation. While no interview is recorded, « Show me how » there shows how to move the application to the « Interview » status so you can add one.

Start with « Match » to see what fits and what is missing, then prepare your Tailored CV and your cover letter in « Documents ». You always check them before saving. Update the status in the fiche as you go: « Applied » once you have sent your application, « Interview » to record your interviews. If it gets no answer, your coach suggests a follow-up.

Your coach can propose to change the application's status (up to « Rejected » or « Abandoned », which archive it), to link it to another of your profiles, or to mark as sent a follow-up you sent yourself (two at most). The proposal shows under its reply: « Apply », « Edit before applying » or « Ignore ». Nothing changes without your click, and « Undo », on the applied proposal, puts back what was there before as long as nothing has changed since. « Origin », under the status, says it came from its proposal. Your coach never deletes an application: ask it to, and it sends you to this page. It can likewise propose to add, move or cancel an interview: once applied, the interview in the « Interviews » section carries « Origin » too.

Your coach can also propose to prepare your tailored CV, or to draft your cover letter or outreach message: it never writes them in its reply. The proposal shows under its reply. « Prepare » starts the tailored CV proposal, which then opens beside the conversation for you to accept or reject each change; it never replaces the tailored CV already saved. If a tailored CV proposal is still waiting for your review, the proposal says so and asks you to confirm replacing it, your answers included: « Replace and prepare » or « Keep my proposal ». « Draft » starts writing the draft; if there is one already, the proposal says so and asks you to confirm replacing it, your edits included: « Replace and draft » or « Keep my draft ». While it is being prepared, « Stop » ends it and nothing is kept. A preparation started cannot be undone: stop it.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. « Fiche » opens the panel over it, full screen, and the other views are under « … »; a section or a result opens full screen too: « Close » or « Close the panel » closes them and brings you back to the conversation.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), an application's fiche lists its documents: its tailored CV, its cover letter and its outreach message, with their language and the date they last changed. Tap a document to open it: it shows as a PDF in your phone's reader, Quick Look on iPhone and iPad, your PDF viewer on Android, and stays open beside it with « Open », « Share », « Download as PDF » and « Download as Word ». « Share » and « Download » go through the share sheet: save the file in Files, or send it by Mail or another app. The outreach message shows as text, with its subject: « Share » sends it as it is. The files you open stay in the app until you sign out, which deletes them from the device. In the app, documents are read, shared and downloaded; they are changed on jobbbox.ai.

In the app, an application's page is the same conversation, titled with its job offer's position; « Fiche », at the top of the screen, opens its fiche: full screen on a phone, beside the conversation on a tablet. The fiche shows the application, its sections, its activity and its results. Tap the status, under « Status », to choose another: it is saved at once, as on the site, and « Status saved » confirms it. The « Interviews » section lists your interviews, with their date and time in Paris, their format, their place or link and their note; a rescheduled interview says when it was first planned, a cancelled one stays struck through with the date it was cancelled. While the application is at the « Interview » status, the form at the bottom adds one: the date (dd/mm/yyyy), the time in Paris (hh:mm), then, if you wish, the duration, the format, the place or link and a note, and « Add the interview ». Once the interview is added, if it has never asked you on this device, the app offers « Get a reminder the day before your appointments? »: « Turn on notifications » or « Not now ». « Edit the interview of … » changes its date, which reschedules it, or its other details; « Cancel the interview » asks you to confirm with « Yes, cancel »; « Delete the interview of … » removes an interview entered by mistake. « See in the agenda » opens your agenda. « Match », « Company dossier », « Contacts » and « The job offer » open to read. The company dossier shows what Jobbbox gathered about the employer: how reliable it is, its identity, its key figures, the roles to contact and its news; it is built and updated on jobbbox.ai. « Contacts », where enriched contacts are available, lists the people found and your enriched contacts: tap an email or a number to write or call; contacts are searched for, and their details asked for, on jobbbox.ai, and the app does not keep those details offline. Your human coaches' reviews are on jobbbox.ai, and your coach's suggestions show in the conversation. An archived application opens and reads like the others. Offline, nothing is saved: the status and the interviews change once the network is back.
