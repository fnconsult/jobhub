title: Quota

What your plan includes: the number of profiles you can have, and each week the number of Match Scores, ATS Scores, job searches and enriched contacts, as well as the Job Digest frequency.

The « Weekly » bar, at the foot of the menu, shows where you stand; « Your usage this week », in My subscription, details it quota by quota. The count starts again from zero every Monday at midnight (Paris time).

Talking to your coach does not count against the quota. The first job search of a new profile, started while it is created, is on us: it does not count against it either, even on the Free plan.

An increase in your plan applies at once. A reduction is announced to you by email at least 30 days ahead, and you can then cancel at no cost, with a prorata refund.
