title: Results
synonyms: [results, result, produced documents, tailored cv, cover letter, outreach message, follow-up]
---
Results are what your AI coach made for an application and kept with it: the Tailored CV, the cover letter, the outreach message, the follow-ups it suggests. They are listed in the fiche's « Results » group, each with where it stands (« Draft of… », « Saved on… »).

A result opens beside the conversation, on its own (over it, full screen, on a phone): you review, edit, download or copy it there. Nothing is sent without your click. « Close » or the Escape key closes it.
