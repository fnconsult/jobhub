A profile is a role you are aiming for: a Master CV and search criteria. You can have several, depending on your plan.

If you have a CV, upload it: your coach reads it and proposes a Master CV. If not, answer its questions about your career, one at a time; allow about ten minutes.

Either way, you check and correct everything before saving. Nothing is added that you did not say.
