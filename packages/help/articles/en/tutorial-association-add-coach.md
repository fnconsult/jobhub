title: Add a coach to your Association
synonyms: [invite coach, Association coach]
---
1. Open “Association workspace”, accept the requested agreement, then open “My coaches”.
2. Under “Invite a coach”, enter “Full name” and “Email address”, then choose “Send invitation”.
3. The recipient opens “Read invitation”, signs in with that address and chooses “Accept invitation”. They then accept their own agreement. These steps grant no Candidate access beforehand.
4. Under “Offers”, select the seller, duration and your Association’s price, then “Save offer”. Third-party payment requires its validations before becoming bookable.
5. To end the relationship, open “My coaches” and choose “Retire … from Jobbbox”. The coach is also removed from Jobbbox; past sessions remain. The Association never reads their Candidates or notes.
