title: Read a document with the Candidate
requires: coach-space-v2
---
1. Open the current session in “My sessions”. When the Candidate shows a document, Shared Reading follows their page and scrolling. You see only that document, even without Coach Access.
2. “Stop” removes the document immediately. No document is kept in your coach workspace.
