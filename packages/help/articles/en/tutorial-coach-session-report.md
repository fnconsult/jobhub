title: Write a Session Report
synonyms: [compte rendu, session report, brouillon, publier]
requires: coach-space-v2
---
1. In “My sessions”, open a completed session. “Session Report” offers “What we covered”, “Next steps” and “Resources”: one HTTPS link per line. “Save draft” keeps your work without showing it to the Candidate on screen.
2. Review it, then click “Publish”. The report appears in that session’s conversation, signed with your name and “Human Coach”. The AI Coach reads it as a document and cannot copy its text into an action. A published text cannot be edited: a correction is a new message. The draft and published text are included in the Candidate’s data export.
