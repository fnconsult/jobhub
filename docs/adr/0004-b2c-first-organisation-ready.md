# B2C first, ready for organisations later

At launch, Candidates pay for themselves (Free / Standard / Premium Plans). Outplacement firms, APEC and corporate HR departments (PSE, rupture conventionnelle) are a likely second market, where an Organisation pays for its beneficiaries and its advisors can see their progress. Ownership and billing are designed so an Organisation layer can be added above Candidates without migrating data, even though no Organisation features ship in the MVP.
