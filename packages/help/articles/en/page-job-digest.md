A Job Digest's page is its conversation with your AI coach: it has the new job offers found for the profile and their Match Scores in view. Until you have written anything, suggestions wait above the message box: « Which offers to pick? », « Refine my criteria ». A Job Digest never counts against a quota.

On the right, the Job Digest's fiche: its profile, the day it was sent and how many job offers it holds. In « Results », each job offer with its Match Score; in « Sections », « Job offers found » lists them all and « Settings » opens how often this profile's Job Digest comes, which you change in place, with the latest ones sent.

A job offer or the settings open beside the conversation, without hiding it: save a job offer to follow it in your applications. « Close » or the Escape key closes them.

On a phone, the conversation takes the whole screen. « Fiche » opens the fiche over it, full screen, and so does a job offer: « Close » closes them and brings you back to the conversation.

In the Jobbbox app (iPhone, iPad, Android), a Job Digest's page shows its profile, the day it was sent and its job offers, closest to your master CV first, above its conversation. Tap a job offer to open it: its Match Score against the profile, a button explaining the score detailing each criterion, its summary and its text. « Follow » creates its application with this profile; once followed, « See the application » opens it. « Set aside » takes it out of your « Offers to handle », with a reason if you wish, and « Undo » goes back on it; a job offer set aside keeps « Go back on this choice ».
