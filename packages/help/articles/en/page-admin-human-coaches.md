Coaches (Coaching menu) opens « Human coaches »: the HR professionals Jobbbox selected, whom Candidates can book for paid sessions on top of the AI Coach. Jobbbox pays them outside the platform.

« Active coaches » lists each coach: their « Name », their « Sign-in email address », their « Cal.com booking link » and their « Introduction (optional) ». A coach signs in to Jobbbox with the address given here and finds their coach space. The « Add a coach » card adds one; « Retire … » takes them off the list. Their past sessions are kept.

Coach managers and super-admins add and retire coaches; Support reads the list. Each change is recorded in Administrator actions, section « Human Coaches ».

## Coaching Offers and Sellers

This section links each offer to its Seller and duration. Third-party Sellers provide their legal name, status and SIRET and set their price. Jobbbox retains its central price and Plan discount. Commission is set per Seller in basis points (100 = 1%); edits are audited and do not change paid sessions. Third-party offers remain unavailable until payments and legal conditions are activated.

## Coach applications

This section lists confirmed coach and Association applications. Review their details and private proof, then approve or decline with a reason. The decision is audited and emailed. Approval creates an identity awaiting contract acceptance and publication; it grants no Candidate access.

“Coach profiles to approve” lists public-profile proposals. “Approve profile” replaces the visible version; “Refuse profile” requires a reason emailed to the coach. The former Back Office biography becomes the initial version. In the new workspace, the coach proposes subsequent changes.
