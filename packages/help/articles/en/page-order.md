This page shows when you choose a plan, before payment. The summary gives the plan, its price including VAT, how often it is charged and that it renews: the subscription renews automatically until you cancel it.

Once our terms of sale are published, tick « I accept the terms of sale (version of …) » to go on; the link just above opens that version. The French text prevails. The box is never ticked for you.

The second box, « I ask for the service to start at once », is optional. Without it, you pay and your plan starts just the same, but a withdrawal is refunded in full. With it, a withdrawal refunds the amount paid, less the share of the days elapsed since the start of the period.

« Go to payment » opens the secure page of Stripe, our payment provider. Once the payment is confirmed, you receive the confirmation of your order by email, with the link to the version of the terms of sale you accepted and where to find « Renoncer au contrat ici » (withdraw from the contract here): keep it.

If you already have a paid plan, you change plans on Stripe's page, from « Manage my subscription », without this page.
