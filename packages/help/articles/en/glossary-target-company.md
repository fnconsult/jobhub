A target company is a company the job search aims at for a profile, even without a published job offer: one La Bonne Boite gives as likely to hire for your job and area, or one you add. They are in the profile's « Target companies » section, with their « Hiring potential ».

To add one, enter its name or its SIREN in « Company name », then click « Add »: it is looked up in the company register and comes before La Bonne Boite's. A sole trader, a person, is never searched.

For each, open its « Company dossier », look for « Enriched contacts » or click « Prepare a spontaneous application ». « Set aside » removes it from this profile for good.
