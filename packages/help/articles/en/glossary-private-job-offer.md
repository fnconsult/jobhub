title: Private job offer

A private job offer is a job offer only you see. It is the one Jobbbox draws from a recruiter's message you forward to it: that message was addressed to you, and the role is sometimes never published.

You follow it like any job offer: match score, tailored CV, cover letter, application. But it is never shown to another candidate, nor merged with the same job offer found elsewhere: if the role is published later, it becomes another job offer, and the private one stays as it is.

It is deleted with your account, and is part of the data you download. A job offer drawn from a job board's alert, on the other hand, describes a public posting: it is not private.
