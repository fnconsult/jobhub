This page, the Subscription section of My account, shows your plan (Free, Standard or Premium) and what it includes: the number of profiles, and each week the scores, job searches and enriched contacts. « Your usage this week » shows where you stand on each; the count starts again from zero every Monday. « Plans » compares them side by side: « Your Plan » marks yours, « Recommended » the one above it.

« Cancel your contract » ends your subscription at the end of the period already paid for, in two clicks from this page. Once the cancellation is scheduled, the page shows « Your current Plan: … until … », and an acknowledgement is sent to you by email.

« Manage my subscription » opens the secure page of Stripe, our payment provider: that is where you change plan or payment method, and where you undo a scheduled cancellation. Jobbbox never sees your card number. « Invoices and payment method » leads there too, to download your invoices.

« Choose the … Plan » first opens the Your order page: the plan's summary, the box to accept the terms of sale and the request for the service to start at once, before payment on Stripe's page. On the Free plan, a « Promotion code » entered here, then « Apply », is checked, and will apply at payment for a new subscription.

For 14 days after you subscribe, « Withdraw from the contract here » lets you withdraw, without a reason: your subscription is cancelled at once and you are back on the Free plan. What you paid is refunded in full or, if you asked for the service to start at once, less the share of the days elapsed since the start of the period. Changing plan in Stripe does not start this period again.

If we reduce what your plan includes (fewer job searches a week, a feature removed…), you are told by email at least 30 days ahead, with the reason and the date. A « Your … plan changes on … » box also shows it on this page: its « Cancel at no cost » button stops your subscription at once, and the part of the paid period that will not have been used is refunded to you prorata. You can do so up to 30 days after the date of the change.
