kind: faq
title: How do I get or delete my data?
synonyms: [data, gdpr, delete, erase, export, download, privacy, close my account]
---
In My account, under My data: « Download all my data » gives you one file with everything Jobbbox keeps about you, including the versions of the Terms of use and Terms of sale you accepted.

In the same place, « Permanently delete my account » closes it at once, once you have typed your email address to confirm. Your profiles, CVs, applications and documents are erased, for good within 30 days at most, and your subscription ends. Only the proof of the terms you accepted (Terms of use and Terms of sale: which version, when) is kept, without your name or email address: 5 years for the Terms of use, 10 years for the Terms of sale.

Download your data first if you want to keep it.
