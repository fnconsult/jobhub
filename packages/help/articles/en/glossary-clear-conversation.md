title: Clear the conversation
synonyms: [clear, empty the conversation, delete the messages, start the conversation over]
---
« Clear the conversation », a link under the conversation's context, at the head of the activity (its icon, at the top right of the page), deletes all the messages of the page's conversation; on a phone, it is also under « … », at the top right. Jobbbox asks you to confirm first; « Cancel » deletes nothing. While the coach is replying, « Clear the conversation » waits: stop its reply first with « Stop ».

The coach's memory stays: what you asked it to remember is not cleared. The page becomes a new conversation again.
