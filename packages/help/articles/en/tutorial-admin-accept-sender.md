When Candidates add for themselves an offer sender the platform's list lacks, it is proposed to you. Accepting it has its emails read for every Candidate. You need the Technique or Super-admin role.

1. Open Paramètres (Platform menu), « Sources » tab, then the sheet of MTA Jobbbox (Haraka).
2. Under « Proposed senders », read the domain or address, the number of Candidates (never which) and « Last added ».
3. Check that the sender really sends Job Offers: a job board's or a careers site's alert, not a private person. When in doubt, do not accept.
4. Enter its label in « Label of … », as « platform or company – site », at most 120 characters.
5. Click « Accept … ». The page shows « Sender added to the platform's list. » and it appears under « Recognised senders ».

To add a sender yourself, fill in « Add a sender »: the « Sender's domain or address », then the « Label », and click « Add the sender ». A sender is disabled or removed from the same list. Each change is recorded in Administrator actions.
