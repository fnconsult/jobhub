Feedback is handled oldest first. You need the Support or Super-admin role.

1. Open Feedback (Candidates menu). Keep « Filter by status » on « To handle » and, if needed, choose a type in « Filter by type ».
2. Click « Open » on the oldest one. Read the message, its « Context » (page, version, browser, language, text size) and, for a question, the « Question asked to the AI Coach » and « The AI Coach's reply ».
3. Click « Mark “Seen” » once you have taken it in without replying yet.
4. To reply, write « The team's reply » (one reply, at most 2,000 characters) and click « Save the reply ». The Candidate sees it signed « L'équipe Jobbbox », never with your name.
5. Once the bug is fixed, the idea noted or the question answered, click « Mark “Handled” ». A question is marked « Handled » only once replied to, and the reply can no longer be changed afterwards.
6. Click « All feedback » to move to the next one.

A bug that can be reproduced becomes an issue for the technical team; a question that shows a gap in the help deserves an article. Never copy the Candidate's email or message into an AI tool.
