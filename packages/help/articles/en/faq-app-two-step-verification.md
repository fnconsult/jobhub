title: Two-step verification in the app

If your account has two-step verification, the Jobbbox app asks for it after each sign-in by magic link, as the website does, and before opening anything else. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app in « App code », then tap « Verify ». Without your app, tap « Use a backup code » and enter one of your backup codes: each one works only once.

If your account has a passkey, tap « Use a passkey »: your phone asks for Face ID, your fingerprint or your security key. A passkey created on jobbbox.ai works in the app when it is synced to your phone (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager). A passkey of another account is refused, and the sign-in still waits for its second step. On an Android device older than version 9, only the codes are offered.

The rules are the website's: after 5 wrong codes in a row, codes are refused for 15 minutes, and the app tells you when to try again. The app keeps neither your codes nor your passkey. To turn on two-step verification, add a passkey or renew your backup codes, go to the « Security » section of My account. « Sign out » stays possible at any time.
