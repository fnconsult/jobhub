# The Human Coach epic is merged behind one Platform Setting, « Espace coach v2 », off by default

The Human Coach epic (#867, tickets #868 to #888) rebuilds the Coach space (ADR-0099) and adds the marketplace: Coach Enrolments, Associations, Coach Pages, Coaching Offers and Sellers, Stripe Connect, Coach Earnings, the B2B contract accepted by version, booking in Cal.com, cancellation and refunds, reminders, content reports and DAC7. #867 planned it « derrière le Platform Setting « Espace coach v2 », éteint par défaut ». The consolidated delivery (#975) had instead made the new workspace permanent, as #888's switch-over does. That would have changed the Coach space and Candidates' pages as soon as it merged, and locked every Human Coach out, since the B2B contract (#518) has no approved version yet. Decided with the product owner on 2026-11-12.

- **One Platform Setting, `COACH_SPACE_V2`, boolean, off by default**, on Paramètres' « Environnement » tab, set by a super-admin with a confirmation, and recorded in the Back Office Audit like every Platform Setting. Env can set it as well. There is one switch for the whole epic, not one per ticket.
- **Off is main as it was before the epic, for everyone outside the Back Office**: the former Coach space (its Coaching Sessions and Coach Access list, no contract to accept), the Candidates' Agenda in Paris time, their conversation, data export, orders and checkout, the session dialog and its history, Application and Profile pages, Préférences and Rappels. It also covers the e-mails, push notifications and worker jobs, the apps' reads and screens, and the Help Centre's articles and Motion Scripts. Every route the epic adds answers 404. Each entry point keeps the former code beside the new one and chooses with `coachSpaceV2On()` (`apps/web/src/coach-space-v2/`), or with the worker's resolved settings at each run, so the change needs no restart.
- **On is the epic as delivered**. Turning it on approves nothing: the legal gates (#889, #890, #518), the provider gates (Stripe Connect, Cal.com and its reminders confirmation) and the fiscal gate (#887) each still decide, and fail closed.
- **What no Candidate or Coach sees is not gated**: the database migrations (new tables, columns with defaults, triggers acting only on the new tables' rows), the Back Office's coaching pages, its Outils and its provider setup. An Administrator can prepare offers, Sellers and providers while the setting is off.
- **The app reads carry it** (`coachSpaceV2`, false when absent), so a new app build follows the server, and a build published before the epic behaves as off.
- **Both states are tested**: unit tests of each gate, off and on. The e2e suite runs with it off, with the specs and screenshot baselines of main. The epic's specs run in the `coach-v2` Playwright project, between a setup that turns it on and a teardown that turns it off, in a CI shard of their own, since the setting is global to the suite's database.
- **The epic's Release Note fragments wait for #888** in `docs/ops/human-coach-888-release-notes/`: until the setting is on, a Candidate sees nothing of it.
- **#888 becomes « turn it on, then retire the former Coach space »**: on staging, then in production, once its gates are met; then the former code paths, the legacy pages and the setting itself are removed in a later change.

## Considered Options

- **Merge as delivered, the new workspace permanent**: every Human Coach would lose their Candidates until #518 has an approved version, and Candidates would see times, reports, bookings and articles no Release Note announced.
- **One setting per ticket**: closer to each ticket's acceptance, but 21 switches whose combinations nobody tests, for an epic that is turned on once.
- **Hold #975 until the legal texts are approved and merge it as the switch-over**: the branch keeps drifting from main, as it already had over a week (#985, #987, #998).
- **Hide the new pages but keep the changed Candidate pages as delivered**: the changes look small (a time zone after a time, a contact block), but they describe flows that are not open yet.
