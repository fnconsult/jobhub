Your coach searches for job offers that meet a profile's search criteria. Choose the profile, or all your active profiles, then start the search.

The page updates by itself while the search runs, and you can stop it. The latest job offers found are shown with their Match Score.

Each search counts once against your plan's quota. If it fails, or if you stop it before it has found a job offer, it does not count.

With the new layout, « Job searches » is a conversation with your AI coach, the same one every visit. Your searches are listed there in a card, by profile: when each was started, where it stands and how many job offers it found; « New results » marks those you have not seen yet. Click a line to open the search. To start one, use the « Start a search » suggestion above the message box, one per profile if you have several. « See the job offers received by email » opens them beside the conversation.

In the menu, « Job searches » unfolds into your most recent searches, those with results not seen yet marked with a dot; « See all » leads to this page.
