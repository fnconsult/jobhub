kind: faq
title: How do I report a problem or reach the team?
synonyms: [feedback, report, bug, problem, idea, support, team, person, reach, talk to someone]
---
For a problem or an idea, send feedback: « Report a problem » or « Suggest an idea », under the message box of every conversation. You also find it with the icon beside « My account », at the foot of the menu. The form opens beside the conversation, without hiding it (over it, full screen, on a phone); « Close » or the Escape key closes it and your draft is kept.

To ask a person a question, ask your AI coach first. If its reply doesn't help, answer « No » under it: « Ask the team » sends your question and the coach's reply to the Jobbbox team, in the same form.

The Jobbbox team reads every piece of feedback. You follow its status and the team's reply in Jobbbox.

There is no support email address: feedback is how you reach a person.
